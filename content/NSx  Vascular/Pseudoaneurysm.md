
#vascular #pseudoaneurysm #trauma 
## Definition
- abnormal outpouchings or dilatation of arteries which are bounded only by the [tunica adventitia](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/histology-of-blood-vessels?embed_domain=jW56G&lang=us), the outermost layer of the arterial wall. 
	- these lack all three layers of the arterial wall (intima, media and adventitia).
- These are distinguished from [true aneurysms](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/true-aneurysm?embed_domain=jW56G&lang=us), which are bounded by all three [layers of the arterial wall](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/histology-of-blood-vessels?embed_domain=jW56G&lang=us). 
## Etiology
- Pseudoaneurysm typically occur when there is a breach in the vessel wall such that blood leaks through the inner wall but is contained by the adventitia or surrounding perivascular soft tissue.
