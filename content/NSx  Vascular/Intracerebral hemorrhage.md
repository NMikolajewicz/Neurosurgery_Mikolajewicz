### Causes

* Hypertension microangiopathy
	* deep brain structures
	* structural and mechanical age-related changes to arterial walls
* Cerebral amyloid angiopathy
	* lobar regions
	* amyloid deposits weaken vascular integrity
* AVM
	* abnormal connections between arterial and venous vasculature
* Cavernous angioma
	* neurovascular disorder
	* dilated leaky capillaries following dysfunctional endothelium
* Moyamoya
	* rare condition
	* vascular constriction within arteries at the circle of willis
	* results in abnormal vessel formation compensating for blockage of normal blood flow
* [[Aneurysms]]
	* dilations of blood vessels
	* causes by weakening of parent artery angioarchitecture

### ICH score

* Predicts 30-day mortality following ICH
* Range 0-6
* Components
	* GCS
	* ICH volume (>30 mL)
	* IVH
	* Infratentorial origin
	* Age (>80 y)
