**Spinal needle will traverse the following layers:**

* Skin
* Subcutaneous tissue
* Supraspinous ligament
* Interspinous ligament
* Ligamentum flavum
* Epidural space
* Dura
* Arachnoid

### Procedure Steps

1. Patient Consent and Assessment
2. Equipment Preparation:
	* sterile gloves, drapes, local anesthetic, lumbar puncture needle, catheter, sterile saline, syringes, and dressing materials.
3. Sterile Field Preparation:
4. Position the Patient: 
	* lateral decubitus
	* sitting position
5. Identify Anatomic Landmarks
	* L3-L4 or L4-L5 intervertebral space.
6. Local Anesthetic
	* Inject a local anesthetic
	* into skin down to the level of the ligamentum flavum
7. Insert the Needle
	* aseptic technique
	* insert needle aiming slightly towards the umbilicus.
	* Advance the needle until 1) loss of resistance or 2) the appearance of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF).
8. Catheter Insertion
	* thread catheter into space once subarachnoid space is confirmed by CSF flow
9. Secure the Catheter
	* Secure with sutures
	* cover the insertion site with a sterile dressing.
10. Adjust Drainage System
	* Connect  catheter to the external drainage system
	* Adjust the height of the collection bag to regulate the amount of CSF drainage
11. Monitor Patient
	* Monitor signs of headache, nausea, or neurological changes.
	* Regular monitoring of vital signs and neurological status is crucial.
