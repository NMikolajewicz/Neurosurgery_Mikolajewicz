Approach to ensure neurostatus

* Clinical exam
* Sleep, intubation
	* Avoid awake fiberoptic to minimize risk of cough/dislocation
* Pre flip neuromonitoring
* Post flip neuromonitoring

### Closed reduction

1. Mayfield and traction

### Open reduction

1. towel clips, manual traction.
	1. Risk of flavum in folding
2. Burr down SAP to reduce jump height for jumped facet
3. Optimize mechanical advantage
	1. Head positioning in mayfield
		1. Mneuver is to flex, traction and dorsal translation
	2. Place screws above and below and distract
		1. Use temporary rod on contralateral side to disipate force and reduce pull out force of distracting screw
