### Background

* Proposed by Harms and Rolinger in 1982
* Alternative to two-step anterior and posterior lumbar interbody fusion
* Allows access to disc space via far lateral approach after facetectomy

### Indications

* Spondylolisthesis
* Symptomatic degenerative disc disease
* Recurrent lumbar disc herniation with low back pain or radiculopathy
* Pseudoarthrosis
