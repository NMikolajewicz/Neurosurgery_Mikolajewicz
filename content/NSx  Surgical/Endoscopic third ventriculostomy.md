Endoscopic third ventriculostomy (ETV)

* surgical alternative to placing a CSF shunt in certain patients with hydrocephalus

The ETV Success Score (ETVSS)

* reliable, simple method to estimate the success of the procedure by 6 months of postoperative follow-up.
* The highest score is 90, estimating a 90% chance of the ETV effectively treating hydrocephalus without requiring a shunt.
* ETVSS ~ age + prior shunt + etiology
