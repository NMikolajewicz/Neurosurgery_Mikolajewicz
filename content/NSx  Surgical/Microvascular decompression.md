Indications: trigeminal neuralgia

* character of the pain in trigeminal neuralgia is typically unilateral, episodic, severe, stabbing, shock-like or lancinating, and exacerbated by cutaneous stimuli such as tactile pressure, chewing, brushing, a breeze of air, or shaving.
* This character is considered neuralgic in contrast to neuropathic pain: A burning pain associated with numbness.

Positioning: parkbench
Preop: ancef 2g, dexamethasone
Adjuncts: neuronavigation, ultrasound, microscope
major structures to avoid: TS, SS
