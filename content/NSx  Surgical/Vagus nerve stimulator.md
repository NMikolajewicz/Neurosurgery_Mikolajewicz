## Efficacy

* 50% reduction in seizure frequency in 70% of medication refractory epilepsy patients

## Mechanism

* mechanism of seizure suppression by vagus nerve stimulation unknown
* theories
	* altered activity systems associated with vagal projections
		* including reticular activating system, central autonomic network, limbic system, noradrenergic projection system

## Indications

* adjuvant therapy to reduce frequency of seizures in individuals ==\>12 years with partial onset seizures== that are refractory to antiepileptic medications
* treatment of depression

## Contraindications

* prior L cervical vagotomy

## Vagus nerve anatomy

* Fiber composition
	* 80% afferents from vicsera
	* 20% efferents to larynx and parasympathetics of herat, lung and GI
* cervical vagus nerve
	* target of VNS device
	* lateral and level to mid laryngeal skeleton
	* vagus nerve within carotid sheath between and posterior to CCA and IJV
* Avoid superior and inferior cervical cardiac branches of vagus nerve
	* innervate cardiac plexus

## Consent

* scar on neck
* palpable subcutaneous VNS pulse
* hoarse voice during stimulation cycle (30 seconds)
* able to abort stimulation cycle using VNS therapy magnet
* limited batter life, requires replacement q6-11 years
* risk of VNS therapy failure, injury to vagus nerve injury

## Complications

* infection (1-8% risk)
* Left true vocal cord paralysis (0.7% risk)
* bleeding, seroma, hematoma
* nausea, paresthesias
* cardiac: bradycardia, ventricular asystole (0.125-0.25% risk)

## Side effects

* coughing
* laryngismus
* voice alterations
* local strap/deep cervical muscle movements/twitching
* dyspnea
* local pain in vicinity of device

## Positioning and landmarks

* supine
* head rotated to right with mild extension
* left arm secured, abducted 70-90 degrees
* incisions:
	* cervical: 5 cm curvilinear
		* mid to lower larynx
		* between lateral laryngeal skeleton and SCM
	* preaxillary: 5 cm
		* lateral edge of pectoralis major muscle

## Procedure

1. ### Placement of VNS therapy lead
	
	* cervical incision
		* subplatysmal flap elevated and retracted
		* muscle dissection
			* SCM dissected medially, retract posteriorly
			* strap muscles retracted anteriorly
	* vagus nerve explosure
		* sharp/blunt dissection of neurovascular sheath
			* CCA anterior retraction
			* IJV posterior retraction
			* VN found between CCA and IJV
	* electrode placement
		* measure vagus nerve diameter
		* handle electrodes using embedded sutures, not electrode array
		* 3 components:
			* distal electrode (most cephalad, negative polarity)
			* middle electrode (positive polarity)
			* proximal inactive anchor
		* secure strain relief band and tie down to fascia with 4-0 vicryl.

2. ### Placement of therapy pulse generator
	
	* sharp/blunt dissection over pectoralize major muscle
		* 5 cm incision
		* create pocket for pulse generator
	* pass lead wire subcutaneously from cervical incision to preaxillary incision with tunnler

## Post-operative

* 3 days Abx
* 2 week follow up with referring neurologist to program VNS system
