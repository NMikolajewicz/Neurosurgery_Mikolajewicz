# Cervical

## C1 (atlas) lateral mass

Draw these lines

* A: Inferior border of C1 posterior arch.
* B: Vertical midline of the C1 lateral mass.
	Entry: At A×B (i.e., midpoint of lateral mass just under the arch) or ~3 mm lateral to the arch–mass junction.
	

Trajectory: 10–15° medial, parallel to the posterior arch or slightly cephalad toward the anterior tubercle.
Checks: Stay below vertebral artery groove (superolateral).
Mnemonic: “C1 = Midpoint to the Tubercle.” (aim to AT)

## C2 (axis) pedicle/pars

Draw these lines

* A: Ridge where superior lamina transitions to pars.
* B: C2–3 facet line (inferior articular surface).

Entry (pedicle): “4–4 rule” → 4 mm lateral & 4 mm inferior to A.
Trajectory: 20–30° medial, sagittal ~parallel to C2 superior endplate.
Pars option: Mid-pars, 0–10° medial, straight anterior.
Checks: Palpate medial wall with nerve hook before tapping.
Mnemonic: “C2 = 4-4 & 20–30.”

## C3–C7 pedicle (subaxial)

Draw these lines

* A: Lateral vertebral notch (LVN) vertical line.
* B: Horizontal through center of lateral mass.

Entry (C3–6): 2 mm medial to LVN on line B.
Entry (C7): ~center of lateral mass, 2 mm superolateral tweak acceptable.
Transverse medial angles: C3–5 ≈45° → C6 ≈38° → C7 ≈28°.
Sagittal: C3 ~7° up; C4–7 ~parallel to superior endplate.
Mnemonic: “2-mm medial rule; 45→38→28 caudally.”

## Cervical lateral mass (C3–C6)

* Roy-Camille:
	* Entry: exact center of lateral mass.
	* Aim: ~10° lateral, perpendicular to posterior surface.
* Magerl:
	* Entry: 1–2 mm medial + 1 mm cranial to center.
	* Aim: ~25° lateral / 45° cranial, parallel to the facet.
	* Line trick: Put a Penfield in the facet → shoot parallel to it.

Mnemonic: “Roy = Right in the middle; Magerl = Medial & Cranial.”

# Thoracic (T1–T12) pedicle

Universal landmark (works across levels):
SFBC cross = Superior Facet Base Crosshair: outline the superior articular facet, mark the junction of medial 2/3 and lateral 1/3 at its base.
Entry: At that junction, slightly caudal to facet midpoint.
Trajectory (freehand rules):

* Transverse: modest medial (≈5–15°; more medial in upper thoracic, less caudally).
* Sagittal: ~parallel to the local superior endplate (“orthogonal to the lamina/rod contour”).

Regional nudges:

* T1–3: a touch more cephalad start, slightly more medial.
* T7–12: entry trends more lateral & caudal; cephalad tilt decreases.

Mnemonics:

* “Facet-first in thoracic.”
* “2/3–1/3 rule at the superior facet base.”
* “Crossover around T6–7” reminds you the TP and pedicle line up—don’t drift too medial.

# Lumbar pedicle

## Traditional (freehand)

Draw these lines

* A: Vertical line along lateral border of the superior facet.
* B: Horizontal at mid-transverse-process height

Entry: Near the A×B corner (mamillary–transverse corner), typically just inferior to the facet midpoint; trend slightly lateral at L5.
Trajectory: Medial angulation increases ~5° per level caudally → L1 5°, L2 10°, L3 15°, L4 20°, L5 25°. Sagittal parallel to superior endplate.
Mnemonic: “Lumbar = Corner shot; +5° each level.”

## Cortical Bone Trajectory (CBT)

Draw these lines

* A: Pars–lamina junction.
* B: Pedicle “clock face.”
	Entry: At 5-o’clock (left) / 7-o’clock (right) on the pedicle eye, low on the laminar slope.
	Trajectory: 10° lateral & 25–30° cranial, targeting posterior 1/3–1/2 of the superior endplate; typical depth 35–40 mm.
	Safety tip: Start as caudal as possible to avoid cranial facet violation.
	Mnemonic: “CBT = Low, Lateral, Look-Up (10°/30°).”
	

# Sacrum & Pelvis

## S1 pedicle

Draw these lines

* A: Midline of S1 superior facet.
* B: Horizontal through S1 TP base.

Entry: Just lateral to A and slightly cranial to B.
Trajectory: ≈30° medial, toward the sacral promontory.
Mnemonic: “S1 = Lateral-to-mid-facet, 30-to-promontory.”

## S2 alar–iliac (S2AI)

Draw these lines

* A: Identify S1 and S2 dorsal foramina; mark the midpoint between them on the lateral sacral crest.
* B: Aim line from entry toward the ASIS/AIIS–greater trochanter planeo.

Entry: ~1 mm inferior & 1 mm lateral to S1 dorsal foramen (or midpoint between S1–S2 foramina, ~2 mm medial to lateral crest).
Trajectory: About 40° lateral & 40° caudal, toward the AIIS, staying above the sciatic notch.
Mnemonic: “S2AI = 40/40 to AIIS.”

# Fast safety checks (works everywhere)

* Facet respect: don’t start too cranial (avoid iatrogenic facet violation).
* Probe the walls: ball-tip feel medial, inferior, lateral cortex after gearshift.
* Sagittal sanity: keep ~parallel to superior endplate unless CBT.
* Fluoro truths: AP for pedicle centering; lateral for length/sagittal; oblique if in doubt.
* CT plan = law: know pedicle width, VA groove (C1), high-riding VA (C2), dysmorphic thoracic pedicles, lumbosacral transitional anatomy.
