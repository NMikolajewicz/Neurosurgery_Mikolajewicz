## Definition
- typically **benign neuronal tumors** that arise in the **lateral ventricles**
	- most often near the **foramen of Monro**
- They are thought to originate from ==**neurocytic precursor cells**== and are ==**WHO grade II**== tumors.
## Epidemiology
- <1% of intracranial tumors
- young adults 20-40 years
- slight male predominance
- intraventricular >>> extraventricular
## Subtypes
- classic central neurocytoma: intraventricular
- extraventricular neurocytoma: parenchymal
- atypical neurocytoma: Ki67 > 2-3%, increased mitosis/necrosis/vascular proliferation
## Histology
* uniform round cells with neuronal differentiation
* (+) **synaptophysin**, **NSE**, **NeuN**
* (-) glial markers (GFAP)
## Imaging
* T1 isodense ventricular
* T2 hyperintensive +/- calcification +/- cystic

## Management
* surgery
* no role for chemotherapy
* +/- radiation if recurrent
## Prognosis
* 80% 1 year survival
* >90% 5y OS
* Recurrence risk
	* >2-3% Ki67 
	* subtotal resection
	* atypical histology
