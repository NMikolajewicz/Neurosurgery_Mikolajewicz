## Background
* no genetic associations
* inclusion during primary neurulation
* theoretical risk during LP/LD
* linear growth pattern
	* as opposed to exponential growth for tumors
## Epidermoid vs. Dermoid

![[Neurosurgery/NSx  Oncology/_resources/DermoidEpidermoid.resources/image.png|500]]

| Feature          | Epidermoid Cyst                       | Dermoid Cyst                           |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Typical Location | Off-midline (CPA, middle fossa)       | Midline (sellar, frontonasal)          |
| CT Appearance    | Hypodense, CSF-like                   | Hypodense, fatty ± calcified           |
| T1 MRI Signal    | Iso/hypointense to CSF                | ==Hyperintense== (fat-rich)            |
| T2 MRI Signal    | ==Hyperintense, CSF-lik==e            | Variable (fat, tissue, bone)           |
| DWI              | ==Restricted diffusion, bright==      | Facilitated (no restriction)           |
| FLAIR            | Incomplete suppression                | Variable                               |
| Additional Notes | Lined by squamous epithelium, keratin | Contains skin appendages, oily content |
## Management
* surgical if symptomatic
* complications
	* aseptic/chemic meningitis if contents rupture
		* post-op steroids essential
