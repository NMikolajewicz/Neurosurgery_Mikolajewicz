## Suprasellar extension
* O: none
* A: expanding into the suprasellar cistern
* B: anterior recesses of third ventricle obliterated
* C: floor of the third ventricle displaced
## Parasellar extension
* D: intracranial (intradural)
* E: into/beneath cavernous sinus (extradural). 
	* +/- carotid encasement
## Sellar Turcica
* I: intact or focally expanded
* II: sella enlarged
* III: localized perforation of sella floor
* IV: diffuse destruction of sella floor
## Surgical implications
* A-C: optic apparatus compression/chiasmal descent
* E: cavernous sinus involvement, ocular motor deficits
* III-IV: higher reconstruction required (vs. I-II)
