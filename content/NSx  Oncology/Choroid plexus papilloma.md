> [!Choroid plexus papilloma]
> - pediatric tumor, choroid plexus origin
> - cauliflower appearance on MRI
> - Grade 1: CPP, surgery, 95% 5y OS
> - Grade 2: atypical CPP, surgery
> - Grade 3: CPC (Li-Fraumeni, Aicardi syndromes), neoadjuvant chemo (ICE) + surgery, 30% 5y OS
> 
> 

## Background
* pediatric tumor
* +++vascular
* localiation
	* adults: 4th ventricle
	* pediatric: lateral ventricle
* male predominance
* Presentation
	* hydrocephalus
		* decreased LOC
		* craniomegaly
## Grading
* Grade 1
	* choroid plexus papilloma
	* GFAP negative
* Grade 2
	* atypical CPP
* Grade 3
	* choroid plexus carcinoma
			Associations
			* [Li-Fraumeni syndrome](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/li-fraumeni-syndrome-2?lang=us) 
			* [Aicardi syndrome](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/aicardi-syndrome?lang=us) 
			* simian virus 40 (SV40), on the basis of this virus' DNA having been identified in up to 50% of cases 
	* malignant
## Imaging
* cauliflower appearance

## Management
* manage hydrocephalus
	* EVD +/- VPS
* consider angiogram for preop embolization
* surgery
	* CPP
		* resect
	* CPC
		* pre-op chemotherapy
			* ==ICE==: ifosfamide, carboplatin, etopside
			* helps reduce bleeding, facilitates easier resection
		* resect
			* divide feeder
			* 3rd ventricle: medial posterior choroidal
			* 4th ventricle: ICA branch
## Prognosis
* CPP: 95% 5 year survival
* CPC: 30% 5 year survival
