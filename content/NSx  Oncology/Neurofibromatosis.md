## Summary 

| Domain                     | NF1 (von Recklinghausen)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | NF2 (Merlin; a.k.a. NF2-related schwannomatosis*)                                                                                                                            | Schwannomatosis (SMARCB1/LZTR1)                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gene / inheritance         | **==NF1==**, chr17q11.2; AD; ~50% de novo                                                                                                                                                                                                                | **==NF2==**, chr22q12; AD; mosaic common                                                                                                                                     | **==SMARCB1==** or **LZTR1** (chr22); AD; mosaic common                                                                                 |
| Diagnostic anchors (quick) | ≥2 of: ≥6 CALMs (≥5 mm prepubertal, ≥15 mm post), axillary/inguinal freckling, ≥2 neurofibromas or 1 plexiform, optic pathway glioma, ≥2 Lisch nodules, distinctive osseous lesion (e.g., sphenoid wing dysplasia/tibial dysplasia), 1st-degree relative | ==Bilateral vestibular schwannomas== **or** (FHx + combinations of: unilateral VS, ≥2 meningiomas, non-VS schwannomas, spinal ependymoma, juvenile cataracts)                | ≥2 non-cutaneous schwannomas (≥1 path-proven), typically **no bilateral VS by >30**, no constitutional NF2 mutation; pain is a hallmark |
| Hallmark tumors            | Dermal + **plexiform ==neurofibromas==**; **optic pathway gliomas**; low-grade gliomas                                                                                                                                                                   | ==**Bilateral VS**==, multiple **meningiomas**, spinal **ependymomas**, non-VS schwannomas                                                                                   | Multiple ==painful== **==schwannomas==** (peripheral/spinal); vestibular nerves usually spared                                          |
| Cutaneous / ocular         | CALMs, axillary/inguinal freckling; **Lisch nodules**                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Fewer skin findings; **juvenile/posterior subcapsular cataracts**                                                                                                            | Skin often normal; no CALMs requirement                                                                                                 |
| CNS/PNS pattern            | Optic pathway tumors (childhood); “UBOs” on T2; dysplastic sphenoid wing; plexiforms track along nerves                                                                                                                                                  | “==Multiple intracranial tumors==” signature: bilateral VS ± meningiomas; spine with ependymomas + schwannomas                                                               | Numerous peripheral and spinal schwannomas; cranial schwannomas outside IAC possible; IACs typically clean                              |
| Imaging pearls             | T2 “UBOs” in kids; optic pathway thickening/enhancement when active                                                                                                                                                                                      | CPA/IAC enhancing masses (VS); look for **multiple meningiomas**; whole-neuroaxis to pick up spine ependymomas/schwannomas                                                   | Scatter of enhancing nerve-sheath lesions along named nerves/roots; IACs usually spared                                                 |
| Vascular association       | ↑ risk of **Moyamoya** (rare)                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | —                                                                                                                                                                            | —                                                                                                                                       |
| Malignancy risk            | ==**MPNST** risk== (esp. from plexiforms)                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Malignant change rare; morbidity from tumor burden                                                                                                                           | Malignancy rare; disability/pain dominate                                                                                               |
| Surveillance (pragmatic)   | Annual BP/skin; annual peds ophthalmology; MRI brain/spine **if symptomatic**                                                                                                                                                                            | **Annual audiology** from adolescence; periodic MRI brain/IACs + entire spine (interval by burden/growth); ophthalmology                                                     | Symptom-guided MRI targeting pain generators; genetics follow-up                                                                        |

MPNST: malignant peripheral nerve sheath tumor


## Diagnostic criteria
### NF1 diagnostic criteria
* six or more ==**café au lait**== spots
* peripheral neurofibromatosis
* hyperpigmentation in the axillary or inguinal areas
* ==optic gliomas==
* Two or more Lisch nodules
* Distinctive osseous (e.g. sphenoid dysplasia)
* One first degree relative with NF-1

### NF2 diagnostic criteria
* A) Definite diagnosis if **==bilateral vestibular schwannomas==** on imaging
* B) Definite diagnosis if ==first degree relative== with NF-2 **==_AND_==** either unilateral vestibular schwannoma at age less than 30 or any two of the following: 
	* meningioma
	* schwannoma
	* glioma
	* posterior subscapular lens opacity

## Clinical features of NF-2
a. Multiple **==intradural spinal tumors==** are common
b. Most NF-2 patients will become **==deaf==**
c. retinal hamartomas
d. ==Pregnancy may accelerate==the growth of eight nerve tumors
