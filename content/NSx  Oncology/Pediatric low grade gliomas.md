- KIAA1549-BRAF, BRAF p.V600E, and NF1 mutations account for ==2/3 of pLGG==
- 84% of pLGG targetable MAPK-pathway drivers.

- pLGG comprise two distinct clinical subgroups:

## RAS/MAPK Pathway

### RAS-MAPK signaling

- **Trigger:** RTK → GRB2/SOS → **RAS-GTP**

- **RAF activation:** RAS-GTP recruits RAF (ARAF/BRAF/CRAF) to membrane → RAF **dimerizes** and is activated (relief of autoinhibition via 14-3-3 and activation-segment phosphorylation).

- **Cascade:** ==RAF → **MEK1/2** → **ERK1/2** → transcription, cell cycle, survival.==

### BRAF V600E

- **Mutation:** ==Val⁶⁰⁰→Glu== in the **activation segment** (adjacent to T599/S602).

- **Biophysical effect:** The glutamate's negative charge **mimics activation-segment phosphorylation**, stabilizing BRAF in the **active (DFG-in) conformation**.

- **Functional consequence:**
    - ==**Constitutively high kinase activity**== (≈10–500× WT, context-dependent).
    - ==**RAS-independent signaling==:** V600E can signal effectively as a **monomer** (doesn't require RAS-driven dimerization).
    - Sustained **MEK→ERK** activation with partial escape from normal feedback brakes.
