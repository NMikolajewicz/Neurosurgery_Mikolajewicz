areas where the ==blood–brain barrier is absent.==

Seven different such areas have been identified:
1\. Pineal gland
2\. Subfornical organ
3\. Organum vasculosum of the lamina terminalis. 
4\. Median eminence of the hypothalamus
5\. Neurohypophysis
6\. Area postrema
7\. Subcommissural organ.
