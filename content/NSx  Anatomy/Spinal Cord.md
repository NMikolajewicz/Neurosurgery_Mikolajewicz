### Rexed Lamina

* dorsal horn (I-V)
* intermediate cord (VI-VII)
* ventral horn (VIII-IX)

	system of ten layers of [grey matter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey_matter) (I–X), identified in the early 1950s by [Bror Rexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bror_Rexed) to label portions of the [grey columns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grey_column) of the [spinal cord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinal_cord).
	
	Similar to [Brodmann areas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brodmann_area), they are defined by their cellular structure rather than by their location, but the location still remains reasonably consistent.
