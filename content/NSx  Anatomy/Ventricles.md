Lateral ventricles

* * *

* atrium of the lateral ventricle is deep to supramarginal gyrus (area 40)
* Walls of frontal horns of lateral ventricles formed by:
	* lateral walls - caudate nucleus
	* medial wall - septum pellucidum
	* roof - corpus callosum

Fourth ventricle

* * *

* The posterior surface of the [pons](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=pons) and the upper open part of the [medulla oblongata](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=medulla_oblongata) form the ==floor of the [fourth ventricle](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=fourth_ventricle).==
	* It is covered by [ependyma](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=ependyma)
* because of its rhomboid shape, is called the [rhomboid fossa](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=rhomboid_fossa).
* Obex
	* The lower end of the fossa
	* the upper end continues into the aqueduct of the midbrain.
* CSF flow
	* Inflow
		* cerebral aqueduct
	* Outflow
		* Foramina of Luschka - 2 lateral foramina
		* Foramina of Magendie - 1 midline foramen
* The dorsal and ventral cochlear nuclei of CN VIII are positioned in the lateral recess near the foramen of Luschka.

What form the lateral walls, medial wall, and roof of the frontal horns of the lateral ventricles?

* Lateral: The caudate nucleus
* Medial: septum pellucidum
* Roof:  corpus callosum
