### Background

* Tokuhashi score is specific to metastatic disease to spine
* prognostic score based on 6 elements
	* general condition
	* extraspinal bony metastasis
	* number of vertebral bodies with metastasis
	* visceral metastasis
	* primary tumor
	* neurologic compromise
* Interpretation: score of 0-8: <6 months; 9-12 > 6 months, 12-15 >1 yea

* carcinomas that commonly spread to bone include
	* breast (16-37% of breast cancer patients develop spine mets)
	* lung (12-15%)
	* thyroid (4%)
	* renal (3-6%)
	* prostate (9-15%)
