## Clinical Relevance

* In patients with CSM/OPLL/DCM, the risk factors for postoperative misalignment:
	* ==preoperative kyphosis (C2–7 angle ≤ 0–10°)==
	* ==SVA ≥ 30–42 mm==
	* ==greater T1 slope==
	* ==spondylolisthesis==
	* ==K-line (−)==
	* flex K-line (−)
	* greater flex ROM
	* gap ROM exceeding 30°
	* age over 75
	* large OPLL occupation ratio
* Surgical Approaches
	* In CSM patients ==without kyphosis==, there are no significant differences between ADF, laminoplasty only, and PDF overall.
	* In patients ==with severe local kyphosis==, ADF was the better surgery for the maintenance of the cervical alignment.
	* K-line can determine appropriate surgical procedures for patients with C-OPLL.
	* ADF and/or PDF are suitable surgical treatments for K-line (–) OPLL with local kyphosis, large OPLL, and/or cervical kyphosis.

## Metrics

### Cobb angle

* most common metric used for cervical sagital alignment (CSA)
	* measure of lordosis/kyphosis in cervical spine
* Definition
	* The Cobb angle is obtained by drawing parallel lines extending from the lower endplate of the most superior vertebral level (C2) and the lower endplate of the most inferior vertebral level (C7).
	* Perpendicular lines are then drawn from the parallel lines, and the angle of their intersection is equal to the Cobb angle.
* Population characteristics
	* average lordosis in the C2-C7 region: ==13.9 ± 12.3 degrees==
* Associations:
	* male and female lordosis is similar
	* upward trend in lordosis with age
* Abnormal > 10 degrees
* anterior approach if > 13 degrees

![[Neurosurgery/NSx  Spine/_resources/Cervical_sagittal_alignment.resources/image.1.png]]

### Cervical SVA

* Definition:
	* Horizontal distance between the posterosuperior corner of the C7 vertebral body and a plumb line drawn from the centroid of C2
	* plump line from C2 to C7
* Population characteristics:
	* C2-C7 SVA in standing asymptomatic volunteers: ==16.8 ± 11.2 mm==
	* Abnormal > 40 mm
		* associated with worse myrelopathy and disability score
* Associations:
	* C2-C7 SVA is significantly correlated with mJOA  in patients with cervical spondylotic myelopathy

![[Neurosurgery/NSx  Spine/_resources/Cervical_sagittal_alignment.resources/image.png]]

### K-line

* Definition
	* a straight line connecting the midpoints of the spinal canal at C2 and C7 on plain lateral radiograph
* as a new index to evaluate cervical alignment and OPLL size in one parameter
	* i.e., OPLL did not exceed the K-line in the K-line ( +) group and did exceed the K-line in the K-line (-) group.
* Population characteristics
	* mean K-line tilt is ==11.28 ± 8.31°==.
* Associations:
	* does K-line predict the prognosis of laminoplasty (LP) for cervical spondylotic myelopathy (CSM)? yes
		* recovery of K-line (-) group (19%) was worse than K-line ( +) group (51%, _p<0.05_)
	* The K-line tilt was correlated with the C2-C7 SVA (r = 0.81, P = 0.000) and T1S-CL (r = 0.32, P = 0.026)

### T1-slope

* Definition
	* the angle between a horizontal line and the upper end plate of T1
* T1 slope is thus analogous to the sacral slope.
	* Similar to how a greater pelvic incidence requires a greater lumbar lordosis to compensate, individuals with a greater T1 slope will also require more cervical lordosis to balance the head over the thoracic inlet
* Associations
	* Cobb's angle C2-7 gets higher as the T1 slope gets higher
		* i.e., higher lordosis required to compensate for the greater T1 slope
	* SVA C2-7 value decreases as T1 slope gets higher
