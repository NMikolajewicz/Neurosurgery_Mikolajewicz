## Summary

* Rheumatoid Cervical Spondylitis comprises of 3 specific patterns of cervical spine instability:
	* atlantoaxial subluxation (50-80%)
	* basilar invagination (40%)
	* subaxial subluxation (20%)
* Diagnosed radiographically with c-spine flex-ex x-rays
	* MRI  to measure spinal cord compression. 

## Rheumatoid Arthritis

* overview
	* chronic inflammatory symmetric polyarthritis
* signs/symptoms
	* morning stiffness, >1 hr
	* improves with activity
	* joint pain/swelling
* Labs: RF, anti-CCP (specific), ESR/CRP
* Treatment
	* steroids (prednisone; acute flairs)
	* DMARDS: methotrexate, sulfasazine, hydroxychloroquine, leflunomide
	* JAK inhibitors: toficitinib, baracitinib, upadacitinib
	* Biologics: Anti-TNF (adalimumab, infliximab, etc). IL-6, rituximab

## Patterns of spinal instability

### Atlantoaxial subluxation

* 50-80% of RA patients
* Mechanism
	* Pannus forms between dens and ring of C1.
	* Leads to destruction of transverse ligament and dens
* Diagnosis
	* anterior atlanto-dens interval (aADI)
		* instability: >3.5 mm motion between flex-ex
		* alar ligament disruption: >7 mm motion
		* surgical indication: >10 mm motion
	* Space available for cord (SAC)
		* surgical indication: <14 mm
* Management
	* posterior C1-C2 fusion
		* indications: AADI > 10 mm, SAC < 14 mm
	* occiput-C2 fusion +/- C1 arch resection
		* indication: atlantoaxial subluxation + basilar invagination

### Basilar invagination

* AKA superior migration of odontoid (SMO)
* 40% of RA patients
* Mechanism
	* Cranial migration of dens from erosion and bone loss between occiput and C1/C2
* Diagnosis
	* Ranawat C1-C2 index < 15 mm
		* measurement is same regardless of flex/ex position
	* McGregor's line > 4.5 mm
	* Chamberlain's line > 4.5 mm
	* McRae's line
* Management
	* occiput-C2 fusion
		* indication: cranial migration (> 5 mm), neurological compromise, cervicomedullary angle < 135 deg (MRI)

### Subaxial subluxation

* 20% of RA patients
* Mechanism
	* Pannus formation and soft tissue instability of facet joints and Luschka joints
* Diagnosis
	* cord compression: subaxial subluxation >4mm or >20%
	* neurological compromise: cervical height index (body height/width) < 2.0 (100% sensitive)
* Management:
	* posterior fusion and wiring
		* 20% subaxial subluxation + intractable pain or neuro symptoms.
