## Definitions

### Sagittal balance

* Center of mass is centered over the pelvis and feet
* Imbalance is defined as ==deviation in sagittal vertical axis== (SVA)
	* positive SVA: >2 cm in front of the superior posterior corner of S1.
	* global sagittal imbalance: SVA > +5 cm
* SVA: sagittal vertical axis
* SFD: sacro-femoral distance
* Global sagittal balance (GSB)
	* balanced or compensated: SVA/SFD < 0.5
	* partially compensated: SVA/SFD > 0.5
	* unbalanced: SVA/SFD > 1 or SVA > 5 cm

Other parameters used to evaluate GSB include:

* Spino-sacral angle (SSA)
* T1 pelvic angle (TPA)
* T1 spinopelvic inclination (T1SPI)
* Barrey index
* Odontoid hip axis (OD-HA)
* Full Balance Index (FBI)

### Sagittal alignment

* Whether the angular configuration of the spine permits standing with maximal muscular efficiency
* Misalignment defined as ==deviation in PT and/or PI-LL==
* Possible for spine to be balanced in the presence of misalignment due to compensatory mechanisms

## Notes

* Calculated from:
	* Sagittal vertical axis
	* C7 plumb line
* Positive saggital balance is associated with poor outcomes (i.e. higher ODI scores)
* Restoration of sagittal balance is central tenet of surgical treatment of spinal deformity
