### Background

* a ==patient-completed== questionnaire
* subjective percentage score of level of function (disability) in activities of daily living in those rehabilitating from low back pain.
* developed by Jeremy Fairbank and Graham Pynsent in Oswestry, England in 1980
* ==tool for assessment of low back pain severity==
	* considered the ‘gold standard’ of low back functional outcome tools
* Assess ==10 domains== of daily living:
	* Pain intensity
	* Personal care
	* Lifting
	* Walking
	* Sitting
	* Standing
	* Sleeping
	* Sex (if applicable)
	* Social
	* Travel
* max score 50. Calculate percentage as ODI = x/50 \* 100%
