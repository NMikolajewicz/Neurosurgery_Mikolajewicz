### Background

* Juvenile spine has inherent elasticity that allows for self reduction of intersegmental injuries but appears normal on x-ray/CT

### Risk factors in children (<9 years)

* Ligaments more elastic
* Facets have more horizontal orientation
* Higher water content in disc allows for easier mobilization
* Segments slip more readily due to anterior positions
* Uncinate processes are unformed thereby allowing lateral movement
* Growth zone susceptible to fracture
* Head is disproportionately heavy compared to neck strength

### Causes and mechanisms

* Cause
	* MVA or falls
* Mechanisms
	* flexion, hyperextension, crush
* Injury type
	* central cord, complete transection, mild partial cord injuries

### Imaging

* Abnormal MRI 22-65%
