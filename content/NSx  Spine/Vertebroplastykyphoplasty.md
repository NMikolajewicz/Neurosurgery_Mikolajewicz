### Background

* Most compression fractures heal and resolve
* There is sub population where vertebral augmentation offers relief of pain and return to pain

### Complications

* Cement leakage 30-67%
* Radiculopathy 4%
* Cord compression 0.5%

### Outcomes

* Pain relief 70-90%
	* Reduced opioid requirements
* Reduced deformity (with kyphoplasty)
	* Height restoration

### Indications

* Failed conservarice treatment (6 weeks)
* Mechanical pain
* Focal tenderness corresponding to fracture site
* Fracture pattern that is less likely to heal
