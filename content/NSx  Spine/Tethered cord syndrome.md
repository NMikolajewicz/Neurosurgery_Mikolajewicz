Tethered spinal cord syndrome is a neurologic disorder caused by tissue attachments that limit the movement of the spinal cord within the spinal column

* In meningomyelocele it is usually **impossible** to permanently untether
* Repeated untethering is advised till patient stop growing

Two criteria. 
i. Conus below level **L2**
ii. Thick filum greater than 2 mm diameter

A preop test that is strongly recommended is a cystometrogram

**characteristics used to identify the filum.** 
a. The vessel on the surface is **squiggly**
b. The color of the filum is more white than the nerve roots
