overview

* transition zone between cranium and spine
* mesodermal embryologic origin
	* appears at 3w GA
* two components
	* central pillar
		* central basioocciput
		* odontoid process (dens; c2)
		* c2 vertebral body
	* ringed structure
		* foramen magnum
		* ring of C2 vertebra (atlas)
* ligaments bind central pillar and ring

alar ligaments

* paired ligaments
* attach axis (dens) to base of skull
* =="V-shaped"== configuration
* function:
	* ==primary stabilizers== of craniocervical junction
	* ==prevent excess rotation== of atlanto-axial joint

cruciform (cruciate) ligament

* transverse and vertical parts that form cross posteriod to dens
* vertical part
	* attaches clivus to body of axis
	* function: weak, minimal stability
* transverse (part) ligament
	* a.k.a transverse atlantal ligament
	* arguable most important ligament in body
		* ==strongest ligament in spine==
	* spans behind dens and attaches to tubercle arising from medial aspect of lateral atlas
	* function:
		* ==primary stabilizer== of craniocervical junction
		* ==permits rotation== of atlanto-axial joint

tectorial membrane

* thin structure
* upward extension of ==posterior longitudinal ligament==
* posterior to cruciform ligament
* cranial portion is adherent and anatomically indistinguishable from dura

apical ligament

* attaches tip of dens to basion
* absent in 20% subjects
* function:
	* minimal contribution to stability

anterior atlanto-occipital membrane

* thin structure
* attaches ==anterior atlas== to anterior rim of foramen magnum
* function:
	* ==limits atlanto-occipital extension== at craniocervical junction

posterior atlanto-ocipital membrane

* attaches ==posterior arch of atlas== to posterior margin of foramen magnum
* continuous with posterior atlantoaxial membrane and subsequently ligamentum flavum

nuchal ligament

* cephalic extension of ==supraspinous ligament==
	* extends from C7 to inion of occipital bone
* function: ==limits hyperflexion== of cervical spine
