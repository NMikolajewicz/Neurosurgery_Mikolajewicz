## Prosencephalon

* refers to the future forebrain
* includes the telencephalon and the diencephalon
	* give rise to the cerebral hemispheres and the thalamus/hypothalamus.

![[Neurosurgery/NSx  Pediatric/_resources/Segmentationcleavagemidline_defects.resources/image.png|700]]

## Pathophysiology

* Appears during 5-8 weeks of gestation
* malformation of ventral induction
* failure of prosencephalon to grow into 2 symmetric hemisheres
* defects in lamina terminalis

### 1. Holoprosencephaly
* failure of prosencephalon to separate into 2 cerebral hemispheres
* presentation
	* severe facial malformation
	* ==cyclopia== +/- agnathia
	* posterior fossa normal

### 2. Septo-optic dysplasia
* absence of ==septum pellucideum==
* ==optic nerve== hypoplasia

### 3. Agenesis of corpus callosum
* absence of ==cingulate gyrus + corpus callosum==
* clinically silent
* +/- disconnection syndrome
* Imaging
	* <u>Frontal horns of lateral ventricles</u> widely separated and curved upwards, resembling ==buffalo horns== (coronal view)
	* <u>Third ventricles </u>elevated and interposed between lateral ventricles (normally separated by corpus callosum)
	* <u>interhemispheric fissure</u> widened

### 4. Arrhinencephaly
* absence of olfactory bulbs/tracts
	* anosmia
