## Pathophysiology
* punctate failure of dysjunction skin from neural tubes
* thin communication between dorsal skin and spinal canal
## Presentaiton
* small opening in skin above intergluteal crease
* +/- infection
## Workup
* MRI
	* look for dermoid/epidermoid tumors along course of dermal sinus tract
## Management
* surgical excision of entire tract
	* urgent to prevent infection
	* follow sinus to the bottom
		* 1/3 lead to lamina
		* 1/3 lead to dura
		* 1/3 lead intradurally
			* may cause cord tethering
