## Pathophysiology

* a.k.a. ==positional posterior plagiocephaly==
	* or occipital plagiocephaly
* flattened posterior head due to head position during sleep
* normal lambdoid sutures

## Differential Diagnosis

* lambdoid synostosis
	* rare (2% of posterior plagiocephaly cases)
	* head shape is trapezoid
	* ear position more posterior on side of flattning
	* frontal bossing is contralateral

## Presentation

* head is parallelogram
	* skull pushed ventrally on one side
* ear position more anterior on side of flattening
* frontal bossing is ipsilateral

## Management

* tummy time
* molding helmets
	* 23 h per day untill 1 year old
* no surgical treatment
