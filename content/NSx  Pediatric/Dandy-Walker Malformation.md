## Definition
**Dandy-Walker malformation (DWM)** is  most common posterior fossa malformation
triad of:
* ==vermis hypoplasia== and cephalad rotation of the vermian remnant
* ==cystic dilatation of the fourth ventricle== extending posteriorly 
* ==enlarged posterior fossa== with torcular-lambdoid inversion (the [torcula](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/confluence-of-sinuses?lang=us) lying above the level of the lambdoid due to abnormally high tentorium)
## Epidemiology
* prevalence is  1 in 30,000 live births
* accounts for ~7.5% (range 4-12%) of the cases of infantile [hydrocephalus](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/hydrocephalus?lang=us).
* It is known as the most common posterior fossa malformation
## Differential Diagnosis
* mega cisterna magna
	* normal 4th ventricle
	* normal vermis
	* no hydrocephalus
* epidermoid cyst
* retrocerebellar arachnoid cyst
	* anterior displacement of cerebellum and 4th ventricle
	* no hydrocephalus
* blake pouch cyst
	* normal vermis, not hypoplastic
	* normal torcular position
	* tetraventricular hydrocephalus
* joubert anomaly
	* vermian hypoplasia
* isolated 4th ventricle
