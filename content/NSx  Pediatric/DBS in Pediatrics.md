Deep brain stimulation (DBS) has emerged as a promising therapeutic option for various refractory neurological and neuropsychiatric conditions in pediatric patients.

> [!Target summary]
> DRE - thalamic targets
> Dystonia - GPi
> Self-injurous behavious - NAc

## Indications for DBS in children

* drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE)
* dystonia
* severe self-injurious behavior (SIB)
### Drug-Resistant Epilepsy (DRE)
* DBS reduced seizure frequency in pediatric patients with DRE.
* Efficacy: 75% of pediatric patients experienced >50% reduction in seizures following DBS, with some achieving seizure freedom.
* Targets:
	* centromedian nucleus of the ==thalamus== (CMN)
	* anterior ==thalamic== nucleus (ATN)
### Dystonia
* DBS is also utilized in managing pediatric dystonia, including primary and secondary forms
* Efficacy
	* improvements in motor function and quality of life.
	* e.g., for dyskinetic cerebral palsy, reported improvements in dystonia severity and quality of life measures following DBS.
* Targets:
	* The ==globus pallidus internus== (GPi)
### Severe Self-Injurious Behavior (SIB)
* Efficacy:
	* A phase I trial demonstrated significant reductions in SIB and associated behaviors, with improvements in quality of life and safety profiles.
* Targets:
	* ==nucleus accumbens== (NAc)
## Adverse effects
* infection
* lead migration
* hardware-related complications
## Future Directions
* refine patient selection
* optimize stimulation parameters
* expand the indications for DBS in pediatric populations.
## DBS Targets
### Centromedian Nucleus of the Thalamus (CMN)
* The CMN is located in the ==thalamus==
* Role in modulating cortical activity and seizure propagation.
* It is a common target for DBS in pediatric patients with ===drug-resistant epilepsy (DRE)===, particularly those with generalized and diffuse onset epilepsies.
### Anterior Thalamic Nucleus (ATN)
* The ATN is another thalamic target situated in the anterior part of the thalamus.
* It is involved in the ==limbic system ==and has connections to the hippocampus and cingulate gyrus.
### Globus Pallidus Internus (GPi)
* ==GPi== is located in the basal ganglia and is a critical component of the motor circuit.
* It is the primary target for DBS in pediatric patients with ==dystonia==, including primary and secondary forms.
### Nucleus Accumbens (NAc)
* The ==NAc== is part of the ventral striatum and is involved in ==reward and motivation== pathways.
* DBS targeting the NAc is used for severe ==self-injurious== behavior (SIB) in children, particularly those with ==autism== spectrum disorder.
### Subthalamic Nucleus (STN)
* The ==STN== is located below the ==thalamus== and is part of the basal ganglia circuitry.
* It is less commonly targeted in pediatric DBS but has been used in cases of refractory ==dystonia and epilepsy.==
### Pedunculopontine Nucleus (PPN)
* The PPN is located in the brainstem and is involved in gait and postural control.
* It has been investigated as a DBS target in pediatric patients with ==dystonia==, particularly those with axial and orofacial symptoms.
### Dentate Nucleus (DN)
* The DN is located in the cerebellum and is involved in motor coordination.
* DBS targeting the DN has been explored in patients with ==dyskinetic cerebral palsy== (DCP)
