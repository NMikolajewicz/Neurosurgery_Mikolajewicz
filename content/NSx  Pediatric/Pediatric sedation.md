### Conscious sedation
* PO: ==chloral hydrate== 50 mg/kg PO/PR + 24 mg/kg x1 PRN
### Procedures
* IM: ==ketamine== 4 mg/kg, then 2 mg/kg x2 PRN
* IV:
	* ==fentanyl== 1-2 mcg/kg
		 or
	* ==morphine== 0.1-0.2 mg/kg
	* ==medazolam== 0.5 mg/kg (titrate 2-4 doses)
