### Background
* also known as benign external hydrocephalus (BEH)
### Clinical characteristics
* macrocephaly
* frontal bossing
* neurologically intact
* no history of trauma
### Epidemiology
* most common cause of macrocephaly
* males > females
* family history is common
### Associations
* scaphocephaly
### Radiographic findings
* widening of bifrontal/anterior CSF spaces
* no flattening of adjacent gyri
* CSF space follows gyral contours
* usually normal sulci posteriorly
* anterior fontanelle frequently enlarged
* normal ventricular calibre
### Differential diagnosis
* subdural fluid collection
	* distinguish with cortical vein sign
	* BESSI - cortical veins adjacent to inner table
	* SDH
		* cortical veins displaced away from inner table
		* collection may have mass effect
* cerebral atrophy
	* associated with small head circumference
### Treatment and prognosis
* resolves spontaneously by 2 years age
* macrocephaly may persist
* good developmental prognosis
