## Components
* mental status
* cranial nerves
* motor
* sensory
* cerebellar function
* reflexes

* * *

### 1. mental status
* baby easily arousable with minimal stimuli
* irritable
* lethargic with weak cry
### 2. cranial nerves
* I
	* cannot assess
* II
	* blink response to strong light
	* pupillary reflex (direct and consensual)
	* tracking objects
* III/IV/VI
	* spontaneous eye movement
	* conjugate eye movements
	* tracks objects
	* vestibulo-ocular reflex
* V
	* mastication
	* suck and swallow
* VII
	* facial symmetry
	* sucking and crying
* VIII
	* reaction to sharp sounds in each ear (e.g., clapping)
* IX/X
	* gag reflex
* XI
	* shoulder droop (SCM/trapezius)
### 3. motor exam
* spontaneous movement + tone
	* asymmetry in posture/movement?
* pull from laying to sitting position
	* head lag?
* hold under axilla
	* hypotonia?
	* slips through hands?
* place finger in baby's palm (<5 months)
	* grasp reflex?
* extend limbs and release
	* limb recoil? (present after 34w GA)
### 4. sensory exam
* grimace or withdrawal of limbs in response to pain in extremities
### 5. reflexes
* moro reflex
	* change in head position
	* arm extension and spread fingers
* rooting reflex
	* stroking cheek causes turning of head towards stimulus
* patellar/bicep/ankle appears at 33 weeks
### 6. others
* head circumference
	* trend over time
* fontanelles
	* sunken, flat, full, bulging
* bradycardia / desaturations / apneic spells
* foot symmetry/size
* sacral/coccygeal dimple
	* asymmetry of gluteal folds
	* tufts of hair
