## Quick practice guidelines

* **Primary targets:**
	* Prevent brain hypoxia—treat when **PbtO₂ <20 mmHg**
	* keep **PaO₂ 80–120 mmHg**; maintain adequate **CPP**.
* **Adjuncts:**
	* Short **FiO₂ escalations** to correct low PbtO₂;
	* optimize ventilation/PEEP;
	* consider red cell mass, sedation, temperature, and seizure control to reduce CMRO₂.
* **Avoid:** Routine steroids; routine/prolonged severe hyperventilation; permissive hypoxemia.

## BOOST Trial Series

The Brain Oxygen Optimization in Severe TBI (BOOST) trials represent the most significant evidence for PbtO2-guided therapy:
**BOOST-II Results:**

* Phase II randomized trial of 119 severe TBI patients across 10 US ICUs[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih+1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5679063/)
* **Primary outcome:** ==PbtO2+ICP monitoring reduced proportion of time with brain tissue hypoxia== (0.16 vs 0.45, p<0.0001)[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5679063/)
* Trend toward lower mortality (25% vs 34%) and better outcomes in PbtO2+ICP group[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5679063/)
* Safe and feasible implementation of multimodal monitoring protocol[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5679063/)

**BOOST-III (Ongoing):**

* Phase III trial enrolling 1,094 patients across 47 level 1 trauma centers[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih+1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8915289/)
* Primary endpoint: Glasgow Outcome Scale-Extended at 180 days[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8915289/)
* Uses sliding dichotomy based on baseline severity predicted by IMPACT core model[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8915289/)

## PbtO2 Treatment Thresholds

**Current Recommendations:**

* **PbtO2 <20 mmHg** commonly used as treatment threshold based on observational studies[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih+1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4564619/)
* Some studies suggest **PbtO2 <15 mmHg** for more aggressive intervention[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5679063/)
* **Level III:** Insufficient evidence for specific PbtO2 targets, but ==PbtO2 <20-25 mmHg associated with poor outcomes==[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)

## Arterial Oxygenation Targets

**Current Evidence:**

* Traditional ARDS targets (PaO2 55-80 mmHg) may be inadequate for TBI patients[frontiersin+1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2022.999885/full)
* **R==ecommended PaO2 targets:==** ==80-120 mmHg== in patients with clinically significant ICP elevation[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7655906/)
* High-normal PaO2 levels reasonable for severe TBI with ARDS[frontiersin](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2022.999885/full)

## Normobaric Hyperoxia Studies

**Clinical Trial Evidence:**

* Menzel et al. (1999): Increasing FiO2 to 100% for 6 hours increased brain PO2 by 359% and reduced brain lactate by 40%[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10389873/)
* Taher et al. (2016): Randomized trial of 68 severe TBI patients showed improved outcomes with 80% vs 50% oxygen in first 6 hours[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4869427/)
* Vilalta et al. (2011): Normobaric hyperoxia improved brain redox state in patients with baseline brain lactate >3 mmol/L[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3136740/)

## Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

**Clinical Evidence:**

* Multiple Phase I trials showed reduced ICP and improved brain metabolism[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih+1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6909681/)
* HOBIT trial (ongoing): Phase II adaptive trial for acute severe TBI[clinicaltrials+1](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02407028)
* Consistent preclinical evidence but limited large-scale clinical validation[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih+1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29132229/)

## Hyperventilation Guidelines

**Brain Trauma Foundation Recommendations:**

* **Level II B:** Prolonged ==prophylactic hyperventilation with PaCO2 ≤25 mmHg is== **==not recommended==**[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)
* Hyperventilation may be used as temporizing measure for acute ICP elevation[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)
* Avoid during first 24 hours when cerebral blood flow often critically reduced[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)
* If used, monitor with SjO2 or PbtO2 to assess oxygen delivery[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)

**Permissive Hypercapnia Considerations:**

* ==PEEP up to 12 mmHg safe in TBI patients== without significant ICP elevation[frontiersin](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2022.999885/full)
* Monitor respiratory compliance and CPP when using higher PEEP[frontiersin](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2022.999885/full)
* Personalized PCO2 goals based on ICP monitoring in severe cases[frontiersin](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/medicine/articles/10.3389/fmed.2022.999885/full)

## Jugular Venous Oxygen Saturation

**Recommendations:**

* **Level III:** Jugular venous saturation <50% may be threshold to avoid for reduced mortality[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)
* AVDO2 monitoring provides assessment of global cerebral oxygen extraction[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27654000/)

## Current Trial Landscape

### Active Phase III Trials

**BOOST-III (NCT03754114):**

* 1,094 patients across US and Canadian centers[bmjopen.bmj+1](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/12/3/e060188)
* Primary completion expected 2024-2025[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8915289/)

**BONANZA (ACTRN12619001328167):**

* 860 patients in European centers[kce.fgov](https://kce.fgov.be/en/kce-trials/funded-trials/bonanza-a-randomised-controlled-trial-that-is-testing-whether-a-management-strategy-guided-by-early)
* Comparing PbtO2+ICP vs ICP-only management[kce.fgov](https://kce.fgov.be/en/kce-trials/funded-trials/bonanza-a-randomised-controlled-trial-that-is-testing-whether-a-management-strategy-guided-by-early)

**OXY-TC Results:**

* Recent European trial showed no difference in 6-month outcomes[bmjopen.bmj+1](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/10/8/e040550)
* Subgroup analysis suggested benefit in patients with elevated baseline ICP[thelancet](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/laneur/article/PIIS1474-4422(24)00023-1/fulltext)

### Meta-Analysis Evidence

Recent systematic reviews provide mixed evidence for multimodal monitoring:
**Utsumi et al. (2024):**

* Meta-analysis of 5 RCTs (512 patients)[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39147023/)
* No significant difference in favorable neurologic outcomes (RR 1.21, 95% CI 0.93-1.58)[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39147023/)
* No difference in survival (RR 1.10, 95% CI 0.99-1.21)[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39147023/)
* ==Concluded insufficient evidence that PbtO2+ICP superior to ICP alone==[pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39147023/)
