triphasic response of pituitary stalk injury

"Stun, spill, die"

DI arises from AVP neuron stunning/severing, leading to polyuria and hypernatremia; SIADH follows as damaged neurons unpredictably release AVP, worsening hyponatremia; final phase is depletion, causing chronic DI.

Serum sodium and urine osmolality trends track these phases closely

DI is managed with DDAVP and sodium correction, SIADH with fluid restriction

MRI is valuable for confirming posterior pituitary
