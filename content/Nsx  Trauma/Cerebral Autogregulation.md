Cerebrovascular Reactivity

# Pressure Reactivity Index (PRx)

	The PRx represents the gold standard for continuous bedside cerebrovascular reactivity monitoring
		defined as the Pearson correlation coefficient between thirty consecutive 10-second averages of MAP and intracranial pressure over 5 minutes of data, updated every minute.
		
		==positive PRx (>0.3) indicates impaired cerebral autoregulation== where MAP increases lead to ICP increases
		
		negative PRx values suggest intact compensatory cerebrovascular responses.
		
	validated to accurately detect the lower limit of autoregulation in animal models and shows strong association with unfavorable outcomes following severe traumatic brain injury.
	
	The continuous nature of PRx monitoring allows real-time visualization through risk bar charts, with sustained positive values creating "red solid line" patterns that precede refractory intracranial hypertension and brain death
