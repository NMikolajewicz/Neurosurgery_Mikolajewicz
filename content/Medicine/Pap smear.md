* What is the _recommended management_ for women age 25 and older with **atypical glandular cells of undetermined significance (AGUS)** discovered on Pap testing and **no HPV testing**? **Colposcopy**

![[Neurosurgery/00_Neurosurgery_Mikolajewicz/content/Medicine/_resources/Pap_smear.resources/Image 20231112 160603.jpeg]]

**Atypical glandular cells not otherwise specified (AGC-NOS)** are the highest grade lesion that can be found on **pap smear**

* _it is associated with a 17% rate of cancer (8% carcinoma in situ, and 9% invasive carcinoma)_
* _This lesion has higher risk than HSIL, which has a 3% associated cancer rate_
