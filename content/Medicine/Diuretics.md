Diuretic indications

* Edema
	* Edema: accumulation of fluid inn extacellular (interstitial space)
	* MOA:
		1. renal excretion of Na+ and H2O reduces plasma volume
		2. excretion result is in hemoconcentration and increase in oncotic pressure
		3. This  attracts water resulting in fluid shift from interstitium into capillary bed
		4. resolution of edema.
* Antihypertensive therapy
	* MOA: reduce peripheral resistance
* CHF
	* MOA:
		* lower peripheral resistance
		* increase cardiac output (reduced after load)
		* reduce venous congestion; ankle edema, hepatic enlargement
	* agents: thiazides, K-sparing diuretics, loop diuretics
* renal failure prophylaxis
	* in context of circulatory failure, renal perfusion and urine production may cease
		* e.g., massive transfusion, shock
	* MOA: maintain urine flow
	* agents: osmotic, loop diuretics

Agents

* * *

Osmotic diuretics

* agents: ==mannitol, sorbitol==
* site of action: ==proximal tubules==
* MOA:
	* osmotic diuretics are not reabsorbed into tubules after glomerular filtration, therefore retain water in tubular lumen via osmotic pressure
* indications
	* renal hypovolemia failure prophylaxis
	* brain edema
	* acute glaucoma

Carbonic anhydrase (CAH) inhibitors

* agents (_\-zolamide_): ==acetazolamide, methazolamide, brinzolamide, dorzolamide==
* site of action: ==proximal tubules==
* MOA:
	* catalyze H + HCO2 <-> H2CO3 <-> H2O + CO2
	* CAH used in tubule cells to generate H+ which is secreted into tubular fluid in exchange for Na.
	* Tubular H+ then captures HCO3 to form CO2
	* CO2 is absorbed by tubule cells and used to regenerate H and HCO3.
	* If CAH is inhibited, less HCO3, Na and water is reabsorbed, resulting in HCO3 loss and acidosis.
* indications
	* acute glaucoma
	* acute mountain sickness
	* epilepsy

loop diuretics

* agents: ==furosemide, tosemide, piretanide, numetanide==
* efficacy:
	* with PO intake, ==diuresis occurs within 1 h for 4 h duration.==
	* effect is rapid, intense and brief
* site of action: ==thick portion of ascending limb of Henle's loop==
* MOA:
	* inhibit Na/K/2Cl cotransport
	* results in excretion of H2O + Na/K/2Cl
	* Ca and Mg excretion also increases
* toxicity:
	* reversible hearing loss
	* sensitivity to renotoxic agents
* indications:
	* pulmonary edema
	* refractory to thiazides
	* actue renal hypovolemic failure prophylaxis
	* hypercalcemia

Thiazides

* agents: ==hydrochlorothiazide, indapamide, benzthiazide, trichlormethiazide, cyclothiazide, chlorthalidone==
* site of action: ==intermediate segment of distal tubules==
* MOA:
	* inhibits reabsorption of NaCl (Na/Cl cotransport) and water
	* Ca excretion decreases
	* Mg excretion increases
* Indications
	* HTN
	* cardiac failure
	* mobilizaton of edema
	* urolithiasis
	* nephrogenic diabetes insipidus
* complications: hypercalcemia

Potassium-sparing diuretics

* agents: ==spironolactone==
* site of action: distal portion of distal tubule, proximal part of collecting duct
* efficacy:
	* low diuretic effect
	* ==time of onset: several days==
* MOA:
	* inhibits
* complications
	* gynecomastia
* indications:
	* conditions with increased aldosterone secretion; e.g., liver cirrhosis with ascites
	* congestive heart failure
