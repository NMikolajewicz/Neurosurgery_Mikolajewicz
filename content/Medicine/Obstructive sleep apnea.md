STOP-BANG

* Subjective
	* Snore
	* Tired
	* Observed pause in breathing
	* BP elevated or being treated
* Objective
	* BMI >35
	* Age > 50
	* Neck circumference (>40 cm)
	* Gender

Gold standard diagnosis: polysomnography
First line management: CPAP
