Workup

* Physical: petechiae, purpura, ecchymoses, splenomegaly
* CBC (repeat)
* blood film
* DIC panel: PT/PTT, fibrinogen, D-dimer
* HIV/HCV testing
* +/- hematology consult

General Management (see below for specific conditions):

* activity restriction (e.g., boxing, rugby, martial arts)
* hold anticoagulation if PLT < 50,000/micoL
* hold ASA, NSAID
* PLT transfusion if bleeding in severe thrombocytopenia
* treat cause

Notes

* Causes: 1) production, 2) destruction, 3) sequestration
* isolated thombocytopenia does not protect against venous/arterial thrombosis

* * *

Differential Diagnosis

* TTP/DIC/HUS
	* RBC fragments on blood film
* ITP
	* normal film
	* low PLT
	* responsive to steroids
* Splenomegaly
* HELLP syndrome (pregnancy)
* chronic liver disease
	* reduced TPO
	* e.g., alcoholic hepatitis, NAFLD
* Viral infection
	* rubella, mumps, varicella, parvovirus, HCV, EBV, COVID, HIV
* HIT

Bicytopenia (anemia + thrombocytopenia)

* DIC
* TTP, HUS, DITMA
* autoimmune
* nutrient deficiencies
* infections
* BM disorders

* * *

**Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (TTP)**

* * *

Pathophysiology

* ADAMTS-13 deficiency
* formation of hyaline clots that shred RBCs and PLTs
* complications: obstruction of flow to kidneys (renal failure), brain (stroke)

Presentation

* FAT RN
	* fever
	* anemia (MAHA)
	* thrombocytopenia
	* renal failure
	* neurosymptoms (stroke)

Workup

* CBC
* blood smear
	* schistocytes (fragmented RBCs)
* DIC panel: PT/PTT, fibrinogen, D-dimer normal

Diagnosis

* thrombocytopenia with schistocytes
* normal DIC panel

Management

* exchange transfusion
* NEVER transfuse PLTs for TTP

Prognosis / treatment response

* Improvement in neurological signs is an initial indicator of response to plasma exchange.
	* mean time for resolution of the neurologic deficit in TTP is 3 days
* Everything else takes longer (e.g. LDH, platelets, renal function, anemia)

* * *

**Disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC)**

* * *

Pathophysiology

* fibrin clot formation after acute illness
* depletes all factors/PLTs
* i.e., problem with loss of hemostasis

Presentation

* sick for another reason (e.g., sepsis)
* acute illness followed by bleeding

Workup

* CBC
* blood smear
	* schistocytes
* DIC panel
	* ↑ PT/PTT
	* ↓ fibrinogen
	* ↑ D-dimer

Management

* supportive
* address underlying disease
* tranfuse deficits

* * *

**Heparin-induced thromocytopenia (HIT)**

* * *

Pathophysiology

* anti-platelet antibodies
* occurs 7-14 days after starting heparin

Presentation

* clot when they shouldn't
* bleed when they shouldn't

Workup

* HIT Ab positive

Management

* stop heparin
	* never use again
* anticoagulate
	* argatroban
	* bridge to warfarin

* * *

**Immune thrombocytopenic purpura (ITP)**

* * *

Pathophysiology

* anti-platelet antibodies?
* Preceded by viral infection

Workup

* diagnosis of exclusion
	* rule out other causes of thrombocytopenia
* no diagnosis test

Management

* steroids
* IVIG
* splenectomy (curative)
* rituximab
