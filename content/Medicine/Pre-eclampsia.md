What is the _next step in management_ in a pregnant patient at **37 weeks gestation**and **pre-eclampsia**?

* **Vaginal examination** 
* **calculation of Bishop score**
* ==_Immediate induction of labour_== _is the best management option_
	* _However, induction of labour should not be attempted before evaluating the likelihood of success by vaginal examination and assessing cervical characteristics and calculating the Bishop score_

What is the _next step in management_ in a pregnant patient with **eclampsia** and **fetal bradycardia**?

* **Magnesium sulfate**
* _Fetal bradycardia is an expected occurrence during an eclamptic seizure and does not necessitate an emergency cesarean section unless it fails to resolve within a few minutes_

_Blood pressure management_

* _do not prescribe antihypertensive therapy for mild hypertension in the context of preeclampsia, which we define as blood pressures consistently less than 150/100 mmHg_
* In patients with **severe pre-eclampsia**, antihypertensive drugs are used to ==maintain blood pressure <== **==160==**==/==**==105==**==mmHg==

Bishop score
_This score is calculated based on_

* _cervical position_
* _consistency_
* _effacement_
* _dilatation_
* _station of the fetal head._

_Each of these five components is graded from zero to three. A score or 6 or higher are associated with a high likelihood of successful vaginal delivery with induction of labour._ ==_If the score is < 6 cervical ripening agents can be used to augment_== _the score before induction of labour._
