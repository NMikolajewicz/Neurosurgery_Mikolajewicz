Pathophysiology

* CD4 T cell entry via CXCR4 and CCR5

Risk factors

* sex (0.2%)
	* receptive partner higher risk
* IVDU (0.3%)
* vertical transmission (25%)

* * *

Presentation

* * *

Acute retroviral syndrome

* acute HIV infection
* symptoms
	* flu-like
	* myalgias
	* arthralgias
	* lymphadenopathy
	* febrile
* workup
	* influenza and EBV negative
	* HIV serology negative
		* ELISA/HIV Ab (-)
		* 3-4 week window
	* PCR viral load positive (+)

Opportunistic infections

* immunocompromised: CD4 < 200
* preventable with asymptomatic screening
* workup
	* ELISA/HIV Ab positive (+)
		* confirm with western blot
	* PCR viral load
		* characterize trajectory
	* CD4 count
		* current status
	* genotype
		* can inform adjunt therapy in 2+1 tx
* CD4 counts
	* CD4 > 500
		* pathogens: normal
		* prophylaxis: none
	* CD4 > 350
		* pathogens: increased frequency of normal
		* prophylaxis: none
	* CD4 < 200
		* pathogens: ==pneumocystic jiroveci pneumonia (PJP)==
		* prophylaxis: ==septra,== dapsone, atovaquone
	* CD4 < 100
		* pathogens: ==toxoplasmosis==
		* prophylaxis: ==septra,== pyrimethamine + leucovorin
	* CD4 < 50
		* pathogens: ==mycobacterium avium complex (MAC)==
		* prophylaxis: ==azithromycin==
* Other opportunistic pathogens
	* TB
	* cryptosporidium
	* candida

* * *

Management

* * *

acute retroviral syndrome

* 2+1 ARVs

mainstay treatment: antiretrovirals (ARVs)

* ==2+1 (2 NRTI + protease/entry/fusion inhibitors)==

Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP)

* 2 agents
	* ==emtricitabine==
	* ==tenofavir==

Post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP)

* same as PrEP
* 2 agents
	* emtricitabine
	* tenofavir

Pregnancy

* 2+1 therapy with AZT for vertical transmission prophylaxis

* * *

Additional Notes

* * *

* When the **possibility of acute or early HIV infection** is being considered,  perform the most sensitive diagnostic tests available
	* sensitive test: HIV virologic (viral load) test.
	* also send antigen/antibody test
		* negative antigen/antibody test with detectable HIV viral load suggests early HIV infection.
