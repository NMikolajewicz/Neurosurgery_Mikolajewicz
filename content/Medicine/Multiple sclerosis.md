multiple sclerosis

* autoimmune demyelinating disease

presentation

* women, 20-40 years
* neurological symptoms separted by space and time
* relapsing and remitting variant
* optic neuritis
	* blurry vision
	* EOM pain

workup

* MRI
	* periventricular white matter plaques
* lumbar puncture
	* oligoclonal bands

management

* flare - ==steroids==
* chronic - ==IFN, glatiramer, fingolimod==

complications

* urinary retention - bethanechol
* urinary incontinence - amitriptyline
* spasms - baclofen
* neuropathic pain - gabapentin
