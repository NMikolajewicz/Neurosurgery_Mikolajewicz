Adult bradycardia

* * *

Unstable patient

* acute AMS
* signs of shock
* hypotension
* ischemic chest discomfort
* acute heart failure

Management

* atropine
* transcutaneous pacing
* dopamine
* epinephrine
	* 2-10 mcg / min infusion
