AML (acute myeloid leukemia)

* age: >60 y
* risk: benzene, radiation, CML
* presentation
	* acute
	* bone pain
	* fever
	* infection
* workup
	* neutrophilic leukocytosis
	* blood smear with blasts
		* ==auer rods (M3 variant)==
	* bone marrow biopsy
		* ==\>20% blasts==
	* myeloid marker: ==myeloperoxidase (+)==
* management
	* M3 variant - ==transretinoic acid (vitamin A)==
	* all other AMLs - ==chemotherapy==

ALL (acute lymphocytic leukemia)

* age: pediatric
* presentation
	* acute
	* bone pain
	* fever
	* infection
* workup
	* blood smear with blasts
	* bome marrow biopsy
		* \>==20% blasts==
	* lymphoid marker: ==cALLa (+) and Tdt (+)==
* Management
	* ==chemotherapy==
	* +/- ==radiation==
	* ARA-C prophylaxis for CNS intrathecally

CML (chronic myeloid leukemia)

* age: middle age (40s)
* presentation
	* asymptomatic
* workup
	* neutrophilic leukocytosis
	* bone marrow biopsy
		* no blasts
		* ==philadelphia chromosome (t9;22) - BCR-ABL==
* management
	* ==imatinib==
* complications
	* blast crisis - AML

CLL (chronic lymphoid leukemia)

* age: elder, geriatric (80 y)
* presentation
	* severe leukocytosis
	* typically asymptomatic
	* if symptomatic
		* hyperviscosity syndrome
			* headache
			* bleeding
* workup
	* bone marrow biopsy
	* ==smudge cells on smear==
* management
	* \>65 y and asymptomatic
		* ==monitor==
	* \>65 y and symptomatic
		* ==chemotherapy==
	* <65 y and available donor
		* ==stem cell transplant==
