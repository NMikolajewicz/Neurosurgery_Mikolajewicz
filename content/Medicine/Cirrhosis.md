Workup

* CBC (HB, PLT)
* electrolytes (+ extended)
* Creatinine
* Group & Screen
* Liver enzymes
	* LFT: AST/ALT/ALP
	* synthetic: BILI, ALB, INR, glucose
* viral hepatitis serology
* autoimmune workup
	* anti-SM IgG
	* ANA
	* anti-mitochondrial IgG
	* anti-LMK
* ceruloplasmin
* ferritin, iron, transferrin
* urine electrolytes/microscopy
	* r/o hepatorenal syndrome
* diagnosis paracentesis
	* r/o SBP

Complications (==VASE==)

* acute
	* ==variceal bleeding==
		* Px: rapid GI bleed in context of cirrhosis
			* coffee ground emesis
			* hematoemesis
			* melena
			* anemia signs
		* Dx: OGD
		* Mx:
			* 2 large bore IVs
			* BPO
			* pantoprazole IV
			* octerotide
			* ceftriaxone (survival benefit)
			* urgent scope and banding
	* ==spontaneous bacterial peritonitis (SBP)==
		* Px: acute abdominal pain, fever, encephalopathy
		* Dx: paracentesis
			* neutrophils > 250
			* positive gram stain
		* Tx: ceftriaxone 2g IV q24h
	* ==Encephalopathy==
		* Px: confusion, asterixis
		* Dx: clinical
		* Tx: lactulose
			* titrate to LOC
* chronic
	* ==ascites==
		* Px: clinical
		* Dx: imaging
			* U/S, CT, MRI
		* Tx:
			* salt restriction
			* spironolactone + lasix
				* 5:2 ratio
			* therapeutic paracentesis
	* ==HCC==
		* Px:
			* weight loss/cachexia
			* abdominal pain
				
		* Dx:
			* U/S screen q6 months
			* spiral CT
		* Tx: surgery
