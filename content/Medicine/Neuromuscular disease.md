**Myasthenia gravis**

* * *

pathophysiology

* autoimmune disease
* anti-Ach receptor antibodies
	* block ACh receptors
	* more ACh required to outcompete antibodies
	* fatigable

presentation

* fatigable weakness
	* most comonly eyes, throat, fine movement of distal extremities
	* prosis, trouble swalling, etc.
	* worse in evening

workup

* ACh-R antibodies
* EMG
* CT
	* r/o thymoma

management

* cholinesterase inhibitors
	* pyridostigmine
* steroids (refractory disease)
* IVIG - severe

* * *

**Lambert-eaton myasthenic syndrome (LEMS)**

* * *

pathophysiology

* paraneoplastic syndrome
* anti-calcium channel antibodies
	* presynaptic calcium channels

presentation

* improvement with use
* muscles that are used less are worst (i.e., proximal muscles)
* inability to rise out of chair, shoulder girdle

workup

* anti-Ca channel IgGs
* EMG
* CT
	* r/o SCLC

* * *

**Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)**

* * *

pathophysiology

* sporadic mutation resulting in UMN/LMN lesions
* sensory spared

presentation

* LMN
	* areflexia
	* weakness
	* fasciculations
* UMN
	* hyperreflexia with weakness
* different spinal levels impacted
* asymmetric

workup

* EMG

management

* supportive
	* eventually all muscles die and they cannot breath
* riluzole
	* extends life 3 years
