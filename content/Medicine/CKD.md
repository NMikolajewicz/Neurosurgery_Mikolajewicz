Definition: renal injury > 3 months

Common causes:

* diabetes
* HTN
* chronic glomerulonephritis
* others:
	* PCKD, reflux/obstructive nephropathy

Severity grading

* Grade I - GFR > 90
	* Mx: prevent progression
* Grade II - GFR 60-90
	* Mx: prevent progression
* Grade III - GFR 30-60
	* Mx: manage complications
* Grade IV - GFR 15-30
	* Mx: prepare for dialysis (AV fistula)
* Grade V - GFR < 15; ESRD
	* Mx: hemodialysis

Work up

* urinalysis
* 24 h urine for protein
* creatinine, GFR
* UPEP
* ANA, C3/C4, Hep B/C, ANCA, cryo, SPEP
* U/S abdo
	* small kidneys = atrophy = CKD
* biopsy
	* if unexplained renal failure

Management

* progression prevention
	* if HTN, BP target < 130/80
		* ==ACEi/ARB==
	* if diabetic, A1C target < 7
		* ==avoid metformin== - causes lactic acidosis
		* insulin/sulfonylurea cleared by kidneys - risk of hypoglycemia
	* proteinurea
		* ==low protein diet==
		* ACEi/ARB
* dialysis
	* hemodialysis
		* requires AV fistula
		* usually 3x/week, 4h per session
	* peritoneal dialysis
		* requires peritoneal catheter
		* nightly, 6-8h each session

complications

* acidosis
	* target: HCO3 > 22
	* Mx: oral NaHCO3
* anemia
	* target: HB > 100, ferritin > 500, Tsat > 30
	* Mx: iron replacement, EPO
* hyperkalemia
	* target: K < 5
	* Mx: low K diet, diuretics, avoid NSAIDs
* hyperphosphatemia
	* target: normal range
	* Mx: low PO4 diet, PO4 binder (calcium carbonate)
* hypocalcemia
	* target: normal range
	* Mx: calcitriol (vit D)
* tertiary hyperparathyroidism
	* target: 3-9x ULN
	* Mx: calcitriol (if PO4 low/normal), cinacalcet, parathyroidectomy
* HTN target < 130/80
	* Mx: diuretics/ACEi/ARB
* volume overload
	* Mx: loop diuretics
	* maintain urine output to delay onset of dialysis

PEACHED

* proteinuria
	* ACEi
* electrolyte distrubances
* anemia
	* EPO
* hypocalcemia
	* vit D-dependent
* HTN
	* ACEi/ARB
* euvolemia
* drug dosing
