insulin sliding scale protocol

* check BG TID prior to meals and QHS
* administer rapid-acting insulin (aspart or lispro) per sliding scale
* Sliding scale:
	* BG < 4 = give juice, notify MD
	* BG 4-12 = no insulin needed
	* BG 12-14 = insulin 2 U
	* BG 14-16 = insulin 4 U
	* BG 16-18 = insulin 6 U
	* BG 18-20 = insulin 8 U
	* BG > 20 = insulin 10 U, notify MD

Insulin Dosing

* total daily insulin (TDI): 0.5 U/kg
* coverage:
	* basal coverage
		* ensure insulin always present in body
		* long-acting: lantus, levenir
	* pos-prandial coverage
		* control glucose from eating
		* rapid-acting: humalog, novolog
* strategy
	* divide TDI into 50% basal and 50% bolus
	* basal insulin 1x daily
	* bolus insulin 3x daily, with each meal
	* in hospital, include sliding scale as well
	* therefore: **basal-bolus + sliding scale**

Insulin types

* rapid-acting
	* lispro (humalog), aspar (novorapid)
	* inject 15-30 min before meals
		* typically used in conjunction with long-acting insulin
* long-acting
	* glargine (lantus), detemir (levermir)
	* inject once daily

Glucose conversion (CAD vs USA)

* blood glucose expressed in different units in Canada and USA
	* Canada: mmol/L
	* USA: mg/dL
* 1 mmol/L = 18 mg/dL glucose
* 0.0555 mmol/L = 1 mg/dL glucose
