### Counselling about steroid treatment
**Early common side effects:**
* Psych: elevated mood, hallucinations, depression, insomnia (psychosis and suicidality more common if pre-existing psychiatric disorder)
* Endocrine: hyperglycemia, hypertension

**Predictable long term side effects:**
* H&N: glaucoma, cataracts, puffiness
* CVS: accelerated atherosclerosis, hypertension, edema
* MSK: osteoporosis, fractures
* Infectious: increased common and opportunistic infections, reactivated TB, Hepatitis B/C
* Skin: fragility, bruising, acne, purple/pink abdominal striae
* Endocrine: diabetes, weight gain, fat redistribution
* Avascular necrosis of the hip. Watch for sudden groin pain, needs urgent medical attention for imaging and probable surgery.
