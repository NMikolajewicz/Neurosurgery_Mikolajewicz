Lymph nodes

* malignant: fixed, firm, non-tender
* reactive (benign): mobile, soft, tender

Workup

* excisional biopsy
	* NOT FNA
* gram stain
* AFB stain
* fungal workup
* staging
	* CXR
	* CT CAP
	* bone marrow biopsy
	* PET scan

Lymphoma staging

* Ann Arbor system
* Criteria
	* number of lymph nodes
	* lymph node location relative to diaphragm
* Stages
	* I: 1 LN
	* II: >1 LN above diaphragm
	* III: >1 LN below diaphragm
	* IV: metastatic
* Quick staging with CXR (I/II vs. III/IV)

Hodgkins Lymphoma

* * *

Prognosis: remission or cure (80%)

Characteristics

* reed sternberg cells
* Pel-Epstein fevers - cyclic fevers
* Alcohol lymph nodes
	* lymphadenopathy becomes painful with alcohol consumption
* Spreads contiguously
	* tumor burden near tumor origin
	* easier to target

Presentation

* initial presentation stage IIa or better
* constitutional symptoms
	* fevers
	* night sweats
	* weight loss

Management:

* chemotherapy
	* ABVD: doxorubicin, bleomycin, vinblastine, dacarbazine

				  or

* BEACOPP (if severe):  Bleomycin, Etoposide, Doxorubicin (also called Adriamycin), Cyclophosphamide, Vincristine (also called Oncovin), Procarbazine, Prednisolone (a steroid)

* * *

Non-Hodgkins Lymphoma

* * *

Prognosis: can achieve remission, recur in 7-10 years

Characteristics

* burkitt's lymphoma - starry sky pattern on histology
* extra-nodal disease
* spreads hematogenously
	* distal metastases

Presentation

* initial presentation stage IIa or worse
* constitutional symptoms are rare

Management

* rituximab

		  or

* R-CHOP
	* Chemotherapeutics: cyclophosphamide, doxorubicin hydrochloride (hydroxydaunomycin),  vincristine sulfate (Oncovin)
	* Targeted therapy: rituximab
	* Steroids: prednisone

* * *

Chemotherapeutics

* * *

* Vincristine & Vinblastine → peripheral neuropathy
	* R-CHOP, ADVD
* Adriomycin (Doxorubicin) → cardiomyopathy
	* R-CHOP, ADVD, BEACOPP
* Bleomycin → pulmonary fibrosis
	* ADVD, BEACOPP
* Cisplatin → ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity
* Cyclophosphamide → hemorrhagic cystitis
	* R-CHOP, BEACOPP
