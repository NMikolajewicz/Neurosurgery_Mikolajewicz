Sepsis: organ dysfunction caused by response to infection
Septic shock: decreased end-organ perfusion in septic patient

* septic shock  = sepsis + shock despite adequate IVF resuscitation (30 cc/kg)
* Criteria
	* vasopressin requirement to maintain MAP > 60
		* MAP = (1/3)SBP + (2/3)DBP
	* lactate > 2

qSOFA (sequential organ failure assessment)

* identifies high-risk patients for in-hospital mortality with suspected infection outside ICU
* indications: >18y in non-ICU setting with infection
* Criteria (LOC, resp, BP)
	* GCS < 15
	* RR > 22
	* SBP < 100
* Interpretation:
	* 0-1: low risk
	* 2-3: high risk of in-hospital mortality
		* 3- to 14-fold increase

Management

* aim to treat within 6 hours
* antibiotics (undifferentiated or hospital-acquired)
	* ==piperacillin-tazobactam 4.5g IV q8h==

				   and

* ==vancomycin 15 mg/kg IV q12h==
	* requires vanco troughs
* ==+/- gentamicin 5 mg/kg IV q24h==
	* if hospital-acquired

* fluids (2-3L)
* remove source
	* lines, plastic
	* interventional source control
* vasopressors
* Monitoring for multi-organ dysfunction
	* cardiovascular - hypotension
	* neurologic - AMS
	* renal - ↑ BUN and ↑ creatinine
	* liver - ↑ LFTs
	* lungs - ARDS
