_risk factor_ for **intrauterine growth restriction** is

* **coagulopathy** in the mother, which leads to uteroplacental vessel thrombosis
* **hypoxia**in the mother, which leads to fetal oxygen deficiency
* **diabetes mellitus** in the mother, which leads to uteroplacental vasculopathy

_complication_ of **intrauterine growth restriction** is **meconium** aspiration in the child
