Prolactinoma

* * *

presentation

* women
	* amenorrhea
	* galactorrhea
	* no optic chiasm compression
* men
	* decreased libido
	* bitemporal hemianopsia

workup

* review medications
	* e.g., DA antagonis, anti-psychotics
* TSH
	* low TSH -> high PRL
	* TRH induces PRL production
* PRL levels
* MRI

management

* cabergoline > bromocriptine
* surgery
* levothyroxine (if hypothyroid)

* * *

Acromegaly

* * *

presentation

* pediatric
	* gigantism
	* pre-growth plate closure
* adults
	* growth of hands/feet, face, visceral organs
	* diabetes
	* diastolic heart failure (LVH)

workup

* IGF-1
* glucose suppression test
	* positive test: glucose fails to suppress GH
	* glucose is normally regulated by insulin, however insulin competes with other hormones (EPI, cortical, GH)
		* glucose should result in insulin increase and decrease in counter-regulatory hormones (i.e., GJ)
* MRI

management

* surgery
* octreotide
	* inhibits IGF-1
