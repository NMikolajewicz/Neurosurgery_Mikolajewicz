* Vincristine & Vinblastine
	* side effects: peripheral neuropathy
	* regimens: R-CHOP, ADVD
* Adriomycin (Doxorubicin)
	* side effects: cardiomyopathy
	* regimens: R-CHOP, ADVD, BEACOPP
* Bleomycin
	* side effects: pulmonary fibrosis
	* regimens: ADVD, BEACOPP
* Cisplatin
	* side effects: ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity
* Cyclophosphamide
	* side effects: hemorrhagic cystitis
	* regimens: R-CHOP, BEACOPP
