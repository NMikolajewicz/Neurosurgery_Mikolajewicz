Pathology

* prototypic autoimmune inflammatory collagen vascular disorder
* complex mediated
* broad symtpoms

Risk

* female
* 10-50 y

Workup

* Diagnosis: SOAP BRAIN MD (4 criteria)
	* serositis
		* pleuritis, pericarditis
	* oral ulcers
	* arthritis
	* photosensitivity
	* blood disorders
		* anemia, leukopenia, thrombocytopenia
	* renal
	* ANA+
	* immunologic
		* DS DNA, etc.
	* neurological
	* malar rash
	* discoid rash
* serology
	* ANA - sensitive
	* ds-DNA - lupus nephritis
	* anti-Smith
	* anti-Histone - drug-induced lupus
* ESR/CRP
	* degree of inflammation
* C3/C4
	* monitor disease course
	* if not low, symptoms likely secondary to infection rather than lupus

Complications

* libman-sacks endocarditis (LSE)
	* non-bacterial endocarditis
	* associated with SLE

Management

* NSAID
* hydroxychloroquine (1st line)
* steroids
	* use during acute flare up
* immunosuppressives
	* AZA, MTX, MMF
	* cyclophosphamide - lupus nephritis

Drug-induced lupus (DIL)

* * *

pathophysiology

* caused by anti-histone antibody

Presentation

* rash and pain
	* serositis, arthritis, photosensitivity
* no dangerous visceral involvment

risk

* hydralazine
* procainamide
* methyl-dopa

workup

* anti-histone IgG

management

* stop offending agent

* * *

Lupus nephritis

* * *

pathophysiology

* caused by anti-dsDNA
* life-threatening, requires renal transplant

presentation

* asymptomatic screen shows proteinuria
* HTN, proteinuria, hematuria possible

workup

* biopsy
	* required before treatment

management

* IV cyclophosphamide
* PO mycophenolate
