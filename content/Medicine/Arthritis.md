Differential

* acute: red, hot, swollen, tender, febrile
	* trauma
	* infection
	* crystal
	* reactive
* chronic
	* non-inflammatory
		* OA
			* obese, pain, no inflammation
			* non-inflammatory arthritis
			* failure repair of joint damage
	* inflamattory
		* 1 joint
		* \>1 joint
			* seronegative (PEAR)
				* psoriatic arthritis, enteropathic (IBD) arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, reactive arthritis
			* seropositive
				* RA, SLE, myositis, scleroderma, sjogrens
			* CT disorders

workup

* extra-articular manifestations
	* nodules, rashes, mouth ulcers, eye inflammation, lung findings
* arthrocentesis
	* normal
		* serous <200 WBC, 25% PMN
	* osteoarthritis
		* serous, <2000 WBC, 25% PMN
	* inflammatory
		* cloudy, >2000 WBC, 50% PMN, +/- gram stain and crystals
	* septic
		* purulent, >50000, positive gram stain

Gout

* * *

crystal arthritis

* differential: CPPD (pseudogout)

work up

* arthrocentesis
	* microscopy
		* negative birefringement crystals under polarized light
	* crystals
	* negative culture
	* WBC < 50,000

Management

* acute
	* intra-articular steroids
	* colchicine
	* NSAID
	* systemic steroids
* chronic
	* allopurinol
		* lowers serum UA levels

Rheumatoid Arthiritis

* * *

chronic inflammation resulting in bone destruction
risk: genetics, smoking
presentation

* symmetric polyarthritis
	* small and large joints
		* spares DIP
	* commonly affects hands/feet
* morning stiffness
* extra-articular manifestations
	* felty syndrome
		* RA + neutropenia + splenomegaly
	* vasculitis
	* nodules
	* scleritis
	* ILD
	* CAD risk

Workup

* ==RF==
* ==anti-CCP==
* xray
	* bilateral hands/feet
	* pannus formation causing erosions
		* active inflammatory synovial membrane that is destructive due to inflammatory membrane
	* peri-articular osteopenia

Management

* NSAID
* DMARDs
	* ==methotrexate== - _1st line_
	* sulfasalszine, hydroxychloroquine, leflunamide
	* steroids - _flares_
		* ==prednisone==
	* biologics - _severe disease_
		* TNF inhibitors
			* ==infliximab==
			* rituximab
			* etanercept
		* non-TNF inhibitors

Septic arthritis

* * *

workup

* arthocentesis
	* no crystals
	* positive culture
	* WBC > 50,000
	* gram stain
		* (+) S aureus
		* (-) gonnorhea
	* NAAT

Non-gonorrhea septic joint

* organism: S. aureus
* location: hip, knee
* gram stain: positive
* management
	* nafcillin (MSSA)
	* vancomycin/linezolid (MRSA)

gonorrheal septic joint

* organism: gonorrhea
* gram stain: negative
	* require NAAT
* management
	* ceftriaxone
	* +/- doxycycline/azithromycin for chlamydia infection
	* remove hardware
