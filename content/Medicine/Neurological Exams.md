**BASIC SCREENING:** (**sensitive + objective**)

1. **Mental status:** orientation
2. **Cranial Nerves**
	1. VF, pupils, EOM, facial weakness
3. **Motor:**
	1. **Pronator drift**
	2. Hip flexors
	3. Dorsiflexion
	4. **Reflexes** and plantars
4. **Sensory:**
	1. Light touch
	2. **Vibration**
5. **Coordination**
	1. Finger-nose
	2. Heel shin
6. **Gait**

**Rule out systemic signs:**

* vitals
* meningeal
* Systemic, skin signs
* Fontanelle and head circle for infants
