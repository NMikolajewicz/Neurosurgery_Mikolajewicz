DIMS Workup
**Drugs** – anticholinergics, benzodiazepines, opioids, alcohol, withdrawal from drugs

	medication review
	

**Infections** \- CNS infections (meningitis, encephalitis) and systemic infections

	CBC
	
	blood/urine cultures
	
	urinalysis
	

**Metabolic** – electrolyte disturbances, endocrinopathies, CO2 retention, etc.

	CBC
	
	electrolytes + extended
	
	glucose
	
	venous blood gas (VBG)
	
	TSH
	

**Structural** – Space occupying lesions, stroke, seizure, intracerebral bleeds, urinary retention, constipation

	PVR
	
	CT brain
	
	BM monitoring
	

Non-pharmacological management

	encourage mobilization
	
	reorient patient
	
	cognitive activities
	
	hydration
