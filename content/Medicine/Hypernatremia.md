Cause: free water deficit

* low urine output
	* non-renal losses
		* GI loss: watery diarrhea
		* insensible loss
			* burns, fevers, ICU patients, hyperventilation
	* hypodipsia
		* decreased water intake, e.g., reduced GCS
* high urine output
	* high urine osmolarity (>300 mmol/Kg)
		* hypertonic saline administration
		* osmotic diuresis
	* low urine osmolarity (<300 mmol/Kg)
		* diabetes insipidus
			* low ADH

Severity (mEg/L)

* normal: 135-145
* mild: 145-150
* moderate: 150-170
* severe: 170-180
* extreme: >180

Management

* replenish free water deficit
	* preferred: PO or PEG tube
	* IV fluids otherwise
* severity
	* mild (145-150)
		* PO water intake
	* moderate (150-170)
		* IV NS
	* severe (>170)
		* D5W
