Causes

* hypoparathyroidism
	* iatrogenic from removal of parathyroid gland during thyroid surgery
* vitamin D deficiency
* pancreatitis

Presentation

* tetany
* perioral tingling
* trousseau's sign
	* carpopedal spasm with inflation of blood pressure cuff
* chvostek's sign
	* tapping of facial nerve results in facial spasm

Workup

* electrolytes
* ionized calcium
* albumin
* vitamin D (25 vit D; not 1,25 vit D)
* PTH
* +/- DEXA

Management

* IV calcium gluconate or calcium carbonate
* +/- vitamin D
