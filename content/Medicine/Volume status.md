**[Hypovolemia](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Dehydration_and_hypovolemia#Zeb53713ef6f84bacbbe3e3392a007886)** (fluid deficit)

* [Clinical signs of significant dehydration](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Dehydration_and_hypovolemia#Zcab074498485cc9f54c032767e39fc62) (e.g., dry mucus membranes)
* [Shock](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Shock#Z6023d435aa502ba29fe59ecc0f2f9df5) and other signs of poor peripheral perfusion may be present.
* [Orthostasis](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Syncope#Z0a45f919f21eb0d967bbf76b0a7ac54c)
* [Oliguria](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Diagnostic_evaluation_of_the_kidney_and_urinary_tract#Z923732bc2a869fb5343909709f174a34) (e.g., as a sign of  prerenal AKI)
* ↓ CVP and ↓ [JVP](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Cardiovascular_examination#Z6a75253d8b314a41530698f59f7edd5a)
* [Fluid responsiveness](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Intravenous_fluid_therapy#Z8a6d39458abba96ac5ff843c69fc6ee2)
	

**Euvolemia** (fluid balance)

* No [signs of hypovolemia](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Intravenous_fluid_therapy#Z67f90fc721c4b76620b3107bc207b164) or  hypervolemia

**Hypervolemia** ([fluid overload](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Intravenous_fluid_therapy#Z8691613a1023ed6b2f562d848b9043be))

* ↑ CVP and ↑ JVP
* [Pulmonary edema](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Pulmonary_edema#Z7c92756b9ce2ff6d0c945d6ed0e082c2) or  crackles on pulmonary auscultation
* [Ascites](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Ascites#Z48e76ffce1b364c1ba4dacdd79a56b7b) and/or effusions (e.g., [pleural effusion](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Pleural_effusion#Z4f6cc81490f80454657181033c0f0214))
* [Jugular venous distention](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Congestive_heart_failure#Zfa99fa6ac238739f5e92fd88069c4036)
* [Anasarca](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Edema#Z5b6322038388139545202990553cb5a2) or lower limb edema
* Weight gain
* [Oliguria](https://www.amboss.com/us/knowledge/Diagnostic_evaluation_of_the_kidney_and_urinary_tract#Z923732bc2a869fb5343909709f174a34) or anuria (e.g. in renal or postrenal AKI, or CHF)
