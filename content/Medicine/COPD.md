### Diagnostic Criteria

* Exposure
	* e.g., cigarette smoking > 10 pack years
* Symptoms
	* mMRC dyspnea > 1 (SOB while walking)
	* chronic bronchitis
* CT structural disease
	* emphysema >5%
	* gas trapping >15%
	* airway thickening
* Airflow obstruction
	* non-reversible obstruction (post-bronchodilator spirometry)
	* FEV1/FVC < 0.70 +/- FEV1 < 80% predicted

### Classification

#### COPD severity

* GOLD criteria
* based on FEV1 measurements
* valid in patient with FEV1/FVC < 0.7
* Classification:
	* GOLD 1: mild, FEV1 > 80%
	* GOLD 2: moderate, FEV1 50-80%
	* GOLD 3: severe, FEV1 30-50%
	* GOLD 4: very severe, FEV1 < 30%

#### Modified MRC dyspnea scale

* based on clinical performance
* Classification
	* mMRC Grade 0: breathless with strenuous activity
	* mMRC Grade 1: SOB with hurried walk
		* mild
	* mMRC Grade 2: SOB with normal walk
	* mMRC Grade 3: stop to breath after 100 m walk
	* mMRC Grade 4: too breathless to leave house / breathless with bADLS (e.g., dressing)
### Management

#### Chronic COPD Management
* Puffers
	* Agents
		* ==spiriva (tiotropium): LAMA==
		* ==advair (salmeterol + fluticasone): ICA + LABA==
		* ==ventolin (salbutamol): SABA==
	* Severity
		* Mild (mMRC 1)
			* SABA prn (ventolin)
			* LAMA (spiriva) or LABA
		* Moderate (mMRC > 1)
			* consults: respirology
			* low AECOPD risk
				* LAMA +/- LABA +/- ICS
			* high AECOPD risk
				* LAMA + LABA + ICS
				* oral therapies
* immunizations
	* ==annual flue vaccine==
	* ==peumovax q5y==
* smoke cessation
* +/- long-term oxygen therapy
* +/- pulmonary rehab
#### Acute COPD Exacerbation Management
* Oxygen/ventilation
	* target SpO2 88-92% (if chronic CO2 retention)
* short-acting bronchodilators
	* beta-agonists (SABA)
	* anticholinergics
* prednisone 50 mg PO daily x5d
* +/- antibiotics
	* ==ceftriaxone 1g IV daily x7 days==
	* or
	* amox/clav 875-125 PO daily x7 days
	* or 
	* ==moxifloxacin 400 mg PO or IV daily x7 days== * if allergic
