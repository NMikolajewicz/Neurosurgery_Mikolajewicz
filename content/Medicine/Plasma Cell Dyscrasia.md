Plasma cell dyscrasia: bone marrow disease with monoclonal expansion of plasma cells

Workup

* CRABs presentation
* SPEP
* UPEP
* skeletal survey
* bone marrow biopsy
	* \>10% plasma cells = MM
	* <10% plasma cells = MGUS
	* \>10% lymphocytes = Waldenstrom

* * *

Multiple Myeloma

* Presentation
	* CRAB
		* hypercalcemia
		* renal failure
		* anema
		* bone pain
* Workup
	* protein gap
		* difference between serum protein and ALB
		* secondary to IgG
	* SPEP/UPEP
		* M-spike
	* Bence-Jones protein
		* incomplete IgG that deposit in kidney causing damage
	* Skeletal survey
		* lytic lesions
		* osteopenia
	* Bone marrow biopsy
		* \>10% plasma cells
* Ruling out MM
	* SPEP negative
	* BM biopsy <10% plasma cells
* Management
	* \>70 y or no donor
		* ==chemotherapy==
	* <70 y with donor
		* ==stem cell transplant==

MGUS
_monoclonal gammopathy of unknown significance_

* Presentation
	* asymptomatic
* Workup
	* SPEP (+)
	* UPEP (-)
	* skeletal survey (-)
	* Bone marrow biopsy <10% plasma cells
* Management
	* monitor for MM transformation
	* ==no treatment==

Waldenstrom's
_plasma cell cancer of BM that secretes IgM that form pentamers_

* Presentation
	* hyper viscosity syndrome
	* constitutional symptoms
		* night sweats
		* fevers
		* weight loss
* Workup
	* SPEP (+)
	* UPEP (-)
	* skeletal survey (-)
	* bone marrow biopsy >10% lymphoma
* Management
	* ==rituximab-based chemotherapy==
	* ==plasmapheresis==
		* if hyperviscosity syndrome
