**Hyperthyroidism**

* * *

Hyperthyroid

* low TSH, high T3/T4

Differential diagnosis

* graves disease
* toxic multinodular goiter
* hyperthyroid phase of acute thyroiditis

Workup

* TSH
* T3/T4
* radioactive iodine uptake (I123 RAIU scan)
	* high uptake = graves or TMNG
	* no uptake = acute thyroiditis, exogenous, factious

Management

* propranolol
* PTU/MTZ
* I131 ablation
* thyroidectomy
* if thyroid storm
	* PTU + iodine (Lugol's solution) + propranolol
	* consider glucocorticoids

**Thyroid Storm**

* * *

definition: severe hyperthyroidism causing life-threatening emergency

presentation

* florid Afib
* shock/hypotension
* fever
* altered LOC

management

* cooling blankets / cold IV fluids
* beta blocker (==propranolol==)
	* counter autonomic symptoms
* thionamide (==PTU / methimazole==)
* ==steroids==
	* reduce peripheral conversion of T4
* definitive treatment
	* ==radioactive iodine ablation== \- focal
	* ==surgical removal== - entire thyroid

**Hypothyroidism**

* * *

workup

* TSH
* T3/T4

Differential diagnosis

* hashimoto
* medications (amiodarone)
* thyroiditis (viral, bacterial, post-partum)
* radiation
* iodine

Management

* levothyroxine

**Thyroid Nodule**

* * *

Approach

* ==TSH==
	* low TSH
		* ==RAIU scan==
			* if hot
				* ==medications==
				* ==radioactive iodine ablation==
				* ==surgery==
			* if cold
				* malignancy risk
				* ==U/S==
				* ==biopsy==
	* normal/high TSH
		* no RAIU
		* ==U/S==
			* \> 1 cm - ==FNA==
			* < 1 cm - monitor, repeat U/S in 6-12 months
* FNA
	* cancer - ==surgery==
	* not cancer - ==monitor==
	* unsure - repeat FNA

Cold nodules

* papillary
	* most common
	* lymphatic spread
	* psammoma bodies
	* tx: resection
* follicular
	* spread via blood
	* tx: surgically excise whole thyroid, radioactive iodine ablation
* medullary
	* associated with MENII (pheo, parathyroidism)
* anaplastic
	* 80% annual mortality
* thyroid lymphoma
	* risk: hashimoto
