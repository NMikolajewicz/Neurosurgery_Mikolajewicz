Pathophysiology

* slowly progressive autoimmune disorder
* lymphoplasmacytic infiltration of exocrine glands

Presentation

* dry eyes - ketratoconjunctivitis sicca
* dry mouth - xerostomia
* parotid swelling

Workup

* history of SLE/RA
* dry eyes/mouth
* serology
	* ==ANA==
	* ==RF==
	* ==anti-Ro/Lo==
	* cryoglobulins
	* hypergammaglobulin
* schirmer test
	* test to see if they can make tears
* salivary gland biopsy

Risk

* increased risk of B-cell lymphoma

Management

* good prognosis
* ==lozenges==
* ==lubricants==
	* ==artifical tears, saliva==
