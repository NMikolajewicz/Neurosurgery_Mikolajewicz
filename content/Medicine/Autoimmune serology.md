workup

* CBC
	* cytopenia common in autoimmune disease
* creatinine
	* renal involvement common
* urinalysis and microscopy
	* r/o glomerulonephritis (e.g., SLE)
* serology (see below)
* acute phase reactants
	* produced in liver
	* ESR/CRP
	* can also include ferritin, haptoglobin, fibrinogen
* complement
	* C3/C4
		* low during disease flairs (e.g., SLE)

serology

* ANA - SLE
* RF - RA
* anti-CCP - RA
* anti-dsDNA - SLE; lupus nephritis
* anti-Smith - SLE
* anti-RNP - SLE/MCTD
* anti-histone - SLE; drug induced
* anti-centromere - sclerodema (CREST)
* anti-Scl70 (topoisomerase) - scleroderma
* anti-SM (smooth muscle) - autoimmune hepatitis
* anti-Ro - sjogrens
* anti-La - sjogrens
* anti-Jo - myositis (PM/DM)
* anti-mitochondrial - primary biliary cirrhosis
