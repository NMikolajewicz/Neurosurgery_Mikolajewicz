Vasculitis

* blood vessel inflammation
* results in vessel was destruction
	* thrombosis
	* fibrosis
* Types
	* large vessel vasculitis
		* aorta and branches
	* medium vessel vasculitis
		* muscular arteries
	* ANCA small vessel vasculitis
		* capillaries/venules
	* immune complex small vessel disease

* * *

Large vessel vasculitis

* * *

Giant cell arteritis (GCA)

* affected vessels
	* external carotid artery
	* ophthalmologic artery
	* temporal artery
* presentation
	* \>50 y
	* jaw claudication
	* vision changes
		* amaurosis fugax
	* temporal tendeness
* workup
	* temporal artery biopsy
* management
	* ==steroids==
		* pre-biopsy

Takayasu arteritis (TAK)

* affected vessels
	* aorta
	* subclavian and femoral arteries
* presentation
	* <40 y
	* pulselessness
* diagnosis
	* angiogram
* management
	* ==steroids==

* * *

Medium vessel vasculitis

* * *

Kawasaki disease

* presentation
	* asian child
	* Diagnosis (4/5 criteria): CREAM
		* conjunctivitis (non-exudative)
		* rash (polymorphous non-vesicular)
		* edema/erythema (hands/soles)
		* adenopathy (cervical, unilateral)
		* mucosal involvement
	* +/- coronary aneurysm / MI
* management
	* ==IVIG==
	* ==ASA==

Polyarteritis nodosum

* presentation
	* HBV association
	* gut: mesenteric ischmia
	* renal: renal failure
	* skin: purpura, painful nodules
	* mononeuritis multiplex
* diagnosis
	* aneurysms on angiogram
* managements
	* ==steroids==
	* ==cyclophosphamide==

* * *

ANCA small vessel vasculitis

* * *

Granulomatosis with polyangitis (GPA/Wegner's)

* presentation
	* hemoptysis
	* hematuria
	* nasal findings (sinuses)
	* \*similar to goodpasture disease with hemoptysis and hematuria
* diagnosis
	* C-ANCA
	* tissue biopsy
* management
	* ==steroids==
	* ==cyclophosphamide==

* * *

Immune complex small vessel disease

* * *

cryoglobulinemia

* non-ANCA associated vasculitis with HCV and cryoglobulins
* presentation
	* palpable purpura
	* Hep C
	* decreased complement
* diagnosis
	* cryoglobulins
* management - severity based
	* severe: ==plasmapheresis (PLEX)==
	* ==treat hep C==
	* ==steroids==
	* ==cyclophosphamide==

Henoch-schoenlein purpura (IgA vasculitis)

* presentation
	* palpable purpura
	* GI pain and bleeding
* diagnosis
	* leukocytoclastic vascultiis (IgA) on purpura biopsy
* management
	* ==steroids==
