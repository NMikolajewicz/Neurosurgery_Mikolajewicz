rubella (german measles)  phases 

* **prodrome with post auricular lymphadenopathy & forchheimer sign**
* fine nonconfluent maculopapular **rash that begins at head, primarily behind ears,** spares palms and soles + **polyartrhtiris**

**Complications**

* TORCH infection
* Rubella effects on pregnancy **before 16 weeks, possibly devastating congenital defects, after 16 weeks should be ok**
