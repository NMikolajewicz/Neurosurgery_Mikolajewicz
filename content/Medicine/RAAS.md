Renin

* stimuli
	* low renal perfusion pressure
	* decreased delivery of Na/Cl to distal tubules
	* beta-adrenergic sympathoactivation
* source
	* specialized cells in afferent arteriole of renal glomerulus
	* juxtaglomerular apparatus
		* site of contact between afferent arteriole and distal tubule
* Pathway
	* low BP -> Renin (renal) + angiotensinogen (liver)-> ANG I + ACE (lung, renal) -> ANG II

Angiotensin II (ANG II)

* effect: raises BP
* MOA:
	* vasoconstriction
		* arterial and venous
		* increased peripheral resistance
	* stimulates aldosterone secretion
		* increased renal reabsorption of NaCl and H2O
		* increased blood volume
	* central increase in sympathetic tone
	* peripheral increase in release of norepinephrine
