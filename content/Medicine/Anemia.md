Presentation

* Hb > 130 - no anemia
* Hb > 100 - asymptomatic
* Hb 80-100 - fatigue
* Hb 60-90 - SOBOE, presyncope
* HB 40-60 - MI, CVA, high output HF

Oxygen delivery = CO × Hb × SpO2

Workup

* _History_: sickle cell disease, vegan diet (B12), travel (parasites), infections, bleeding (menses, melena), chronicity, dark urine/jaundice/gallstones (hemolysis), kidney/liver disease, hypersplenism
* CBC
* reticulocytes
* creatinine
* LFTs (ALT, AST)
* _hemolysis:_ LDH, bilirubin (direct, indirect), haptoglobin
* _iron studies_: iron, transferrin/TIBC, ferritin, TSAT (transferrin saturation)
* vitamin B12
* folate (if malnourished)
* blood smear
	* bite/blister cells: G6PD levels
	* microcytosis and target/teardrop cells: hemoglobin analysis or DNA testing (thalassemia)
	* spherocytes/elliptocytes/stomatocytes: hereditary spherocytosis, elliptocytosis, stomatocytosis
* other tests
	* SPEP (monoclonal gammapathy): increased MCV, reduced eGFR, hypercalcemia
	* testosterone (androgen deficiency): normocytic anemia

Common scenarios

* bleeding/hemolysis
	* ↓ HB
	* normocytic
	* ↑ retics
* bone marrow problem
	* normocytic
	* normal/low retics

**Differential Diagnosis**

* * *

Microcytic anemia: TAILS

* thalessemia
	* narrow RDW
	* labs: hemoglobin electrophoresis
		* 4 alpha genes:
			* 1 deleted: asymptomatic
			* 2-3 deleted: anemia
			* 4 deleted: hydrops fetalis
		* 2 beta genes:
			* 1-2 deleted: anemia
	* complications:
		* iron overload (frequency transfusions)
			* treat with deferoxamine, not phlebotomy
* anemia of chronic disease
	* labs: ↓ Fe, ↑ ferritin, ↓ TIBC
	* Management: ==no treatment, EPO if severe.==
* iron deficiency
	* labs: ↓ Fe, ↓ ferritin, ↑ TIBC
	* wide RDW
	* colonoscopy: r/o colon cancer
	* Management: ==iron replacement (325 mg TID)==
* lead
* sideroblastic anemia

Normocytic anemia

* bone marrow problem - normal/low retics
* bleeding - increased retics
* hemolysis - increased retics
	* autoimmune (==DAT positive==)
		* cold: IgM + complements
			* Associated with mono (EBV), mycoplasma
			* Mx: ==avoid cold==
		* warm: IgG (+/- IgA)
			* associated with autoimmune disease, cancer
			* Mx: ==steroids, rituximab, splenectomy==
	* non-autoimmune (==DAT negative==)
		* intrinsic RBC
			* hereditary spherocytosis
				* pathophysiology
					* RBC membrane dysfunction (spectrin, achorin, pallidin)
				* workup: ==spherocytes== on smear, ==osmotic fragility test== to diagnose
				* management: ==splenectomy==
			* G6PD deficiency
				* pathophysiology
					* increased susceptibility to oxidative stressors
					* stressors: dapsone, septra, nitrofurantoin, flava beans
				* labs: ==bite cells and heinz bodies on smear, G6PD levels==
				* management: ==supportive, avoid stressors==
			* sickle cell
				* see [[sickle cell disease]]
			* PNH
				* pathophysiology:
					* deficiency of PIGA gene
					* increased risk of complement fixation due to lack of PIGA
				* workup: ==flow cytometry== (CD55 negative monoclonal expansion)
				* management: ==supportive==
		* extrinsic RBC
			* MAHA
				* TTP/HUS, DIC, pregnant (HELLP), scleroderma, HTN
			* malaria
			* mechanical (valve)

Macrocytic anemia

* high MVC, hypersegmented neutrophils, oval macrocyte
	* folate
		* source: leaky freends
		* storage: 3-6 week reserve
		* risk: "tea and toast" diets, malnourishment due to alcohol
		* Management: folate supplementation
	* B12 deficiency
		* source: animal products
		* storage: 3-10 years
		* risk: vegan, pernicious anemia, crohn's disease, gastric bypass
		* presentation
			* neurological symptoms: degeneration of DCMLS (spinal cord)
				* proprioception, 2-point discrimination, vibration sense
			* floppy feet (loss of proprioception)
		* Management
			* PO supplementation - nutritional deficiency
			* IV supplementation - impaired absorption
* chemotherapy, antimetabolite (MTX, azathioprine)
* thyroid disease
* EtOH
* liver disease
* thyroid disease
* myelodysplasia

MAHA

* * *

* non-immune hemolysis (ie, Coombs-negative hemolysis)
* pathology: intravascular red blood cell fragmentation that produces schistocytes on the peripheral blood smear (_see below for blood smears_)
* etiology:
	* microvascular abnormalities
	* intravascular devices: prosthetic heart values, assist devices

Labs:

* negative DAT
* increased LDH
* increased indirect BILI
* low haptoglobin

MAHA: microangiopathic hemolytic anemia

TTP

* FAT RN
	* fever
	* anemia
	* thrombocytopenia
	* renal
	* neuro
* ADAMTS13
* PTT/INR, D-dimer, fibrinogen normal
* Tx: PLEX

DIC

* SICK
* high d-dimer, high PTT/INR, low fibrinogen
* Tx disorder
