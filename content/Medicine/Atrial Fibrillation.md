**Workup and Management**

* * *

Workup

* History: duration, triggers, severity, prior recordings
* CHADS2:
	* CHF, HTN, >65y, diabetes, stroke
* CBC,
* creatinine
* electrolytes, calcium, magnesium, phosphate
* troponin
* D-dimer
* TSH, T4
	* hyperthyroidism present in 5% of AFib cases
* ECG
* TTE
* CXR
	* r/o other causes of dyspnea
* implantable pacemaker: device interrogation
	* assess rate control

Acute Management:

* ABC, IV, oxygen, monitors
* if unstable, ==electrical cardioversion==
* is stable, rate control:
	* ==metoprolol 2.5 mg IV x1 (max dose 15 mg)==
	* ==diltiazem 10 mg IV x1 (max dose 20 mg)==
	* ==digoxin 0.5 mg IV, then 0.25 mg IV q6h x 2 doses (total 1 mg)==

Chronic Management:

* ABC: i) anticoagulation, ii) better symptom management, iii) cardiovascular risks
* rate control +/- anticoagulation
	* ==metoprolol 25-100 mg PO BID==
	* ==diltiazem 120-480 mg PO daily (controlled release)==
	* _target HR < 110_
* anticoagulation (stroke risk dependent)
	* ==Apixaban 5 mg BID PO (_see AC section below_)==
	* CHADS2
		* CHF
		* HTN
		* \>65y
		* Diabetes
		* Stroke

Urgent vs. elective management

* urgent therapy
	* hemodynamic instability (MI, PE, hypotension)
	* tx: IV rate-control +/- cardioversion
* elective therapy:
	* mild/no symptoms and ventricular rate <120 bpm
	* tx: oral rate-control

* * *

**Anticoagulation**

* * *

Who to anti-coagulate:

* CHAD-VASc > 1 (males) or >2 (females)

CHAD-VASC2

* CHF (+1)
* HTN (+1)
* Age (>65 +1, >75 +2)
* DM (+1)
	* fasting glucose > 7
	* DM medications
* stroke history (+2)
	* stroke, TIA, VTE
* vascular disease (+1)
	* MI, PAD, aortic plaque
* sex (female +1)

Drugs:
==Apixaban 5 mg PO BID==

* 2.5 mg if >80 years, <60 kg, creatinine > 133

Warfarin

* target INR: 2-3

**Rhythm vs. Rate Control**

* * *

Rhythm control:

* aim to maintain sinus rhythm
* strategies:
	* anti-arrhythmic drugs
		* **==amiodarone 100-200 mg PO OD==**, dofetilide, flecainide, propafenone, sotalol
	* electric cardioversion
	* radio-frequency catheter ablation of left atrium
	* surgical ablation

Rate control (preferred)

* aim to maintain ventricular rate within goal range
* strategies:
	* medications to block AV conduction
		* beta-blockers, non-dihydropyridine CCB, digoxin
	* AV nodal ablation + ventricular pacing

Initial therapy:

* beta-blocker
	* preferred if recent MI, HF d/t systolic dysfunction, sympathetic triggers

Goals of therapy

* reduce adverse cardiac remodelling
	* tachycardia-mediated cardiomyopathy
* prevent CV disease/mortality
* alleviate symptoms
	* hemodynamic instability
* reduce thromboembolic risk

**Maze** **Procedure** is a surgery performed to treat **atrial fibrillation**. 

_During the procedure, a number of incisions are made on the left and right atrium to form scar tissue, which does not conduct electricity and disrupts the path of abnormal electrical impulses._
