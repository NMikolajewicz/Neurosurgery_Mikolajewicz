### Diagnostic Criteria

* increase in serum creatinine **> 0.3 mg/dL** over 48 hours
* increase in serum creatinine **>50%** over 48 hours
* urine output **< 0.5 mL/kg/hr** for > 6 hours

### Workup:

* creatinine, eGFR
* pre-renal
	* volume assessment
	* urine electrolytes (UNa < 20)
	* BUN/Cr ratio > 20
	* FeNa < 1%
	* FeUrea < 35%
* renal
	* urinalysis
	* urine microscopy
		* hyaline casts / bland sediment: pre-renal
		* muddy brown casts / hemegranular: ATN
		* WBC casts: AIN
		* fatty casts: nephrotic
		* RBC casts: nephritic/RPGN
* post-renal
	* PVR
	* imaging (renal U/S)? to r/o obstruction
	* foley
* urine volume
* +/- renal biopsy

### Urgent dialysis indications (AEIOU)

* acidosis
* electrolytes
	* e.g., hyperkalemia, hypercalcemia
* intoxication
	* e.g., ASA, ethylene glycol, methanol, lithium
* overload
	* e.g., pulmonary edema
* uremia
	* e.g., pericarditis, encephalopathy

### Management

* prerenal
	* volume deplete - ==IV fluids==
		* e.g., diarrhea, dehydration, hemorrhage
	* volume overload - ==diuresis==
		* intravascular depleted due to third spacing
			* e.g., nephrotic syndrome, cirrhosis, HF
* post-renal
	* relieve obstruction
		* ==foley==
			* if obstruction is distal
		* ==nephrostomy==
			* if obstruction is at level of ureter
		* surgery
* intra-renal
	* disease-specific management
	* ATN - vigorous IV fluids

## **Prerenal**

* * *

### Causes

* ineffective perfusion
	* hypovolemia: hemorrhage, diarrhea, sepsis, unreplenished losses
	* hypervolemic: HFrEF, decompensated liver disease with portal HTN
		* intravascular depletion (i.e., 3rd spacing)
* NSAID, iodinated radiocontrast media
* ACEi, ARB

* * *

## **intra-renal**

* * *

### Causes

* MAHA - TTP/HUS
* scleroderma
* atheroembolic disease
* malignant HTN
* renal infarct - aortic dissection, systemic thromboembolism, renal artery abnormality, acute renal vein thrombosis
* proliferative glomerulonephritis
* nonproliferative glomerulopathy
* ATN - ischemia, nephrotoxic exposure
* AIN - drug-induced, checkpoint inhibitors

### AIN (acute interstitial nephritis)

* workup
	* WBC casts
	* WBC
	* eosionphils
* causes
	* infections
	* drugs
		* septra, penicillins, cephalosporins

### ATN (acute tubular necrosis)

* workup
	* muddy brown casts
		* low sensitivity/specificity
* natural course
	* increased creatinine -> oliguria -> polyuric
		* must give fluids to keep up with polyuria
* causes
	* ischemia
		* secondary to prolonged or severe pre-renal AKI
	* toxin exposure
		* IV contrast
		* myoglobin (rhabdomyolysis)
* management
	* vigorous IV fluids

* * *

## post-renal (obstructive nephropathy)

* * *

### Causes

* ureter
	* cancer
	* stones
* bladder
	* cancer
	* stones
	* neurogenic bladder
* urethra
	* cancer
	* stones
	* BPH
	* kinked foley

### AKI etiology frequencies

* ATN - 45%
* prerenal - 21%
* aoCKD - 13% (ATN, prerenal)
* urinary tract obstruction - 10%
* glomerulonephritis/vasculitis - 4%
* AIN - 2%
* atheroembolic - 1%

### Investigations/finding suggestive of etiology

* volume contraction - prerenal
* drug rash - AIN
* blue toes - cholesterol emboli
* volume overload/HF - cardiorenal syndrome
* ascites/jaundice - liver disease with portal HTN and hepatorenal syndrome
* hematuria without recent UTI - glomerular disease/vasculitis
* sterile pyuria - interstitial nephritis
