**Bicuspid aortic valve**, **VSD**, and  **Turner syndrome** are associated conditions with aortic coarctaion. 

**Chest x-ray** in patients with longstanding **aortic** **coarctation** can show notching of the 3rd-8th ribs due to **erosion by enlarged intercostal arteries**, and a classic "3" sign caused by **indentation** of **aorta** at **the site of coarctation with pre- and post-stenotic dilatation**.

**Balloon angioplasty +/- stent** is the treatment modality of aortic coarctaion.
