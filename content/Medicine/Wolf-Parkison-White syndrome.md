**Wolf-Parkinson-White syndrome**who develop atrial fibrillation with a rapid ventricular rate should be treated with **cardioversion** or **antiarrhythmics like procainamide**.

In WPW, an accessory pathway conducts depolarization directly from the atria to the ventricles without traversing the AV node.

AV nodal blockers like **beta-blockers**, **calcium channel blockers**, **digoxin**, and **adenosine**should be **avoided** in **WPW** syndrome. 

* _because they can cause increased conductance through the accessory pathway._
