Polymyositis

* pathophysiology
	* T-cell mediated
* presentation
	* symmetric **proximal muscle weakness**
	* progresses over months
* workup
	* elevated CK

Dermatomyositis

* pathophysiology
	* complex deposition disease
* presentation
	* similar to polymyositis but with cutaneous manifestations
	* heliotrope rash
		* purple discolouration of eyes
		* pathognomonic
	* gottron's papules
* increased malignancy risk
	* 5-7 fold risk increase
	* adenocarcinomas
		* cervix, lung, ovaries, pancreas, bladder, stomach

* * *

Workup

* biopsy
* CK
* EMG
	* r/o neuropathy
* auto-antibodies
	* anti-Mi
	* anti-Jo

Management

* steroids
