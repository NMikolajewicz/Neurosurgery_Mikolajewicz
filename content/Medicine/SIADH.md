Pathophysiology

* ADH produced by posterior pituitary
* ADH may be inappropriately secreted by brain and lung lesions (SCLC, granuloma, penumonia) resulting in excess water absorption

workup

* urine osmolarity (increased)
	* high ADH
* urine Na (increased)
	* low aldosterone
* serum osmolarity (decreased)

management

* fluid restriction
* treat underlying disease
* demeclocycline
	* if refractory disease
	* MOA: reduces responsiveness of collecting tubules to ADH
	* induces nephrogenic diabetes insipidus
