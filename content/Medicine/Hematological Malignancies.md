Myeloproliferative neoplasms (MPN)
_uncontrolled division of one primary cell line_

* RBC - PRV
	* Jak2, Cal-r mutation
* WBC - CML
	* philadelphia chromosome
* PLT - ET
	* Jak2
* Stromal cells
	* myelofibrosis
	* pancytopenia, tear drop cells

Acute Leukemia

* AML vs ALL
* Presentation
	* B symptoms
	* peripheral blasts
	* \>20% in bone marrow
	* APML: promyelocytic blasts
		* treated with ATRA and arsenic
* Complications:
	* Marrow failure
		* pancytopenia
	* leukostasis
		* tx: hydroxyurea, fluids, leukophoresis, induction chemotherapy
	* tumor lysis syndrome
		* high potassium
		* high uric acid
		* low calcium
		* consult hematology
		* Mx: heme consult, ICU, rasburicase

Multiple Myeloma

* blood film: rouleaux (non-specific)
* diagnosis:
	* monoclonal protein (SPEP, UPEP)
	* \>10% clonal plasma cells
		* or biopsy-proven plasmacytoma
	* Skeletal survey
		* Pathological fractures
	* SLiM CRAB criteria (1 or more)
		* \>sixty (60%) abnormal plasma cells (BM)
		* light chan ratio > 100
		* MRI: bone lesions
		* hypercalcemia (C)
		* renal failure (R)
		* anemia (A)
		* bone disease (B); pain, increased risk of fracture
* suspect in older patient with anemia and renal failure
* management:
	* Chemotherapy (>70 years)
	* Stem cell transplant (<70 years)
	* Hypercalcemia
		* IV fluids
		* bisphosphonate: zoledronic acid, pamidronate
		* calcitonin: if Ca > 3.5 mmol/L
			* avoid lasix unless hypervolemic
