Presentation

* paralysis
* weakness
* ECG findings
	* flattening/invesion of T-waves
	* U waves

Causes

* renal losses
	* hyperaldosteronism
	* conn's syndrome
	* diuretics (thiazide, loops)
* GI losses
	* emesis
	* diarrhea

Management

* PO repletion > IV repletion
	* peripheral IV repletion limited to 10 mEq/hr
		* potassium burns
	* central line repletion limited to 20 mEq/hr
* ==10 mEq of K will increase K by 0.1==
* If K not increasing, check Mg
