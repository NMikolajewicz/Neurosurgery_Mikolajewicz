Pathophysiology

* 6position beta-globin glutamante to valine substituation
	* autosomal recessive
* Sickle cell disease: 1 HbS
* Sickle cell anemia: 2 HbS (instead of beta-globin)
	* Abnormal beta-globin distort RBC into a sickle shape by Hgb polymerization

Premature Hemolysis:

* Anemia, Gallstone, hemolytic anemia

Vaso-occlusive:

* occlude vessels
* mechanism:  RBC deformability, adherence to vascular endothelium, phagocytosis by macrophages
* Presentation: Painful Crises, Chest Crises, Hepatic Crises, ischemic damage, Skin ulcers, Bone infarction, AVN, fat embolism, Priapism, retinopathy, retinal artery occlusion, detachment, Stroke, MI, Autosplenctomy/Hyposplenism: recurrent/serious infection

Workup

* HB electrophoresis
* blood smear
* vaso-occlusive crisis: ABG, lactate

Management:

* Acute
	* Principles:
		* maintain oxygenation + tissue perfusion
		* avoid acidosis, hyperthermia
	* hydration
	* pain management: ASA, NSAID, opioids, heat, tissue mobilization
	* Transfusion
		* simple transfusion: increase HbA content if severe anemia
		* exchange transfusion: HgB level is high
* Chronic
	* hydroxyurea - induces HgbF
	* splenectomy

Complications:

* hemolysis
* dactylitis
	* hydration, pain management, reassurance
* Stroke
	* chronic transfusion
	* maintain HbS above 30%
* priapism
	* urgent urology consult
* pain episode
* chronic pain
	* NSAID, TENS units, relaxation techniques
* pulmonary involvment
	* monitor HB, ABG, O2, hydration
	* pain management
	* ABx
	* exchange transfusion if severe hypoxia
* leg ulcers
	* saline wet-to-dry debridement
	* ABx
	* transfusion
	* skin grafts
