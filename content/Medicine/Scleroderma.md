Limited cutaneous systemic sclerosis (LCSS)

* presentation
	* face and extremities (distal to elbows)
	* CREST syndrome
		* calcinosis
			* calcium deposits in skin
		* raynauds phenomenon
			* BV spasms in response to cold/stress
		* esophageal dysfunction
			* acid reflux, decreased motility
			* unrelenting GERD
		* sclerodactylyl
			* thickening and tightening of skin
			* fingers and hands
		* telangiectasis
			* capillary dilation
			* GI bleeds
			* iron-deficiency anemia
* workup
	* ==anti-centromere==
* associations
	* pulmonary hypertension
* Management
	* maintain warm
	* prostaglandins

Diffuse cutaneous systemic sclerosis (DCSS)

* presentation
	* proximal to forearms and knees
	* LCSS + visceral involvement
		* lungs - interstitial lung disease (pHTN)
		* heart - constrictive pericarditis
		* kidney - scleroderma renal crisis
* workup
	* ==anti-Scl70 (topoisomerase)==
* management
	* target organ-specific

Other management

* CCB (raynauds)
* PPI (esophageal dysmotility)
* penicillamine (sclerodactyly)
* ACEi (renal crisis)
* AVOID STEROIDS
	* can precipitate scleroderma renal crisis
