_And coronary artery disease_

Workup

* ECG
* radionuclide myocardial perfusion imaging (tecnetium sestamibi or thallium)
* TTE
* Stress tests
	* exercise: bruce, modified bruce
	* medications: persantine, dipyridamole, dobutamine

Management of NSTEMI/unstable angina (MONA-BASH)

* morphine
* oxygen
* nitrates
* antiplatelets
	* ==ASA 160 mg PO x1 then 81 mg PO daily==
	* ==clopidogrel 300 mg PO x1 then 75 mg PO daily==
	* ==ticagrelor 180 mg PO x1 then 90 mg PO daily==
* beta-blocker
	* c/i: heart failure
* ACEi/ARB
* statin
* heparin (UGH, LMWH)
	* ==fondaparinux 2.5 mg subcutaneous daily==
	* ==enoxaparin 1 mg/kg subcutaneous q12h==
	* ==unfractionated heparin IV infusion (if renal impairment)==

Chronic CAD management:

* ASA
* ACEi, ARB
* beta-blocker
* statin
* nitroglycerin spray
* risk factor modification
	* smoking cessation
	* exercise
	* control HTN, DM

CABG indications

* left main disease (>50% occlusion)
* multivessel disease with diabetes
* multivessel disease with LV dysfunction/CHF

Other:

* **Reentrant ventricular arrhythmias (eg, ventricular fibrillation)** are the **most common cause of sudden cardiac arrest** in the immediate post-infarction period in patients with acute myocardial infarction.
