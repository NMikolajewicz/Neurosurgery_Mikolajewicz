fever needs to last at least  **5 days** and 4/5 additional criteria need to be present

_CRASH_ 

* _Conjunctivitis, bilateral, nonpurulent_
* _Rash, polymorphous_
* _Asymmetrical cervical lymphadenopathy_ 
* _Strawberry tongue, cracked red lips_
* _Hand feet: swelling, erythema_

Management

* high dose aspirin
	* Risk of Reye syndrome following viral illness, resulting in hepatic failure and hepatic encephalopathy
* IVIG
	* reduces risk of coronary aneurysm
* IV glucocorticoids
	* If treatment-refractory
