Elevated liver enzymes

* hepatocellular
	* elevated AST, ALT >>> ALP, GGT
* cholestatic
	* elevated ALP, GGT >>> AST, ALT

High yield patterns

* AST > ALT (2x) + ↑ GGT
	* alcoholic hepatitis
* AST/ALT > 1000 and ALT>AST
	* viral hepatitis
* AST/ALT > 1000 post-op or hemorrhage
	* ischemic hepatitis (shock liver)
* ↑ direct BILI
	* obstructive (stone/cancer)
	* dubin's johnsons, Rotor
* ↑ indirect BILI
	* hemolysis
	* Gilbert's, Crigler Najjar
* ↑ ALP/GGT
	* bile duct obstruction
	* PSC if IBD
* ↑ ALP + normal GGT/Ca
	* Paget's disease (↑ hat size, hearing loss; Tx: bisphosphonates)
* anti-mitochondrial IgG
	* primary biliary cirrhosis (tx: bile resins)
* ANA + anti-SM IgG
	* autoimmune hepatitis (tx: steroids)
* ↑iron + ↓ferritin + ↓transferrin
	* hemachromatosis (hepatitis, DM, golden skin)
* ↓ceruloplasmin + ↑urinary copper
	* wilson's (hepatitis, psychiatric signs; basal ganglia, keiser fleischer rings)

Transaminitis

* hepatocellular damage
* patterns
	* ==severe elevation (AST/ALT > 1000)==
		* shock liver
		* tylenol overdose
		* viral/autoimmune hepatitis
		* budd-chiari syndrome
			* obstruction of hepatic vein outflow (thrombotic vs. non-thrombotic)
				* thrombotic (most common
					* hypercoagulable state: polycythemia vera, myeloproliferative disoder, OCP, pregnancy, post-partum period, APA syndrome, PNH, clotting abnormalities
				* non-thrombotic (compressive)
					* malignancy (HCC, RCC)
					* hepatic cyst
					* aspergillosis
			* presentation: ascites, jaundice, hepatomegaly
	* ==non-severe elevations (AST/ALT < 1000)==
		* drugs
		* viral/autoimmune hepatitis
		* hemochromatosis
		* NASH
		* wilson's disease
		* alpha-1 anti-trypsin deficiency
	* ==alcoholic hepatitis==

Cholestatic

* typically ALP, GGT, BILI
* bilirubin
	* ==unconjugated==
		* hemolysis
		* Gilbert's
		* Crigler Najjar
	* ==conjugated==
		* obstructive
		* Dubin's Johnsons, Rotor
* Intra vs. extrahepatic
	* intrahepatic
		* medications
		* sepsis
		* PBC
		* post-operative
	* extrahepatic
		* pancreatic/cholangio carcinoma
		* PSC
		* choledocholithiasis
