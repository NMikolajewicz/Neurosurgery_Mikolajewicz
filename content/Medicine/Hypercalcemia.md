Presentation

* kidney stones
* psychic moans - altered mental status
* abdominal growns - abdominal pain
* bone pain

Workup

* electrolytes
	* calcium, phosphate
* PTH
* PTHrp
	* malignancy
* 1,25-OH Vit D
	* malignancy
* 25-OH Vit D
	* granulomas: sarcoid, TB, histo, GPA
	* vitamin D intoxication
* FeCa
	* familial hypocalciuric hypercalcemia
* SPEP/UPEP
	* to r/o multiple myeloma

Differential Diagnosis

* PTH high/normal
	* hyperparathyroidism
		* adenoma, hyperplasia, carcinoma, lithium
		* CDK
	* familiar hypocalciuric hypercalcemia
* PTH low
	* malignancy
		* PTHrP (squamous cell carcinoma, etc.)
		* osteolytic metastases (breast, MM, lymphoma, leukemia)
		* 1,25-OH Vit D (lymphoma, ovarian dysgerminomas)
		* ectopic PTH (ovarian, lung, NE tumor, etc.)
	* vitamin D excess
		* granulomas (sarcoid, TB, histo, GPa)
		* vit. D intoxication
	* increased bone turnover
		* hyperthyroidism, immobilization, vitamin A

Management (FBCL)

* F: ==fluids, fluids, fluids==
* B: ==bisphosphonates==
	* long-term treatment
* C: ==calcitonin==
	* if Ca > 3.5
	* acute treatment - rapid correction
* L: lasix
	* only if fluid overloaded
