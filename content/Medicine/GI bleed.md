management of lower GI bleed

1\. keep pt **NPO** and stabilize

* **if hemodynamically unstable** & LGIB is likely
	* stabilize, then
	* **colonoscopy** if pt stablizes after resusc
	* **angiography** if refractory instability 
* **if hemodynamically stable pt** **determine if high or low risk pt**
	* **if high risk then colonoscopy, otherwise DRE**
