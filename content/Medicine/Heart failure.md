HF investigations

* TTE
* ECG
	* normal ECG has 98% NPV for LV systolic dysfunction
* BNP
* troponin
* CBC
	* risk factors: anemia, infections
* electrolytes
	* hyponatremia ~ severe HG
* blood urine nitrogen
* creatinine
* LFTs
	* possibly affected by hepatic congestion
* glucose
	* screen for diabetes
* CXR
	* cardiomegaly (cardiac:thoracic ratio > 0.5)
	* cephalization of pulmonary vessels
	* kerley B-lines
	* pleural effusions

HF management

* acute treatment (LMNOP)
	* lasix
	* morphine
	* nitrates
	* oxygen
	* position (upright vs. flat)
* **First Line (with survival benefit; HR 0.39):**

		  _"BAMS" beta-block, ARNI, MRA, SGLT2i_

* ARNI/RAAS antagonist
	* ARNI; sacubitril-valsartan
	* use hydralazine if RAAS antagonist cannot be tolerated (e.g., african americans)
* beta-blocker
	* metoprolol, carvedilol, bisoprolol
* MRA
	* eplerenone > spironolactone
* SGLT2i
	* dapagliflozin, empaglliflozin
	* if LVEF < 35% and NYHA class 3-4

* add each drug sequentially, following order: ARNI, beta-block, MRA, SGLT2.
* treatment period is indefinite
* diuretic used to maintain optimal fluid balance, however have no survival benefit.

Triggers of acute exacerbation

* increased demand
	* infection, anemia, thyroid, arrythmia
* infarction
	* STEMI, NSTEMI
* valvular disease
* lifestyle
	* medication compliance, fluids, salts
* new medications
	* steroids, NSAIDS

Mechanisms of HF

* left ventricular dysfunction
	* rEF: ischemic cardiomyopathy, dilated cardiomyopathy
	* pEF: restrictive cardiomyopathy, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, noncompaction cardiomyopathy
* valvular heart disease
	* stenosis, regurgitation
* right ventricular dysfunction
	* pulmonary HTN, right ventricular infarct, right cardiomyopathy
* pericardial disease
	* cardiac tamponade, constrictive pericarditis, effusive-constrictive pericardial disease
* obstructive lesion in heart of great vessel
	* atrial myxoma, pulmonary vein stenosis
* high-output heart failure

ACCF and NYHA classifications
NYHA

* class I: no limitations
* class II: mild symptoms with normal exertion
* class III: activity limited by symptoms
* class IV: symptoms at rest

![[./_resources/Heart_failure.resources/image.2.png]]
Differential Diagnosis:

* dyspnea
	* MI, pulmonary disease, COPD
* fatigue
	* deconditioning, sleep apnea, depression
* fluid retention
	* edema, VTE, renal sodium retention, drugs, cirrhosis
