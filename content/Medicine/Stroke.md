Causes

* 80% ischemic
	* atherosclerosis
	* dissection
	* cardioembolic (e.g., Afib)
	* vasculitis
	* hypercoagulable state
	* PFO
* 20% hemorrhagic

Focal neurological deficits

* ACA - feet and legs
* MCA - hands, arms, face, speech
* PCA - visual cortex, blindness
* basilar artery - locked in syndrome
* vertebrobasilar arteries - diplopia, dizzy, dysarthria
* cerebellar arteries - dysdiadochokinesia, ataxia
	* Ipsilateral findings

Workup

* non-contrast CT
	* rule out hemorrhage
* diffusion-weighted MRI
	* most accurage test
* CBC
* electrolytes (+ extended)
* creatinine
* INR
* troponin
* lipid panel
* HbA1c
* Post-stroke etiology
	* ECG
		* holter to look for AFib
	* TTE
		* r/o thrombus, PFO
	* CTA
		* r/o atherosclerosis, dissection

Management

* ABC
	* do not treat HTN unless BP > 220/120
* Time-dependent
	* < 4.5 hours: TPA
		* TPA contraindictaions
			* BP > 180 SBP
			* stroke < 3 months
			* surgery < 2 w
			* LP < 1 w
			* INR > 1.7
	* \> 4.5 hours
		* start ASA
		* if stroke while on ASA, add dipyridamole or switch to clopidogrel
	* 4.5 - 24 hours
		* indications for endovascular therapy
			* 70-99% occlusion
			* \>60% and <60y
* Nimodipine
	* if SAH
	* reduces risk of ischemic stroke
* Aneurysm
	* clip within days or if <10 mm
