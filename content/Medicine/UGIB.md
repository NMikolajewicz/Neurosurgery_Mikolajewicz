Causes

* ulcers (gastric/duodenal)
* varices (esophageal/gastric)
* esophagitis
* gastritis
* mallory-weiss tear
* angiodysplasia

Management

* 2x large bore IV
	* 14-16 gauge
* pantoprazole IV
* octreotide
	* if varices
* Ceftriaxone IV
	* If cirrhotic and varices suspected
* target Hb > 70
	* serial CBC
		group and screen
		
* OGD
	* consult GI
	* NPO
