Pathophysiology

* problem with ADH
* types
	* central DI: brain production problem
	* nephrogenic DI: ADH resistant
		* demeclocycline
		* lithium toxicity
* kidneys do not reabsorb water, thereby diluting urine

presentation

* polydipsia
* polyuria
* normal BG
* no glucose in urine

workup

* urine osmolarity (decreased)
* water deprivation test
	* psychogenic - urine osmolarity increase
	* ADH problem - no change to urine osmolarity
* ADH administration
	* DDAVP helped - central DI
	* DDAVP did not help - nephrogenic DI

management

* psychogenic polydipsia - stop drinking water
* central DI - ==DDAVPD== intranasally
* nephrogenic DI - gentle diuresis
	* ==hydrochlorothiazide==
