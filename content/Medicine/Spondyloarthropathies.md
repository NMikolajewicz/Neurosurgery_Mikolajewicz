Types of spondyloarthropathies (PAIR)

* psoriatic
	* presentation
		* psoriatic plaques
		* arthritis
		* nail pitting
	* diagnosis: clinical
	* management
		* NSAID
		* local steroids
		* DMADS
		* anti-TNF alpha
* ankylosing spondylitis
	* presentation
		* sacroiliitis (lower back pain)
		* morning stiffness
	* diagnosis
		* lateral xray - bamboo spine
	* management
		* NSAID
		* localized steroids
		* anti-TNF alpha for refractory disease
		* CT to assess for occult fracture
* IBD
	* presentation
		* IBD (UC)
		* arthritis
	* diagnosis: clinical
	* management
		* treat IBD
		* no steroids or NSAID for arthritis
* reactive arthritis
	* presentation
		* secondary to infection (STI)
		* Reiter's syndrome (_can't see, pee ,or climb tree_)
			* urethritis
			* arthritis
			* conjunctivitis
	* diagnosis
		* negative tap
		* find STI infection
	* management
		* treat infection
			* cefriaxone with azithromycine or doxycycline
		* NSAID
		* self-limited disease course

Seronegative arthritis

* HLAB27 associated
* no specific markers (seronegative)
* unique features
	* enthesitis - ligament insertion inflamed
	* dactylitis - whole digit inflamed

workup

* plan x-ray
	* bony erosions
	* joint destruction
* advanced imaging not indicated

general management

* NSAID
* local steroids
* DMARDS
	* cannot help axial skeleton
	* more useful for peripheral disease
* TNF-alpha inhibitors
