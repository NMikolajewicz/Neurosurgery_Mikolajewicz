## Tetrad of symptoms
1. impairment in performing calculations (==acalculia==)
2. discriminating their own fingers (==finger agnosia==)
3. writing by hands (==agraphia==) 
4. impairment of distinguishing left from right (==left-right disorientation==)
## Clinical Examination
- write a sentence (agraphia)
- serial 7's (acalculia)
- naming index/ring fingers, imitate finger configuration (finger agnosia)
- left vs. right on self/examiner (L-R disorientation)
## Anatomy
- dominant (usually left) inferior parietal lobule
	- angular gyrus (brodmann 39)
	- +/- supramarginal gyrus (brodmann 40)
- Vascular supply
	- L MCA inferior-division parietal branches
		- angular/posterior parietal arteries
	- MCA-PCA watershed area
## Causes
- Dominant MCA stroke
- TBI
