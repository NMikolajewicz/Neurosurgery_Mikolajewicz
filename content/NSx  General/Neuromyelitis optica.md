### Background
**Neuromyelitis optica (NMO)** and **neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder (NMOSD)** are closely related severe demyelinating diseases caused by an autoantibody to the aquaporin-4 water channel.
### NMO Triad
* [optic neuritis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/optic-neuritis?lang=us)
* [longitudinally extensive myelitis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/longitudinally-extensive-spinal-cord-lesion?lang=us)
* positive anti-AQP4 antibody
previously referred to as **Devic disease**
### **Risk factors**
* age
* Multiple sclerosis
* Female
* Asian/indian/african
