### 1. vertebral fusion anomalies
### 2. transitional vertebra
* vertebra at junction of major division of spine has characteristics of both divisions
	* e.g., cervical rib
* complication
	* spinal levels wrongly identified preoperatively
### 3. hemivertebra
* incomplete development of lateral half of vertebral body
* presentation
	* kyphosis
	* scoliosis
### 4. butterfly vertebrae
* failure to fuse of two lateral centers of chondrification for vertebral body
* cleft in midsagital plain
### 5. failure of fusion of secondary ossification centers
* commonly confused with fracture
### 6. limbus vertebra
* anterior interposition of intravertebrally herniated nuclear material
* triangle-shaped bony mass
### 7. pedicle anomalies
* absence or hypoplasia
