### Posturing
* Caused by a lesion between the pons and the midbrain.
	* results in blockage of normal stimulation input to the medullary reticular formation from the red nucleus, basal ganglia, and cortex.
	* As a result, there is ==unopposed antigravity muscle tone that is stimulated by the lateral vestibular nucleus and pontine reticular nucleus.==
