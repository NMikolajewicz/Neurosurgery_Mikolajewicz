## Definition

- ==coronal MRI== grading system used to ==assess cavernous sinus invasion== by pituitary macroadenomas
- uses anatomical landmarks—specifically the medial and lateral tangents of the intracavernous internal carotid artery (ICA)—to stratify tumors into grades 0 through 4
## How to grade

Draw three vertical “parasellar” lines tangential to the intracavernous ICA:
- Medial tangent
- intercarotid line
- Lateral tangent  
Judge the most lateral tumor edge vs these lines. (Remember: a tumor can bow the cavernous medial wall without true invasion; carotid encasement is the most reliable MRI sign of invasion.)

## Grades

- grade 0: tumor medial to the medial tangent
- grade 1: tumor between medial tangent and intercarotid line
- grade 2: tumor between intercarotid line and lateral tangent
- grade 3: tumor lateral to lateral tangent
    - 3A: above the intracavernous ICA into the superior cavernous sinus compartment
    - 3B: below the intracavernous ICA into the inferior cavernous sinus compartment
- grade 4: circumferential encasement of intracavernous ICA

## Interpretation

- 0-1: no invasion
	- 0-2% surgical, 0% histological
- 2: possible invasion 
	- 10% surgical, 90% histological
- 3: probable invasion 
	- 25-70% surgical, 90% histological
- 4: definite invasion (100%)
