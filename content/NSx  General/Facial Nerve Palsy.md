### House-Brackmann Scale
* score to grade the degree of [nerve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerve) damage in a [facial nerve palsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facial_nerve_palsy)
* The measurement is determined by:
	* upwards (superior) movement of the mid-portion of the ==top of the eyebrow==
	* outwards (lateral) movement of the ==angle of the mouth==
* Each reference point scores 1 point for each 0.25 cm movement, up to a maximum of 1 cm.
	* The scores are then added together, to give a number out of 8
	* The score predicts recovery in those with [Bell's palsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell%27s_palsy)
