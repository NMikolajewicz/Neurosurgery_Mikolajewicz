## Pathophysiology
* posterior fossa structures formed during ventral induction
* result in anomalies of brainstem and cerebellum
### 1. chiria malformations
- see [[Chiari malformations]]
### 2. dandy-walker malformation
- imperforation of magendie and luschka foramina
- cyst-like dilation of 4th ventricle
* presentation
	* enlarged posterior fossa
	* absent cerebellar vermis
	* obstructive hydrocephalus
- ==Triad: cystic dilation of 4th, enlarged pfossa, vermis hypoplasia==
* management
	* double VP and cystoperitoneal shunt
