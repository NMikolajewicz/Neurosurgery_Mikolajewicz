#Imaging/MRI
Different pulses and different signals provide a variety of sequences.
Unlike CT where we describe "density", MRI images are described by signal intensity ("hyper-" bright, "hypo-" dark).
### Sequences
* T1
	* provides the ==most anatomically-relevant== images
	* fluid (in CSF and orbits) is dark
	* grey matter is darker than the white matter (WM > GM)
* T2
	* standard sequence
	* fluid is bright
	* white matter is darker than grey (GM > WM)
* FLAIR (fluid attenuation inversion recovery)
	* commonly used sequence
	* similar to T2, but the fluid is darker or "suppressed"
	* useful for areas of ==edema or inflammation==
	* used to identify ==plaques in multiple sclerosis== (especially periventricular)
* DWI and ADC (diffusion-weighted imaging and apparent diffusion coefficient)
	* these "blocky" images show how easily water moves around
	* r==estricted diffusion occurs in stroke, abscesses and cellular tumors==
### Diffusion-weighted imaging 
* early identification of [ischemic stroke](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/ischaemic-stroke?lang=us)
* differentiation of acute from chronic stroke
* differentiation of acute stroke from other stroke mimics
* differentiation of [epidermoid cyst](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/intracranial-epidermoid-cyst?lang=us) from an [arachnoid cyst](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/arachnoid-cyst?lang=us)
* differentiation of [abscess](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/abscess?lang=us) from necrotic tumors
* assessment of cortical lesions in [Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease (CJD](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/creutzfeldt-jakob-disease?lang=us))
* differentiation of [herpes encephalitis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/herpes-simplex-encephalitis?lang=us) from diffuse temporal gliomas
* assessment of the extent of [diffuse axonal injury](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/diffuse-axonal-injury-grading-1?lang=us)
* grading of [diffuse gliomas](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/diffuse-glioma?lang=us) and [meningiomas](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/meningioma?lang=us)
* assessment of active [demyelination](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/demyelination?lang=us) 
* grading of prostate lesions (see [PIRADS](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/prostate-imaging-reporting-and-data-system-pi-rads-1?lang=us))
* differentiation between [cholesteatoma](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/cholesteatoma?lang=us) and [otitis media](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/otitis-media?lang=us) 9
