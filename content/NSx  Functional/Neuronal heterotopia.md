### Pathophysiology
* group of neurons fails to migrate fully to cortical destination
* neurons remain in inappropriate location
* cortex generally well-formed grossly, but functionally abnormal (e.g., EEG spikes)
### Types

1. nodular (subependymal/periventricular) heterotopia
	* periventricular
	* multiple nodules in walls of lateral ventricles
2. band heterotopia
	* subcortical stripe of neurons
	* "double cortex"
		* island of subcortical laminar heterotopia
		* separated from malformed cortex by band of white matter
### Presentation
* seizures
* small heterotopias may be asymptomatic
### Workup
* high-resolution MRI (7T MRI)
* genetic counseling
	* mother of child with band heterotopia should undergo MRI, even if asymptomatic
* DTI
	* reveals connectivity of cotex
### Management
* if epileptogenic, surgery
	* isolated heterotopias
		* surgical excision
		* thermoablation
	* diffuse bilateral band heterotopias
		* callosotomy
