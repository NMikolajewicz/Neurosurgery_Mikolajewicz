#seizure #status-epilepticus #lorazepam
### Definition
* \>5 min continuous clinical or EEG seizure activity
		   or
* recurrent seizure without recovery and return to baseline between seizures
### Classifications
* convulsive SE
	* rhythmic jerking of extremities
	* generalized convulsive SE (GCSE)
		* generalized tonic-clonic movements
	* impaired mental status
	* post-ictal focal neurological deficits
* non-convulsive SE
	* seizure seen on EEG without clinical GCSE
	* impaired mental status +/- subtle motor movements
	* semiology highly variable
* refractory SE
	* does not response to treatment (atleast 2 drugs)
	* seizures persist after dosing with benzo + one other epileptic
### Causes
* mass lesion
* meningitis
* metabolic abnormality (e.g., hyponatremia)
* drug intoxication
* alcohol withdrawal
### Management
* emergent initial therapy
	* ==lorazepam 4 mg IV==
		* 0.1 mg/kg IV up to 4 mg per dose
		* rate 2 mg/min
		* repeat if not controlled within 5 mins
	* if no IV access
		* diazepam 10 mg per rectum
		* ==midazolam 10 mg==
			* IM, intranasal, buccal
	* STAT EEG
	* ABC
		* airway patency
		* O2 supplementation
		* +/- intubation
		* vitals - HR, BP, SpO2
		* establish peripheral IV access
		* vasopressor support
			* if SBP < 90 or MAP 70
			* to support CPP
		* neuro exam
	* workup
		* ==blood glucose==
		* CBC
		* metabolic panel
		* serum magnesium, calcium, phosphate
		* LFTs
		* AED levels (if applicable)
		* ABG
		* troponin
		* toxicology screen
		* imaging
		* CSF profile
### Summary
* 2 - 4 mg IV ==Lorazepam== (Ativan) immediately.
* At the same time a 1 g IV ==phenytoin== loading bolus.
* if that doesnt work then a phenobarbital loading dose of 20 mg/kg IV with continuous infusion and Midazolam (Versed) at 0.2 mg/kg at an IV infusion rate of 0.1 to 0.4 mg/kg/h
