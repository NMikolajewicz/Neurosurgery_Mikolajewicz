### Pathophysiology
* milder end of specturm wrt neuronal migration disorders
* disruption in lamination and columnar organization
* ==maloriented neurons in inappropriate layers==
* +/- abnormal neuronal elements
* balloon cells
	* hallmark of FCD
	* not present in all patients
### Types
1. Type 1 - no abnormal cells
	* architectural abnormalities
2. Type 2 - abnormal neurons
	* brain firmer on palpation
	* balloon cells
3. Type 3 - associated pathology present

### Presentation
* childhood seizures
	* +/- delay into adulthood
* medically intractable seizures
	* 50% of cases
	* most common anomaly
	* EEG - focal high amplitude rhythmic activity
* Type 1 FCD
	* clinical silent
	* +/- cognitive impairement
### Workup
* MRI
	* appears normal in 1/3 cases
	* focal abnormal gyral thickening
		* typically at bottom of sulci
	* blurring of cortical-white matter junction
		* reduced demarcation
	* transmantle sign
		* funnel shaped signal across white matter
		* spans from lateral ventricle to cortex harboring FCD

### Management

* surgical extirpation of epileptogenic lesion
	* guided by icEEG
	* early intervention = improved development
	* resection achieves 50-70% seizure resolution
