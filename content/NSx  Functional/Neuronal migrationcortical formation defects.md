## Pathophysiology
* histogenetic period
	* 2-5 months of gestation

## Neuronal migration cortical formation defects
### Lissencephaly
- Lissencephaly = agyria
- pachygyria = macrogyria
	- local lissencephaly
- marked thickened agyric cerebral cortex
* fissures normal

### Neuronal heterotopia
- groups of neurons in inappropriate places
* see also [[Neuronal heterotopia]]
### focal cortical dysplasia
- maloriented neurons
* localize to inapprioriate layers
* see also [[Focal cortical dysplasia]]
### ulegyria
- fusion of layer 1 at depths of sulci
- gyrus in cross section appear like mushrooms
### schizencephaly
- cleft within cerebral hemisphere
	- lined with gray matter
### porencephaly
- cyst lined with white matter
	- communicates with lateral ventrical +/- subarachnoid space
### megalencephaly
- enlarged brain volume
	- white/gray matter volume increased
### hemimegalencephaly
- abnormally large one hemisphere
	- with marked micro/macro disorganization
* management:
	* early hemispherectomy

### colpocephaly
