## Section 2 — Deep dives (PICO · objective · findings · recommendation)

> [!info] Cards are collapsed — click to expand the trial you need. All figures verified against the primary paper.

### 🩸 Aneurysm & SAH

> [!abstract]- **ISAT** — Coil vs clip for ruptured aneurysm _(Molyneux, Lancet 2005; 18-yr 2015)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 2143 ruptured aneurysms suitable for **both** (clip n=1070, coil n=1073) · _I:_ coiling · _C:_ clipping · _O:_ death/dependence (mRS 3–6) at 1 yr.
> - **Findings:** Death/dependence **250/1063 (23.5%) coil vs 326/1055 (30.9%) clip**; ARR 7.4% (95% CI 3.6–11.2, p=0.0001). 18-yr UK cohort: at 10 yr alive 83% vs 79% (OR 1.35), alive+independent OR 1.34 (1.07–1.67); 33 late rebleeds (17 from target aneurysm).
> - **Bottom line:** When suitable for both, **coil**; survival benefit durable, late rebleed real but small. Treat at a dual-capability centre.

> [!abstract]- **ISUIA** — Natural history of unruptured aneurysms _(Wiebers, Lancet 2003)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 4060 patients (1692 observed, 1917 surgery, 451 endovascular) · _O:_ 5-yr rupture by size/location; treatment morbidity.
> - **Findings:** 5-yr rupture (no prior SAH, anterior ICA/AComm/ACA/MCA): **<7 mm 0%, 7–12 mm 2.6%, 13–24 mm 14.5%, ≥25 mm 40%**; posterior/PCom 2.5 / 14.5 / 18.4 / 50%. Treatment risk often exceeded rupture risk for small anterior lesions.
> - **Bottom line:** Risk-stratify by size/location/symptoms (cf. PHASES). Small incidental anterior aneurysms are often watched.

> [!abstract]- **BRAT** — All-comers clip vs coil, saccular subgroup _(Spetzler, J Neurosurg 2017)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 471 enrolled; 362 (77%) saccular (181 each) · _I:_ coil · _C:_ clip · _O:_ poor outcome (mRS >2) over 6 yr.
> - **Findings:** **No significant difference in poor outcome at any time point over 6 years.** Retreatment **0.4% (1/241) clip vs 18% (21/115) coil** (p<0.001); complete obliteration at 6 yr **95% (95/100) clip vs 40% (16/40) coil** (p<0.001); morbidity NS. (64/178 coil-assigned crossed to clipping.)
> - **Bottom line:** Clinical outcomes equivalent; clipping is more **durable**. Modality choice is anatomy-driven.

> [!abstract]- **BRANT (British Aneurysm Nimodipine Trial)** — Nimodipine in SAH _(Pickard, BMJ 1989)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 554 aneurysmal SAH (placebo 276, nimodipine 278) · _I:_ oral nimodipine 60 mg q4h ×21 d · _C:_ placebo · _O:_ cerebral infarction; poor outcome at 3 mo.
> - **Findings:** Cerebral infarction **22% (61/278) nimodipine vs 33% (92/276) placebo** (RRR 34%, 95% CI 13–50). Poor outcome **20% (55/278) vs 33% (91/278)** (RRR 40%).
> - **Bottom line:** **Oral nimodipine is standard** after aneurysmal SAH (neuroprotective; not via large-vessel vasospasm).

### 🫀 Carotid disease

> [!abstract]- **NASCET** — CEA for symptomatic stenosis _(NEJM 1991)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 659 in the 70–99% stratum (medical 331, surgical 328) · _I:_ CEA + medical · _C:_ medical · _O:_ ipsilateral stroke.
> - **Findings:** 2-yr ipsilateral stroke **26% medical vs 9% surgical, ARR 17%±3.5%** (p<0.001); major/fatal ipsilateral stroke 13.1% vs 2.5% (ARR 10.6%). Moderate (50–69%) benefit was smaller (ARR ~6.5%, reported 1998).
> - **Bottom line:** Offer CEA for symptomatic ≥70% (selected 50–69%) if periop stroke/death <6%; best within 2 weeks.

> [!abstract]- **ECST / ACAS / ACST-1** — Carotid stenosis evidence
> 
> - **ECST** _(Lancet 1998):_ confirms CEA for symptomatic severe stenosis (measurement method differs from NASCET).
> - **ACAS** _(JAMA 1995):_ n=1662, asymptomatic ≥60%; 5-yr ipsilateral stroke + periop event **5.1% surgical vs 11.0% medical** (RRR 53%) — contingent on <3% periop morbidity.
> - **ACST-1** _(Lancet 2010):_ n=3120; periop stroke/death 3.0%; net stroke risk **6.9% vs 10.9% at 5 yr** and **13.4% vs 17.9% at 10 yr**; benefit only for age <75 y. (Original 2004 report: ~6.4% vs 11.8% at 5 yr.)
> - **Bottom line:** CEA helps asymptomatic severe stenosis only modestly and only in good-risk, longer-lived patients.

> [!abstract]- **CREST** — Stent vs endarterectomy _(Brott, NEJM 2010)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 2502 symptomatic + asymptomatic · _I:_ carotid stenting · _C:_ CEA · _O:_ composite stroke/MI/death + 4-yr ipsilateral stroke.
> - **Findings:** 4-yr composite **7.2% CAS vs 6.8% CEA** (HR 1.11, 95% CI 0.81–1.51, p=0.51). Periprocedural **stroke 4.1% vs 2.3%** (p=0.01); **MI 1.1% vs 2.3%** (p=0.03). 4-yr stroke/death 6.4% vs 4.7% (HR 1.50, p=0.03). Age interaction: CEA favoured >70 y.
> - **Bottom line:** Choose by age, anatomy, comorbidity, operator expertise.

### 🌐 AVM

> [!abstract]- **ARUBA** — Unruptured AVM management _(Mohr, Lancet 2014)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 223 unruptured AVMs (intervention 114, medical 109; stopped early) · _I:_ intervention (surgery/embolisation/SRS) · _C:_ medical · _O:_ death or symptomatic stroke.
> - **Findings:** Mean 33.3-mo follow-up: primary event **10.1% (11) medical vs 30.7% (35) intervention; HR 0.27 (95% CI 0.14–0.54)**; strokes 45 vs 12 (p<0.0001).
> - **Caveats:** Short follow-up, heterogeneous/incomplete interventions, low microsurgery proportion.
> - **Bottom line:** Default to observation for **unruptured** AVMs; treat selectively. Does **not** apply to ruptured AVMs.

### 💉 Spontaneous ICH

> [!abstract]- **STICH** — Early surgery vs conservative, supratentorial ICH _(Mendelow, Lancet 2005)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 1033 (surgery 503, conservative 530) · _I:_ early surgery (≤72 h) · _C:_ initial conservative · _O:_ favourable extended-GOS at 6 mo.
> - **Findings:** Favourable **122/468 (26%) vs 118/496 (24%)** (OR 0.89, 95% CI 0.66–1.19, p=0.414). Subgroup signal: superficial clots ≤1 cm from cortex.
> - **Bottom line:** No routine early evacuation of deep ICH; consider for superficial/lobar clots or deterioration.

> [!abstract]- **STICH II** — Superficial lobar ICH _(Mendelow, Lancet 2013)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 601 superficial lobar ICH (10–100 mL, **no IVH**), ≤48 h (surgery 307, conservative 294) · _I:_ early surgery ≤12 h · _C:_ conservative · _O:_ favourable extended-GOS at 6 mo.
> - **Findings:** Unfavourable **174/297 (59%) surgery vs 178/286 (62%) conservative** → favourable 41% vs 38% (OR 0.86, 0.62–1.20, p=0.367). Authors note a small, **non-significant** survival advantage.
> - **Bottom line:** Early surgery may offer a marginal survival edge in selected superficial lobar clots; not definitive.

> [!abstract]- **MISTIE III** — Minimally invasive evacuation + tPA _(Hanley, Lancet 2019)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 506 supratentorial ICH ≥30 mL (MISTIE 255, standard 251) · _I:_ catheter + alteplase (aim residual ≤15 mL) · _C:_ medical · _O:_ mRS 0–3 at 365 d.
> - **Findings:** mRS 0–3 **45% vs 41%** (adjusted risk diff 4%, 95% CI −4 to 12, p=0.33). 30-day mortality 9% vs 15% (p=0.07). Secondary: reaching **≤15 mL residual** improved outcomes.
> - **Bottom line:** Efficacy hinges on **adequate clot reduction** — the surgical target matters more than the technique.

> [!abstract]- **ENRICH** — Trans-sulcal parafascicular evacuation _(Pradilla, NEJM 2024)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 300 acute supratentorial ICH (30–80 mL; 69.3% lobar, 30.7% anterior basal ganglia) · _I:_ minimally invasive parafascicular surgery + medical · _C:_ medical · _O:_ utility-weighted mRS at 180 d (adaptive enrichment).
> - **Findings:** uw-mRS **0.458 surgery vs 0.374 control** (diff 0.084, 95% BCI 0.005–0.163; posterior probability of superiority 0.981). **Lobar diff 0.127**; basal-ganglia diff −0.013. 30-day mortality 9.3% vs 18.0%.
> - **Bottom line:** Supports early minimally invasive evacuation for **lobar** ICH; deep ICH benefit unproven.

### ⚡ Acute ischaemic stroke

> [!abstract]- **MR CLEAN** — Thrombectomy ≤6 h _(Berkhemer, NEJM 2015)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 500 anterior LVO ≤6 h (IAT 233, usual 267) · _I:_ thrombectomy + usual care · _C:_ usual care · _O:_ mRS at 90 d.
> - **Findings:** Functional independence (mRS 0–2) **32.6% vs 19.1%** (absolute diff 13.5 pp); adjusted common OR **1.67 (95% CI 1.21–2.30)**. First positive modern thrombectomy RCT; HERMES pooled 5 trials (NNT ~2.6).
> - **Bottom line:** Thrombectomy is standard for anterior LVO within 6 h.

> [!abstract]- **DAWN & DEFUSE 3** — Extended-window thrombectomy _(NEJM 2018)_
> 
> - **DAWN** _(Nogueira):_ n=206; LVO 6–24 h with clinical–core mismatch; mRS 0–2 at 90 d **49% vs 13%** (uw-mRS 5.5 vs 3.4).
> - **DEFUSE 3** _(Albers):_ n=182; LVO 6–16 h with perfusion mismatch; mRS 0–2 **45% vs 17%** (shift OR 2.77); mortality 14% vs 26%.
> - **Bottom line:** Extends thrombectomy to 6–24 h with **imaging selection**.

### 🪖 TBI

> [!abstract]- **DECRA** — Early decompressive craniectomy, diffuse TBI _(Cooper, NEJM 2011)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 155 diffuse TBI with ICP >20 mmHg for >15 min refractory to first-tier · _I:_ early bifrontotemporoparietal craniectomy · _C:_ standard care · _O:_ extended GOS at 6 mo.
> - **Findings:** Craniectomy lowered ICP/ICU stay but **worse** outcomes: unfavourable-outcome **OR 2.21 (95% CI 1.14–4.26, p=0.02)**; worse GOS-E (OR 1.84, 1.05–3.24). 6-mo death 19% vs 18%. (Baseline pupil imbalance; relatively low ICP threshold.)
> - **Bottom line:** **Early** craniectomy for moderately raised ICP is not beneficial and may harm.

> [!abstract]- **RESCUEicp** — Last-tier craniectomy, refractory ICP _(Hutchinson, NEJM 2016)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 408 TBI with ICP >25 mmHg refractory to tiered therapy · _I:_ decompressive craniectomy · _C:_ continued medical (incl. barbiturates) · _O:_ extended GOS at 6 mo.
> - **Findings:** 6-mo death **26.9% (surgical) vs 48.9% (medical)** but vegetative **8.5% vs 2.1%** and more severe disability; 12-mo death 30.4% vs 52.0%. Adverse events 16.3% vs 9.2%.
> - **Bottom line:** As a **last resort**, craniectomy trades mortality for survival with disability — explicit goals-of-care conversation.

> [!abstract]- **BEST:TRIP** — ICP-monitor-guided management _(Chesnut, NEJM 2012)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 324 severe TBI (Bolivia/Ecuador) · _I:_ protocol guided by ICP monitoring (treat >20) · _C:_ protocol guided by imaging + clinical exam · _O:_ 6-mo composite of survival/function/neuropsych.
> - **Findings:** Composite percentile **56 vs 53** (p=0.49); 6-mo mortality 39% vs 41% (p=0.60). Brain-specific treatment days higher in imaging arm (4.8 vs 3.4, p=0.002).
> - **Bottom line:** A **monitor-guided protocol is not superior to a structured clinical/imaging protocol** — _not_ evidence that ICP monitoring per se is valueless.

> [!abstract]- **CRASH-1 (MRC CRASH)** — Corticosteroids in TBI _(Roberts, Lancet 2004)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 10,008 head injury, GCS ≤14 within 8 h · _I:_ 48-h methylprednisolone infusion · _C:_ placebo · _O:_ death at 2 weeks.
> - **Findings:** 2-wk death **21.1% (1052) vs 17.9% (893); RR 1.18 (95% CI 1.09–1.27), p=0.0001** — increased mortality, sustained at 6 mo.
> - **Bottom line:** **Corticosteroids are contraindicated** in TBI.

> [!abstract]- **CRASH-3** — Tranexamic acid in TBI _(CRASH-3, Lancet 2019)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 12,737 TBI within 3 h (TXA 6406, placebo 6331; 72.2% treated ≤3 h) · _I:_ TXA · _C:_ placebo · _O:_ head-injury-related death.
> - **Findings:** Among ≤3 h: head-injury death **18.5% vs 19.8% (RR 0.94, 0.86–1.02)**; **mild–moderate (GCS 9–15) RR 0.78 (0.64–0.95)**; severe RR 0.99 (heterogeneity p=0.030); earlier = better.
> - **Bottom line:** Give early TXA in mild–moderate TBI with intracranial bleeding.

> [!abstract]- **RESCUE-ASDH** — Craniotomy vs craniectomy for acute SDH _(Hutchinson, NEJM 2023)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 450 acute SDH requiring evacuation (craniotomy 228, craniectomy 222) · _I:_ decompressive craniectomy · _C:_ craniotomy (flap replaced) · _O:_ GOSE at 12 mo.
> - **Findings:** GOSE common **OR 0.85 (95% CI 0.60–1.18, p=0.32)**; 12-mo death 30.2% vs 32.2%. Additional cranial surgery ≤2 wk **14.6% (craniotomy) vs 6.9% (craniectomy)**; wound complications **3.9% vs 12.2%**.
> - **Bottom line:** Either approach reasonable; flap-replacement craniotomy avoids a later cranioplasty in many.

### 🦴 Spinal cord injury

> [!abstract]- **NASCIS II / III** — Methylprednisolone in acute SCI _(Bracken, NEJM 1990 / JAMA 1997)_
> 
> - **NASCIS II:** MP 162 / naloxone 154 / placebo 171. Only a **post-hoc** subgroup (MP within 8 h) improved (motor change 16.0 vs 11.2, p=0.03; pinprick/touch likewise).
> - **NASCIS III:** n=499; 24-h vs 48-h MP vs tirilazad. 48-h better **only if started 3–8 h** post-injury, at the cost of more severe sepsis/pneumonia.
> - **Bottom line:** **Not routinely recommended.** AOSpine (2017): 24-h high-dose MP within 8 h may be _offered_ as an option, not a standard.

> [!abstract]- **STASCIS** — Timing of decompression in cervical SCI _(Fehlings, PLoS One 2012)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 313 cervical SCI (early 182 @ mean 14.2 h; late 131 @ mean 48.3 h) · _I:_ early (<24 h) · _C:_ late (≥24 h) · _O:_ AIS grade at 6 mo. _(Prospective cohort.)_
> - **Findings:** ≥2-grade AIS improvement **19.8% vs 8.8%** (OR 2.57, 1.11–5.97; **adjusted OR 2.83, 1.10–7.28**).
> - **Bottom line:** **Decompress early (<24 h)** where feasible.

### 🩻 Degenerative spine

> [!abstract]- **SPORT** — Surgery vs non-op, three lumbar pathologies _(Weinstein, JAMA 2006 / NEJM 2007–08)_
> 
> - **Disc herniation** _(JAMA 2006):_ n=501; ITT non-significant due to crossover (50% of surgery arm operated ≤3 mo; 30% of non-op arm crossed to surgery); both improved; as-treated favours surgery.
> - **Spondylolisthesis** _(NEJM 2007):_ randomized 304 + observational 303; ~40% crossover each way; as-treated 2-yr effects: bodily pain **+18.1**, physical function **+18.3**, ODI **−16.7**.
> - **Stenosis** _(NEJM 2008):_ same direction, favouring surgery.
> - **Bottom line:** Surgery offers faster, durable relief in selected patients; non-op remains reasonable for milder disease.

> [!abstract]- **AOSpine CSM (Fehlings et al.)** — Surgery for cervical spondylotic myelopathy _(2013, prospective multicentre)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ moderate–severe CSM · _I:_ surgical decompression · _O:_ mJOA, Nurick, QoL at 1–2 yr. _(Prospective cohort, not RCT.)_
> - **Findings:** Significant, sustained improvement across functional and QoL measures.
> - **Bottom line:** Offer surgery for moderate–severe CSM; counsel re: recovery in mild disease.

### 🔬 Neuro-oncology

> [!abstract]- **Stupp / EORTC 26981–22981 (NCIC CE.3)** — RT + temozolomide for GBM _(Stupp, NEJM 2005)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 573 newly diagnosed GBM (84% debulked) · _I:_ RT 60 Gy + concomitant/adjuvant temozolomide · _C:_ RT alone · _O:_ overall survival.
> - **Findings:** Median OS **14.6 vs 12.1 mo** (HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.52–0.75, p<0.001); **2-yr survival 26.5% vs 10.4%**.
> - **Bottom line:** **Maximal safe resection → RT + temozolomide** is the standard GBM backbone.

> [!abstract]- **MGMT / Hegi** — Predictive biomarker _(Hegi, NEJM 2005)_
> 
> - **Findings:** MGMT promoter methylated in **45% of 206** cases. Methylated + TMZ: median OS **21.7 mo (17.4–30.4) vs 15.3 mo (13.0–20.9)** with RT alone (p=0.007). MGMT methylation independently prognostic (HR 0.45, 0.32–0.61).
> - **Bottom line:** Test MGMT to inform temozolomide benefit and trial eligibility.

> [!abstract]- **EORTC 22845** — Timing of RT in low-grade glioma _(van den Bent, Lancet 2005)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 314 LGG (157 each) · _I:_ early post-op RT · _C:_ RT deferred to progression · _O:_ PFS / OS.
> - **Findings:** Median PFS **5.3 vs 3.4 yr** (HR 0.59, 0.45–0.77, p<0.0001); median OS **7.4 vs 7.2 yr** (HR 0.97, NS). 65% of control received RT at progression.
> - **Bottom line:** RT timing can be individualised; deferral does not cost overall survival.

> [!abstract]- **RTOG 9802** — RT ± PCV in high-risk LGG _(Buckner, NEJM 2016)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 251 high-risk LGG (age ≥40 _or_ subtotal resection) · _I:_ RT + PCV · _C:_ RT alone · _O:_ OS.
> - **Findings:** Median OS **13.3 vs 7.8 yr** (HR 0.59, p=0.003); 10-yr PFS **51% vs 21%**; 10-yr OS **60% vs 40%**. Oligodendroglioma histology favourable.
> - **Bottom line:** Add **PCV after RT** for high-risk low-grade glioma.

> [!abstract]- **EORTC 26951** — PCV in anaplastic oligodendroglioma _(van den Bent, JCO 2013)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 368 anaplastic oligodendroglial tumours · _I:_ RT 59.4 Gy + PCV · _C:_ RT alone · _O:_ OS.
> - **Findings:** Median OS **42.3 vs 30.6 mo** (HR 0.75, 0.60–0.95). In **1p/19q-codeleted** (n=80): OS not reached vs 112 mo (HR 0.56, 0.31–1.03). (RTOG 9402 concurred — benefit confined to codeletion.)
> - **Bottom line:** Codeletion status is decisive for chemoradiation in anaplastic oligodendroglioma.

> [!abstract]- **5-ALA (Stummer)** — Fluorescence-guided glioma resection _(Stummer, Lancet Oncol 2006)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 322 suspected malignant glioma (analysis population 270) · _I:_ 5-ALA fluorescence-guided resection · _C:_ white-light resection · _O:_ complete resection of enhancing tumour; 6-mo PFS.
> - **Findings:** Complete resection **65% (90/139) vs 36% (47/131)**, diff 29% (p<0.0001); 6-mo PFS **41.0% vs 21.1%** (p=0.0003). OS not significantly different.
> - **Bottom line:** 5-ALA improves **extent of resection** in high-grade glioma.

> [!abstract]- **EF-14 — TTFields** — Tumour-treating fields in GBM _(Stupp, JAMA 2017)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 695 GBM after chemoradiation (TTFields+TMZ 466, TMZ 229; 2:1) · _I:_ maintenance TMZ + TTFields · _C:_ TMZ alone · _O:_ PFS / OS.
> - **Findings:** Median OS **20.9 vs 16.0 mo** (HR 0.63, 0.53–0.76, p<0.001); median PFS 6.7 vs 4.0 mo. Requires ≥18 h/day device use; scalp toxicity in ~52%.
> - **Bottom line:** Evidence-based add-on; adoption varies by access and tolerance.

> [!abstract]- **Patchell I & II** — Brain metastasis _(Patchell, NEJM 1990 / JAMA 1998)_
> 
> - **Patchell I** _(NEJM 1990):_ n=48 single met (surgery 25, radiation 23). Resection+WBRT vs biopsy+WBRT: median survival **40 vs 15 wk** (p<0.01); recurrence at site 20% (5/25) vs 52% (12/23); functional independence 38 vs 8 wk.
> - **Patchell II** _(JAMA 1998):_ n=95 after gross-total resection. WBRT vs observation: brain recurrence **18% (9/49) vs 70% (32/46)** (p<0.001); neuro death 14% (6/43) vs 44% (17/39) (p=0.003); **no OS difference**.
> - **Bottom line:** Resect accessible single mets; adjuvant brain RT cuts recurrence (now usually **cavity SRS** — see N107C).

> [!abstract]- **N107C / Brown** — Post-op cavity SRS vs WBRT _(Brown, Lancet Oncol 2017)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 194 resected brain metastasis (SRS 98, WBRT 96) · _I:_ cavity SRS · _C:_ WBRT · _O:_ cognitive-deterioration-free survival; OS; local control.
> - **Findings:** Cognitive-deterioration-free survival **3.7 vs 3.0 mo (HR 0.47, 0.35–0.63, p<0.0001)**; median OS **12.2 vs 11.6 mo** (HR 1.07, p=0.70). Surgical-bed control favoured WBRT (≈80.6% vs 60.5% at 12 mo).
> - **Bottom line:** **Cavity SRS preferred** to preserve cognition, accepting closer surveillance for local control.

### 🧩 Functional & epilepsy

> [!abstract]- **Wiebe** — Surgery vs medical therapy for temporal lobe epilepsy _(Wiebe, NEJM 2001)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 80 drug-resistant TLE (surgery 40, medical 40) · _I:_ anterior temporal lobectomy · _C:_ continued medical therapy · _O:_ freedom from awareness-impairing seizures at 1 yr.
> - **Findings:** **58% (surgery) vs 8% (medical)** seizure-free (p<0.001); better QoL; 10% surgical adverse effects; 1 medical death.
> - **Bottom line:** Refer drug-resistant TLE for **surgical evaluation** — surgery is highly effective.

> [!abstract]- **ERSET / Engel** — Early surgery in mesial TLE _(Engel, JAMA 2012)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 38 mesial TLE, ≤2 yr drug-resistant (medical 23, surgical 15; stopped for slow accrual) · _I:_ early ATL · _C:_ continued medication · _O:_ 2-yr seizure freedom.
> - **Findings:** Seizure-free in year 2: **0/23 medical vs 11/15 surgical** (p<0.001); as-treated QoL +9.9 points (p=0.01).
> - **Bottom line:** Don't delay surgical referral once drug resistance is established.

> [!abstract]- **Deuschl** — STN-DBS in advanced Parkinson's _(Deuschl, NEJM 2006)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 156 PD with motor complications (randomized pairs) · _I:_ STN-DBS + meds · _C:_ meds alone · _O:_ PDQ-39 QoL; UPDRS-III.
> - **Findings:** 6-mo PDQ-39 improved **9.5 points** (50/78 pairs, p=0.02); off-med UPDRS-III improved **19.6 points** (55/78, p<0.001). Serious AEs 13% vs 4%.
> - **Bottom line:** DBS benefits selected advanced PD with motor fluctuations/dyskinesia.

> [!abstract]- **EARLYSTIM** — DBS earlier in disease _(Schuepbach, NEJM 2013)_
> 
> - **PICO** — _P:_ 251 younger PD (mean 52 y, disease 7.5 yr) with _early_ motor complications · _I:_ DBS + meds · _C:_ meds · _O:_ PDQ-39 QoL at 2 yr.
> - **Findings:** PDQ-39 **improved 7.8 points (neurostim) vs worsened 0.2 (medical)**; between-group difference **8.0 points (p=0.002)**. Superior on motor disability, ADL, and motor complications.
> - **Bottom line:** Consider DBS earlier in well-selected patients, not only end-stage disease.
