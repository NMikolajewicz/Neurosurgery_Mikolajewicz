## Definition
 inflammation of leptomeninges and CSF due to infection

| **Type**                                | **Common Pathogens**                                                       | **Notes**                                |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Community-acquired (Adults)**         | _Streptococcus pneumoniae_ (50%), _Neisseria meningitidis_ (25%)           | Think post-otitis/sinusitis or asplenia  |
| **Post-neurosurgical / Post-traumatic** | _Staph aureus_, _Staph epidermidis_, _Gram-negatives_ (esp. _Pseudomonas_) | EVD/shunt-associated                     |
| **Neonates (<1 mo)**                    | ==_GBS_, _E. coli_, *Listeria*== monocytogenes_                            |                                          |
| **Immunocompromised**                   | _Listeria_, _Gram-negatives_, fungi (_Cryptococcus_)                       | Add ampicillin for _Listeria_            |
| **Subacute / Chronic**                  | _TB_, _Cryptococcus_, _Coccidioides_, _Histoplasma_                        | Basilar meningitis, cranial neuropathies |

## Pathophysiology

- Organism entry → leptomeningeal inflammation → BBB disruption → cerebral edema, ↑ICP, ↓CBF.
- May lead to **hydrocephalus**, **vasculitis**, **SIADH**, **seizures**.
### Clinical presentation
- **Classic triad:** Fever + neck stiffness + altered mental status (present in ~45%).
- Headache, photophobia, vomiting, seizures, focal deficits possible.
- **Kernig’s** / **Brudzinski’s** signs unreliable in older adults.
## CSF findings

| Feature          | **Bacterial**             | **Viral (aseptic)** | **Fungal/TB**      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------ |
| Opening pressure | ↑↑                        | Normal–mild ↑       | ↑↑                 |
| WBC              | ==1000–5000 (PMNs)==      | 10–500 (lymphs)     | 100–500 (lymphs)   |
| Glucose          | ↓ (<40 or CSF:serum <0.4) | ==Normal==          | ↓↓                 |
| Protein          | ==↑↑ (>100)==             | Mild ↑              | ↑↑                 |
| Gram stain       | + in 60–90%               | −                   | May show organisms |
| Culture          | + in 70–85%               | −                   | + (fungus/TB)      |
## Management

- **DO NOT delay empiric antibiotics for LP if septic or obtunded.**
- Obtain **blood cultures ×2**.
- **CT before LP** if focal deficits, papilledema, seizures, or immunocompromise.
#### Empiric antibiotics

| **Age/Setting**             | **Empiric Regimen**                                   | **Add-ons**                   |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 18–50 yrs                   | Ceftriaxone + Vancomycin                              | Dexamethasone                 |
| >50 yrs / immunocompromised | Ceftriaxone + Vancomycin + **Ampicillin**             | Dexamethasone                 |
| Post-op / EVD / trauma      | **Vancomycin + Ceftazidime or Cefepime or Meropenem** | Rifampin if _Staph_ suspected |
| Penicillin allergy          | Moxifloxacin ± TMP-SMX (for _Listeria_)               |                               |
#### **Duration**
- _N. meningitidis_: 7 d
- _S. pneumoniae_: 10–14 d
- _Listeria_: ≥21 d
- _Gram-negatives_: 21 d
- _S. aureus_: 14–21 d
### Adjuvant therapy
- **Dexamethasone 10 mg IV q6h × 4 days** → before or with first antibiotic dose (improves mortality in pneumococcal meningitis).
- Manage ICP: elevate HOB, maintain CPP, consider mannitol if refractory.
- **Seizure prophylaxis:** only if cortical involvement or EVD.
- **Hydrocephalus:** consider **EVD**; beware of infection risk.
## Prognosis
- Mortality (untreated bacterial): >90%.
- Treated:
    - _N. meningitidis_: 5–15%
    - _S. pneumoniae_: 20–30%
- Poor prognostic factors: age >60, altered consciousness, _S. pneumoniae_, delayed antibiotics, hypotension.
## Pearls
- “Empiric therapy waits for no LP.”
- “If in doubt—cover ==_Listeria_==.”
- “==Post-craniotomy== meningitis is **==staphylococcal== until proven otherwise**.”
- “CSF pleocytosis with normal glucose post-EVD? Think _chemical_ rather than bacterial.”
