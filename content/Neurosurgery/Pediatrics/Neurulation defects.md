## Risk factors 
* maternal
	* ==hyperthermia==
	* ==diabetes==
* anticonvulsants
	* ==valproic acid==
	* ==carbamazepine==
* prior fetus with neural tube defect
	* increases risk 10-fold
## Folate supplementation
* ==folate 4 mg/day== before conception and until GA 12 weeks
* all women of childbearing age
	* ==0.4 mg folate daily==
## Prenatal workup
* ==amniocentesis - AFP, AChE==
* ==U/S==
* ==MRI==
* genetic counseling
	* parents with children with major neurologic abnormality
	* risk of subsequent child having defect is high

## Neurulation defects
### 1. Craniorachischisis totalis
* ==total failure of neural tube closure==
* brain/spinal cord necrosis due to amniotic fluid exposure
* iniencephaly
	* occipital bone defect
	* cervical dysraphism (incomplete fusion)
	* head retroflexion
### 2. Craniochisis, s.cranium bifidum
* ==defect of cranial neuropore closure==
* anencephaly
	* complete absence of brain and calvarium
	* no epiderm cover
* exencephaly
	* defect in calvarium (dura, bone, skin)
* encephaloceles, cranial meningoceles
### 3. Spina bifida
* commonly in lumbar region
* spinal bifida occulta
	* defect in vertebral arch
		* skin intact
		* cutaneous signs
			* ==tuft of hair==
			* cutaneous angioma/lipoma
			* sinus tract
* spina bifida manifesta
	* amniotic fluid biomarkers
		* ==increased AFP==
		* ==increased AChE==
	* variants
		* meningoceles
		* ==myelomeningoceles==
			* see also [[Neurosurgery/Spine/Myelomeningocele]]
			* associated with Chiari II (see [[Neurosurgery/Pediatrics/Chiari malformations]])
		* myeloceles
		* rachischisis (myeloschisis + spina bifida)
