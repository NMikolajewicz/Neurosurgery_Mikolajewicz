## Definition
**Leptomeningeal cysts**, also known as **growing skull fractures**, are an enlarging skull fracture that occurs near post-traumatic [encephalomalacia](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/encephalomalacia?lang=us). The term cyst is actually a misnomer, as it is not a cyst, but an ==extension of the encephalomalacia==. Hence, it is ==usually seen a few months post-trauma==.
## Epidemiology
* 0.05%–1% of skull fractures in childhood.
* The majority occur in children <3 years
## Presentation
* enlarging scalp mass
* [seizures](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/investigating-seizures-summary?lang=us)
* focal neurological deficit
* [headache](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/investigating-severe-headache-summary?lang=us)
## Pathogenesis
* ==Pulsations from CSF erode the fracture margin==, resulting in eventual expansion and non-union
* Additional imaging findings
	* [extracranial brain herniation](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/extracranial-brain-herniation?lang=us)
	* [hydrocephalus](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/hydrocephalus?lang=us)
	* unilateral ventricular dilatation
	* [porencephalic cyst](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/porencephaly?lang=us) 
## Complications
* The brain extrusion may present shortly after diastatic linear fracture in neonates and young infants
	* results in ==focal dilation of the lateral ventricle== near the growing fracture.
	* This dilatation is reversible and may normalize after surgical repair
* risk of neurological deterioration and development of seizure disorders
* craniotomies performed without watertight closure of dural lacerations
	* lead to growing skull fractures.
	* supports dural tear as being the major risk factor in the development of a growing skull fracture.
## Management
* craniectomy and repair.
## Rahman classification
* Type I: GSF with leptomeningeal cyst
* Type II: GSF with underlying gliotic brain
* Type III: GSF with porencephalic cyst
	* Porencephaly is a cyst or cavity filled with cerebrospinal fluid to develop in the brain.
