> [!summary] Dorsal dermal sinus (peds)  
> **Congenital epithelial-lined tract** from **midline skin → spinal canal** due to **failure of dysjunction**.  
> **Clue:** **midline pit/opening above the gluteal crease** (≠ simple sacral dimple), ± drainage/infection.  
> **Major risks:** **meningitis/abscess** + **inclusion tumors** (**dermoid/epidermoid**) along the tract.  
> **Best workup:** **MRI spine** to map tract, check **intradural extension** and **tethering**.  
> **Termination:** ~**1/3** to **lamina**, ~**1/3** to **dura**, ~**1/3** **intradural** (may tether cord).  
> **Tx:** **urgent complete excision**—**follow tract to the bottom**; address tethering/tumor if present.

## Pathophysiology

- **Punctate failure of dysjunction** of skin from neural tube
- Creates a **thin epithelial communication** between **dorsal skin** and **spinal canal**
- May terminate:
    - **~1/3 at lamina**
    - **~1/3 at dura**
    - **~1/3 intradural**
        - can contribute to **tethered cord**

## Presentation

- **Small midline skin opening/pit** (often **above the intergluteal crease**)
- ± **drainage or infection**
- Targeted supplementation:
    - Concerning features: **midline pit above gluteal crease**, recurrent cellulitis, **discharge**, meningitis history, associated cutaneous markers

> [!warning] Exam / clinical pearls  
> A **midline pit above the gluteal cleft** is **not a "simple sacral dimple"** → think **dermal sinus** and risk of **meningitis/abscess** + **dermoid/epidermoid** along the tract.

## Workup

- **MRI spine (best test)**
    - Evaluate full course of tract
    - **Look for dermoid/epidermoid tumors** along the dermal sinus tract
    - Assess for **intradural extension** and **tethering**

## Management

- **Surgical excision of entire tract**
    - Treat as **urgent** to prevent infection and intradural complications
    - Operative principle: **follow sinus to the bottom** and remove completely
    - Be prepared for:
        - termination at **lamina / dura / intradural**
        - **tethering** if intradural involvement

> [!tip] Operative checklist (quick)  
> **Expose and trace the tract**, remove en bloc when feasible, inspect intradural space if indicated, address **tethering** and resect associated **dermoid/epidermoid** if present.
