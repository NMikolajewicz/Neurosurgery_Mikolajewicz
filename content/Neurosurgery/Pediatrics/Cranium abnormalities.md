## 1. Microcephaly
* microencephaly, synostosis
* most causes untreatable
* craniosynostosis only treatable cause
## 2. Macrocephaly
* etiology
	* [[Neurosurgery/Pediatrics/Benign enlargement of the subarachnoid space in infancy (BESSI)]]
		* benign enlargement of subarachnoidal space
		* no signs of raised ICP
		* normal clinical development
		* presentation
			* rapid head growth initially, followed by normal rate
			* imaging - wider ventricular system and subarachnoid space
				* brain otherwise normal
		* history
			* positive history in 1-2 parents
		* prognosis
			* size eventually reduces and becomes normal
	* increased ICP
## 3. Craniosynostosis
* premature fusion of >1 cranial sutures
	* 6 cranial sutures total
* presentation
	* facial and cranial deformity
	* increased ICP
* see [[Neurosurgery/Pediatrics/Craniosynostosis]]
