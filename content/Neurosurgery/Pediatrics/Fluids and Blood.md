### **Pediatric**
#### Non-newborn fluid replacement
* ==4:2:1 rule per hour==; ==100:50:20 rule per day==
* 0-10 kg
	* ==per h: 4 mL/kg/h==
	* ==per d: 100 mL/kg/d==
* 10-20 kg
	* ==per h: 2 mL/kg/h==
	* ==per d: 50 mL/kg/d==
* \> 20 kg
	* ==per h: 1 mL/kg/h==
	* ==per d: 20 mL/kg/d==
#### Key points
* boluses:
	* 10-20 mL/kg over 1 h
	* normal saline or ringers lactate
* blood/PLT/FFP/transfusions:
	* 10 mL/kg over 1-4 h
* NG replacement q4-6h with DbLR or D5 1/2NS
* paediatrics dosing should never exceed adult dosing
* urine output target
	* 1 mL/kg/h
#### Circulating blood volumes
* premature infant: 90-100 mL/kg
* full term infant: 80 mL/kg
* 1-12 month: 75 mL/kg
* \> 1 year: 70 mL/kg
#### Transfusions
* PRBC or FFP 10 mL/kg
#### BP norms in children
* SBP < 1 month: > 60 mmHg
* 1 - 12 month: > 70 mmHg
