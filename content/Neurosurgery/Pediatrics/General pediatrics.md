_Alphabetically sorted_
## ADHD
* <7 year old onset
* distractable, impulsive, excitable, hyperactive
* management
	* first line: ==methylphenidate, amphetamines==
	* second line: atomoxetine (SNRI)

## APGAR score
* assessed at 1 and 5 min post-natally
* criteria
	* appearance (color)
		* all blue/pale, pink body/blue extremities, all pink
	* pulse (heart rate)
		* absent, <100 BPM, >100 BPM
	* grimace (reflex response)
		* none, grimace, sneeze/cough
	* activity (muscle tone)
		* limp, some flexion, active
	* respiration
		* absent, irregular/slow, good/crying

## Autism
* pervasive developmental disorder
* qualitative impairments in
	* social interaction deficits
	* spoken language deficits
	* repetitive stereotypes patterns of behaviour
* mental retardation (75% cases)
* occurs before age 3 years

## Bradycardia
* associated with hypoxemia in neonates

## Child abuse
* workup
	* skeletal x-ray (< 5 years)
	* radionuclide bone scanning
	* CT head/chest
	* ophthalmologic exam

## Developmental delay
* failure to achieve expected motor/cognitive milestones
* can be d/t encephalopathy

## Diet supplementation
* common requirements
	* iron (if non-commercial formulas)
	* vitamin D (if breast feeding)
	* fluoride
		* start > 6 months if water fluoride < 0.3 ppm
	* calcium + phosphorus (preterm infants)
* if commercial formula-fed, no vitamin/mineral supplementation required

## Disruptive behavioural disorders
* oppositional defiant disorder (ODD), conduct disorder
* criteria:
	* violates rights of others
	* hostility towards authority figures
	* theft/vandolism
* antisocial personality disorder as adult

## Elimination (Toileting) Disorders
* enuresis, encopresis (primary vs. secondary)
* when to start toilet trainig
	* bowel control - 2-3 years
		* abnormal: incontinence > 4 years
	* urinary control - 3-4 years
		* abnormal: incontinence > 5 years
* self-toileting by 5 years

## Failure to thrive
* inadequate nutrition
* cause: psychosocial, medical
* presentation
	* weight < 3% percentile
	* reduced head circumference (severe cases)

## Feeding
* infants <34w GA must be fed by NG tube
* < 6 months
	* exclusive breastfeeding
* 6-12 months
	* breast feeding + solid foods
	* breast feeding alternatives: formula
		* avoid wheat, eggs, peanutes, chocolate, honey, whole cow's milk in 1st year
* \> 12 months
	* full diet +/- breast feeding
* \> 24 months
	* same diet as family

## Heart rate
* \> 100 BPM + resp effort + cyanosis
	* Mx: O2 supplementation
* < 100 BPM or resp distress or central cyanosis
	* Mx: bag-mask ventilation
* < 60 BPM
	* chest compression
	* intubation + mechanical ventilation
	* epinephrine

## Infant colic
* presentation
	* paroxysms of crying/irritability
	* apparent abdominal pain
	* appetite is good
	* weight gain is normal
* management
	* self-resolving (3-5 months)
	* soothing skills
	* pacified
* if age > 9 months, consider irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)

## Lactating mother diet
* avoid foods that cause colic
* extra 600 kcal
* extra 400 mg calcium

## Language
* 6 months - babbling
* 1 year - 1 word speaker
* 2 years - 2 work phrases
* 3 years - 3 word phrases
* 4 years - close to adult speech competence

## Learning Disabilities
* subtle CNS dysfunction
	* dyslexia, dysphasia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia, dysnomia
* discrepancy between academic potential and performance

## Medication doses
* weight-based

## Mental Retardation
* global developmental delay
	* delayed intellectual development
		* slow progress, not developmental arrest
		* IQ < 70
	* immature behaviour
	* limited self-care skils
* if < 5 years, diagnosis should be developmental delay

## Postmaturity
* failing placental function > 42 w GA
* placental insufficiency syndrome

## Prematurity
* < 37w GA
* neonates <23-24w GA do not have sufficient lung development
	* not compatible with life

## Recurrent Pain Syndromes
* episodic pain occurring at least monthly x 3 months, well between episodes
* no organic pathology
* etiology
	* recurrent abdominal pain syndrome
	* limb pain ("growing pains")
* presentation
	* school phobia
	* separation anxiety
	* stranger anxiety

## Sex chromosome abnormalities
* Turner syndrome
* Triple X syndrome
* Klinefelter syndrome
* 47XYY
* 48XXXX
* 49XXXXX
* Fragile X syndrome

## Small for gestational age
* IUGR; intrauterine growth restriction
* fetal weight < 10% percentile for GA
* low birth weight: < 2500 g
* very low birth weight: < 1500 g

## Trisomy syndromes
* Down syndrome (T21)
* Edwards syndrome (T18)
* Patau syndrome (T13)
