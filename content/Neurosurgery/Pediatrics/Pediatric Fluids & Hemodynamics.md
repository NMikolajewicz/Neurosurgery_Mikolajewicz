> [!summary] Pediatric fluid management summary
> **Maintenance fluids:** use **4:2:1 (mL/kg/h)** or **100:50:20 (mL/kg/day)**.  
> **Resuscitation bolus:** **10–20 mL/kg** isotonic crystalloid over ~**1 hr**.  
> **Replace ongoing losses** (e.g., NG) **q4–6h** and monitor **UOP ≥ 1 mL/kg/h**.

---

## Maintenance fluid replacement (non-newborn)

### Rules of thumb

- **Hourly (4:2:1 rule):**
    - **0–10 kg:** **4 mL/kg/hr**
    - **10–20 kg:** **2 mL/kg/hr**
    - **>20 kg:** **1 mL/kg/hr**
- **Daily (100:50:20 rule):**
    - **0–10 kg:** **100 mL/kg/day**
    - **10–20 kg:** **50 mL/kg/day**
    - **>20 kg:** **20 mL/kg/day**

> [!tip] Quick calculation 
> **Maintenance (mL/hr)** = 4×(first 10 kg) + 2×(next 10 kg) + 1×(each kg >20).  

---

## Boluses, replacement, and practical dosing

### Bolus fluids (resuscitation)

- **10–20 mL/kg over 1 hr**
- Use **normal saline (NS)** or **Ringer's lactate (LR)**

### Blood products / transfusions

- **Blood / PLT / FFP:** **10 mL/kg over 1–4 hr**
- **PRBC or FFP:** **10 mL/kg** (key recall)

### Ongoing losses

- **NG replacement q4–6 hr** with **DbLR** or **D5 ½NS**

> [!warning] Safety / dosing pearl  
> **Pediatric dosing should never exceed adult dosing.**  
> _(Apply across fluids, meds, and blood products.)_

---

## Monitoring targets

- **Urine output goal:** **≥ 1 mL/kg/hr**

---

## Circulating blood volume (CBV)

- **Premature infant:** **90–100 mL/kg**
- **Full-term infant:** **80 mL/kg**
- **1–12 months:** **75 mL/kg**
- **> 1 year:** **70 mL/kg**

> [!note] why CBV matters
> CBV estimates help anticipate **hemodynamic impact** of blood loss and guide **transfusion thresholds/urgency** during OR/ICU management.

---

## Blood pressure norms (minimum SBP)

- **< 1 month:** **SBP > 60 mmHg**
- **1–12 months:** **SBP > 70 mmHg**

> [!tip]  Common bedside rule
> For older children, a widely used quick screen for hypotension is ==**SBP < 70 + 2×age (years)**==.
