## Pathophysiology
* (sub)acute brain lesions due to systemic hypoxemia or cerebral ischemia
* encephalopathy from asphyxia
* severity dependent on brain maturity and duration of ischemia
* ==involvement of multiple organs besides brain is hallmark of HIE==
* most common cause of [[Neurosurgery/Pediatrics/Cerebral palsy]] 
## Typical course / presentation
1. depression
2. hyperalertness/hyperreflexia
3. coma
## Duration of ischemia
* <10 min (profound) asphyxia
	* ==no permanent brain damage==
* \>20-25 min (total) asphyxia
	* results in ==diffuse lesions==
	* rarely compatible with life
* partial asphyxia mins to hours
	* predominantly supratentorial lesions
	* preterm infants
		* damage occurs at _germinal matrix area_
			* periventricular hemorrhage, leukomalacia
	* post-36 week GA
		* lesions involve:
			* cerebral cortex
				* ulegyria - laminar neuronal necrosis in depths of sulci
			* basal ganglia
				* status marmoratus
			* brain stem
			* cerebellar purkinje cells
				* cerebellar atrophy
* minor intrauterine anoxic episodes of undetermined duration
	* involves neurons diffusely
	* preferentially affects _hippocampal areas_
## Workup
* ==clinical diagnosis==
* ultrasound
	* cerebral edema
	* ischemic lesions - diffuse/localized echodensities
* pulsed doppler
	* increased diastolic velocities with reduced resistive index
		* sign of luxury perfusion
* CT
	* cerebral edema
	* infarction
## Management
* +/- ==brain hypothermia==
	* insufficient evidence for strong recommendations
	* potentially useful for mild-to-moderate cases
* seizures
	* ==phenobarbital== (1st line)
	* lorazepam (2nd line)
	* phenytoin IV (3rd line)
