#pneumocephalus #SDH
### Post-SDH evacuation

> [!Summary]
> Mx: 100% O2, Flat bedrest
> Prevention: burrhole, irrigation, young age (<60)
> Complication: cSDH recurrence

#### Management
* 100% O2 supplementation leads to resolution
* Flat bed rest - reduces potential space in subdural space
#### Prevention
* ==Burrhole== instead of mini crani
* Keeping cranial opening at top of cavity to minimize air entry
* Irrigation - disputed whether this helps
* More common in older than younger individuals
	* 86% in >60 yr
	* 14% in <60 yr
#### Complications
* Controversial whether it results in worse outcomes
* May increase rate of cSDH recurrence
* Increased hospital stay
* tension pneumcephalus
