## MRI
### Sequences
#### T1
* fat = bright
* water = dark
* WM (more fat) > GM
* high-resolution anatomic detail
* +/- gadolinium
#### T2
* water = bright
* GM (more water) > WM
* more sensitive in detecting focal abnormalities
* FLAIR
	* T2 with nullified water
	* used in brain
* STIR
	* T2 with nullified fat
	* used in spine
#### GRE/SWI (gradient-recalled echo/susceptability-weighted imaging)
* most sensitive for acute/subacute blood breakdown products
### Screen patients for
* implanted devices
* metallic foreign bodies
	* e.g., metal work history
### Tissue discrimination
#### Normal tissue 
* Fat | ++T1 | +T2
* bone/calcification | -T1 | -T2
* CSF, edema | -T1 | ++T2
* GM | -T1 | +T2
* WM | +T1 | -T2
* flow void in vessels | -T1 | -T2
#### Abnormal tissue
* air | -T1 | -T2
* acute/subacute infarct | -T1 | +T2
* melanin | +T1 | +/-T2
* demyelination | - | +T2
## CT
### non-contrast CT
* acute head/spine trauma
* acute stroke
* bone disease
* spinal column disease
### contrast CT
* The purpose of contrast is to evaluate processes that alter permeability of BBB that causes abnormal contrast enhancement.
* Physiologically, the blood-brain barrier prevents  leakage of contrast into brain, spinal cord and the proximal nerves.
* Alteration of BBB caused by:
	* angiogenesis: new blood vessel formation
	* inflammation
	* ischemia
	* increased pressure
* Useful for characterization of:
	* [brain metastases](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/brain-metastases?lang=us): variable enhancement
	* [meningioma](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/meningioma?lang=us): solid intense enhancement
	* [brain abscesses](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/brain-abscesses?lang=us): 
		* [double rim sign](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/dual-rim-sign-brain-abscess?lang=us): hypodense outer rim and a hyperdense inner rim
		* single rim of hyperdense or isodense
		* fluid/pus: hypoattenuating center
	* necrotic neoplasm (e.g. [glioblastoma](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/glioblastoma-idh-wildtype?lang=us)) or [abscess](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/cerebral-abscess-summary?lang=us): outer enhancing ring surrounding a necrotic center
	* [meningitis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/leptomeningitis?lang=us) and meningoencephalitis: [Leptomeningeal enhancement](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/leptomeningeal-enhancement?lang=us)
	* [multiple sclerosis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/multiple-sclerosis?lang=us): contrast enhancement of plaques
	* [lymphoma](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/lymphomas-of-the-central-nervous-system?lang=us): enhancement of lesion post-contrast
	* [ependymitis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/ependymitis-granularis?lang=us) and [ventriculitis](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/ventriculitis?lang=us): thin linear enhancement of the margins of the ventricles

### Misc
#### acute hematoma
* high attenuation
* due to clot retraction with separation of high-density erythrocytes from lower density plasma
#### unclotted blood
* coagulopathy or hyperacute active bleeding
* relative lucent

## Magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS)
* evaluates presence and concentration of various metabolites. 
* MRS often does not add much value to MR study
	* provides increased specificity
	* predicts histological grade.
### Peaks representing:
* creatinine (CR)
	* cellular energy metabolism
	* glia > neurons
* choline (CHO)
	* cell membranes
	* glia > neurons
	* elevated with abnormal membrane metabolism, myelin breakdown, inflammation, neoplasia
* N-acetyl aspartate (NAA)
	* neuronal marker
	* marker of neuronal integrity
	* reduces with neuronal loss
* lactate
	* anaerobic glycolysis
	* inflammation, infarction, abscess
* lipids
	* products of brain destruction
	* radiation necrosis
### Profiles
* tumor - ↑ ==choline==/lactate
* necrosis - ↑ lipids (else low)
* stroke - ↑ ==lactate== (else low)
* abscess - ==atypical peaks==
* MS - normal

## IV contrast enhancement

### Considerations before constrast

* **allergy** to contrast
	* use MRI or premedication:
		* ==prednisone 50 mg PO x3 doses (-13h, -7h, -1h)==
		* ==diphenhydramine 50 mg PO x1 dose (-1h)==
* **nephropathy** (GFR)
	* after iodinated contrast
		* ==hemodialysis== per regular schedule
		* require GFR > 45 d/t risk of ==contrast nephropathy==
			* increased creatinine > 1 mg/dL per 48h
			* prophylaxis: hydration
	* after gadolinium
		* hemodialysis x3 consecutive days
			* start HD immediately after MRI
		* require GFR > 30 d/t risk of ==nephrogenic systemic fibrosis==
* **diabetes** (metformin)
	* ==hold metformin x48 h== prior to iodine contrast
	* iodine contrast delays excretion of metformin
		* complication: ==lactic acidosis==
* **pregnancy**
	* CT
		* risk
			* ↑ 6% risk in childhood cancer  per 100 rads
			* 2x risk of congenital defects with 5-10 rad
		* exposure
			* maternal abdomen - 3 rad
			* head/cervical spine - <0.01 rad
	* MRI
		* gadolinium contraindicated
### Contrast indications
* infection
* inflammation
* neoplasm
* leptomeningeal / nerve root involvement
* seizures
* spinal
### Contrast contraindications
* hemorrhagic
* ischemic
* congenital anomaly
* head trauma
* neurodegenerative disease
* hydrocephalus
* spinal cord - trauma, degenerative disease
### Normally-enhancing structures
* lack of BBB
	* dural structures
		* falx, tentorium
		* pituitary gland
		* pineal gland
* blood
	* vessels
	* choroid plexus
