## MRI DWI: **"Starfield" pattern**

- **Key finding:** **Multiple punctate foci of restricted diffusion** → scattered "starfield" appearance on DWI
- **Clinical context link:** supports **cerebral fat embolism** when paired with FES features

> [!Pearl]  
> **DWI "starfield" pattern** = **cerebral fat embolism**
