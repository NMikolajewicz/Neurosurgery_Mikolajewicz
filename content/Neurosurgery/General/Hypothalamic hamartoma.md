#hypothalamic-hamartoma #seizures/gelastic-seizures
### Pathophysiology
* congenital non-neoplastic lesions in ventral hypothalamus
* clusters of normal neurons with abnormal architecture
* Subtypes
	* parathalamic / pedunculated (precocious puberty)
	* intrathalamic / sessile (seizures)
### Presentation
* gelastic seizures
	* burst of mechanical laughter with retained LOC
* precocious puberty (GnRH)
* secondary seizure types
	* 38% cases
	* due to kindling
* progressive encephalopathy
### Management
* difficult to manage with anti-epileptic drugs alone
* LITTS
	* treatment of choice
	* aims to disconnect neurons from network
	* complications
		* short-term memory dysfunction
		* endocrine dysfunction
			* hypothalamic obesity
			* diabetes insipidus
