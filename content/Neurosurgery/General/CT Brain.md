### "blood can be very bad"

#### blood
* EDH - lens shaped
	* hyperdense lens shaped bleee
	* no suture crossing
	* life-threatening if lower temporal region (MMA laceration)
* SDH - crescent shaped
	* acute
		* hyperdense on CT
		* thin crescent shape
		* crosses sutures
		* follows curvature of gyri/sulci
		* high mortality
	* chronic
		* hypodense on CT
		* crescent shape, similar to acute SDH
		* crosses sutures
		* often bilateral
* IVH - +/- hydrocephalus
	* associated with idiopathic AMV, hypertensive/tumor IPH, or SAH
	* evaluate for extension up to 3rd or 4th ventricles
	* may produce acute obstructive hydrocephalus
		* d/t blockage of monroe, aqueduct or 4th ventricle
* IPH - basal ganglia or lobar, etc.
	* hyperdense on CT
	* basal ganglia associated with HTN, hemorrhagic transformation of stroke, tumor, AVM, cavernoma or trauma
* SAH - blood in basilar cistern
	* blood distributed in cisterns
	* Fisher grading system
		* predicts risk of [[vasospasm]]
* cisterns
* evaluate effacement/blood in cisterns
* consider following cisterns (4 key highlighted):
	* ==perimesencephalic==
	* interpeduncular
	* prepontine
	* ==suprasellar==
	* ambient
	* ==quadrigeminal==
	* foramen magnum
	* ==sylvian==
* brain
* evaluate symmetry between 2 hemispheres
* grey/white border
	* is border distinct?
	* loss of GM-WM differentiation = anoxic brain injury
* midline shift
	* evaluate at the level of septum pellucidum
* pneumocephalus
	* associated with open fractures or fracture through sinus
* gyri/sulci
	* evaluate effacement and atrophy
* ventricles
* effacement or asymmetry of ventricles
* hydrocephalus and trans-ependymal flow
	* correlate with atrophy, i.e., ex vacuo
* blood in ventricles
* bone
* fractures
	* careful attention to temporal bone
* sinuses, air cells
