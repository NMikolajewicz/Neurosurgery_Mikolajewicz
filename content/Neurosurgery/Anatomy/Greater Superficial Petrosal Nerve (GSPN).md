## Identity

- First branch of the facial nerve (CN VII)
	- Branch of **CN VII (facial nerve)** from the **geniculate ganglion**
- Carries **preganglionic parasympathetic** fibers for **lacrimation + nasal/palatal/pharyngeal gland secretion**
	- Also carries:
	    - **Taste** fibers from **soft palate** → geniculate ganglion
	    - **Vasomotor** fibers

> [!Course]  
> **GSPN + deep petrosal nerve join at the _foramen lacerum_ → form the _Vidian nerve_ (nerve of the pterygoid canal) → synapse in the _pterygopalatine ganglion_.**
## Origin

- **Geniculate ganglion** (facial nerve) within **petrous temporal bone**
- Preganglionic parasympathetics arise from **superior salivatory nucleus**
## Course and Segments

| Segment                         | Key location                                                    | Relations                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Intrapetrous**                | From **geniculate ganglion** in facial canal                    | Preganglionic parasympathetics                                                                                                                       |
| **Suprapetrous (middle fossa)** | Exits via **facial hiatus** → runs in **major petrosal groove** | **Extradural** (beneath middle fossa dura); passes **deep to trigeminal (Gasserian) ganglion / Meckel's cave**; **medial to MMA + foramen spinosum** |
| **Foramen lacerum**             | Reaches **foramen lacerum**                                     | Joins **deep petrosal nerve** (postganglionic sympathetics from **ICA plexus**)                                                                      |
| **Pterygoid canal**             | **Vidian nerve** traverses **pterygoid (Vidian) canal**         | Terminates in **pterygopalatine (sphenopalatine) ganglion**                                                                                          |

> [!tip]  
> In **middle cranial fossa**, GSPN runs **extradurally** in the **major petrosal groove** and is **medial** to **MMA/foramen spinosum**.

## Functional Outputs

- **Secretomotor (parasympathetic):**
    - **Lacrimal gland**
    - **Nasal cavity**, **pharynx**, **palate** mucosal glands
- **Sympathetic companion pathway (via deep petrosal → Vidian):**
    - Postganglionic sympathetics from **internal carotid plexus** join at **foramen lacerum**
- **Special sensory:**
    - **Taste from soft palate** → geniculate ganglion
## Surgical Relevance

### Landmark Use

- **Middle cranial fossa / skull base landmark**
    - Used to help identify **geniculate ganglion** and **internal auditory canal (IAC)**
    - Helps locate **facial nerve** while avoiding **cochlea** and **superior semicircular canal**

### Vulnerable Corridors

- **Middle fossa approaches** (IAC/CPA access)
- **Petrous apex / anterior petrosectomy** corridors
- **Temporal bone fractures** involving petrous segment

> [!warning]  
> **Traction on GSPN during middle fossa dural elevation** can transmit tension to the **geniculate ganglion** → **facial palsy ± GSPN injury**.

---

## Injury Pattern

### Key Clinical Effects

|Injury consequence|Exam features (from source)|
|---|---|
|**↓ Lacrimation**|**Absent ipsilateral lacrimation**, **xerophthalmia (dry eye)**|
|**Nasal hypersecretion**|**Pseudo-CSF rhinorrhea** (clear rhinorrhea from hypersecretion, not CSF leak)|
|Autonomic imbalance symptoms|**Nasal stuffiness**, occasional **facial flushing**|

> [!Clinical Pearl]  
> Clear rhinorrhea after skull base work is **not always CSF**: **GSPN injury → nasal hypersecretion → "pseudo-CSF rhinorrhea."**

## Syndromes

- **Crocodile tears syndrome**
    - Aberrant regeneration after **facial nerve injury** → **lacrimation while eating**
