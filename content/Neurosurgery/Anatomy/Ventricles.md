### Liliequist membrane

**Definition:** An **arachnoid membrane** separating the **chiasmatic cistern** from the **interpeduncular cistern**.

> [!info] Core point  
> **Liliequist membrane** ==separates the chiasmatic and interpeduncular cisterns== and is **arachnoid**.

![[Neurosurgery/Anatomy/_resources/Ventricles.resources/Image 20230919 083924.jpeg|500]]  
![[Neurosurgery/Anatomy/_resources/Ventricles.resources/Image 20230919 084926.png|500]]

> [!tip] ETV relevance
> In **ETV**, after fenestrating the **floor of the third ventricle**, a persistent **Liliequist membrane** can limit CSF egress into the **interpeduncular/prepontine cisterns**. If flow looks "blocked" despite a patent stoma, consider whether an additional arachnoid membrane fenestration is needed (technique-dependent).

## Lateral ventricles

---
### Key cortical relationship
- **Atrium (trigone)** of the lateral ventricle is **deep to the supramarginal gyrus** (**BA 40**).

### Frontal horn boundaries

**Walls of the frontal horns of the lateral ventricles formed by:**
- **Lateral wall:** **Caudate nucleus**
- **Medial wall:** **Septum pellucidum**
- **Roof:** **Corpus callosum**

## Fourth ventricle

### Floor (rhomboid fossa)

- The posterior surface of the **pons** + upper open part of the **medulla oblongata** form the ==floor of the fourth ventricle==.
    - Covered by **ependyma**
- Rhomboid shape → **rhomboid fossa**
- **Obex**
    - Lower end of the fossa
    - Upper end continues into the **cerebral aqueduct** (midbrain)

> [!anatomy] summary
> **4th ventricle floor** = dorsal pons + open medulla, ependyma-lined, rhomboid fossa with **obex** caudally and **aqueduct** rostrally.

---

### CSF flow (in/out)

- **Inflow:** **Cerebral aqueduct**
- **Outflow:**
    - **Foramina of Luschka** (paired lateral apertures)
    - **Foramen of Magendie** (midline aperture)

> [!tip] Clinical supplementation  
> Obstruction at the **aqueduct** or outlets (**Magendie/Luschka**) can produce **obstructive hydrocephalus**; outlet obstruction classically enlarges the **4th ventricle** along with supratentorial ventricles (pattern depends on level).

---

### Lateral recess / Luschka neighborhood

- The **dorsal and ventral cochlear nuclei (CN VIII)** are positioned in the **lateral recess** near the **foramen of Luschka**.

> [!warning] Surgical relevance  
> Work near the **lateral recess** (tumor, vascular, or CPA-adjacent pathology) risks **auditory pathway** structures—helpful mental map when interpreting postoperative hearing changes.

## Third ventricle 



**Borders of the 3rd ventricle**
- **Lateral walls:** medial **thalami** (superior) and **hypothalami** (inferior), separated by the **hypothalamic sulcus**; often bridged by **massa intermedia** (interthalamic adhesion).
- **Roof:** **fornices** with the **tela choroidea/velum interpositum** and **choroid plexus** (surgical “roof” is approached via the fornix region).
- **Floor:** from anterior → posterior: **optic chiasm/optic recess**, **infundibulum & tuber cinereum**, **mammillary bodies**, then **posterior perforated substance/tegmentum**; for ETV the floor is assessed as thin/translucent with **basilar artery and mammillary bodies** as key landmarks.
- **Anterior wall:** **lamina terminalis**, **anterior commissure**, and **columns of fornix** (with the **foramina of Monro** anterosuperiorly).
- **Posterior wall:** **posterior commissure**, **pineal region (pineal recess/suprapineal recess)**, leading into the **cerebral aqueduct**.

### Common operative corridors

- **Endoscopic transventricular** (via lateral ventricle → foramen of Monro → third ventricle)
    - Often paired conceptually with **ETV** or intraventricular lesion work
- **Transcallosal (interhemispheric)** → lateral ventricle → Monro
- **Transcortical** → lateral ventricle → Monro (trajectory varies by lesion position)
- **Translamina terminalis** (anterior third ventricle / suprasellar region–dependent)

Fourth ventricle

* * *

- The posterior surface of the [pons](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=pons) and the upper open part of the [medulla oblongata](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=medulla_oblongata) form the ==floor of the [fourth ventricle](https://operativeneurosurgery.com/doku.php?id=fourth_ventricle).==

- Obex
	- The lower end of the fossa
	- the upper end continues into the aqueduct of the midbrain.
- CSF flow
	- Inflow
		- cerebral aqueduct
	- Outflow
		- Foramina of Luschka - 2 lateral foramina
		- Foramina of Magendie - 1 midline foramen
- The dorsal and ventral cochlear nuclei of CN VIII are positioned in the lateral recess near the foramen of Luschka.
