Here are the high-yield study notes regarding C2 (Axis) Anatomy, extracted from the provided sources.

## Osteology of the Axis (C2)

### General Features

- **Largest** and strongest cervical vertebra.
- **Transitional vertebra:** Connects the mobile upper cervical spine (O-C1) to the subaxial spine.
- **Spinous Process:** Large, bifid; first palpable midline structure below the occiput.
- **Foramen Transversarium:** Directed superolaterally; deviates the vertebral artery (VA) **45° laterally** before it enters the C1 foramen.

### Odontoid Process (Dens)

- **Morphology:** Peg-shaped protrusion; provides the pivot point for C1 rotation.
- **Articulations:**
    - **Anterior:** Articulates with the anterior arch of the atlas (C1).
    - **Posterior:** Articulates with the **transverse ligament** (grooved surface).
- **Embryology & Ossification:**
    - **4 Ossification Centers:** 1 body, 2 neural arches, 1 dens (formed by paired centers that fuse by 3 months).
    - **Neurocentral Synchondrosis:** Cartilaginous disc separating dens from C2 body.
        - Fuses by **age 4–8 years** (visible on X-ray until this age; do not confuse with fracture).
    - **Apical Epiphysis (Tip):** Separate ossification center (ossiculum terminale).
        - Fuses to dens by **age 12 years**.

> [!exam] High-Yield Embryology The dens is phylogenetically the **centrum (body) of C1** that has fused to C2. **Os Odontoideum:** Defined as an ovoid ossicle distinct from the C2 body; results from failure of fusion (or unrecognized trauma) at the neurocentral synchondrosis.

### Pars Interarticularis and Facets

- **Superior Articular Facet:**
    - Located **anterior** to the inferior facet (unlike subaxial vertebrae where they form a vertical pillar).
    - Proximal to the vertebral body.
    - **VA Foramen Relationship:** The foramen is located partially/completely in the **inferior aspect** of the superior facet (critical for screw trajectory).
- **Inferior Articular Facet:** Located posterior to the superior facet; articulates with C3.
- **Pars Interarticularis:** Connects the superior and inferior facets.
    - _Surgical Relevance:_ Target for C2 pars screws.
    - _Pathology:_ Fracture site for **Hangman's fracture** (Traumatic Spondylolisthesis).

## Ligamentous Anatomy (Craniocervical Junction)

|Ligament|Attachments|Function|Clinical Relevance|
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
|**Transverse**|Medial tubercles of C1 lateral masses|Restricts **anterior translation** of C1 on C2|Primary stabilizer of A-A joint. Disruption > **6.9 mm** C1 overhang (Rule of Spence).|
|**Alar**|Upper lateral dens to medial occipital condyles|Limits **axial rotation** and lateral bending|Paired. Tighten on rotation opposite to movement.|
|**Apical**|Tip of dens to basion (anterior FM)|Weak resistance to translation|Derived from proatlas centrum.|
|**Tectorial Membrane**|Posterior C2 body to basion/clivus|Limits **extension** and distraction|Upward continuation of the **PLL**.|
|**Cruciate**|Superior/Inferior bands + Transverse ligament|Stabilizes dens|Cross-shaped complex behind dens.|

> [!tip] MRI Appearance **Transverse Ligament:** Seen as a continuous low-intensity band on gradient-echo sequences. Disruption (Type I) mandates surgery; bony avulsion (Type II) may heal with immobilization.

## Vascular Anatomy (Vertebral Artery)

### Course at C2

1. **Ascent:** VA travels in V2 segment (foramen transversarium C6–C2).
2. **C2 Foramen:** Exits C2 transverse foramen.
3. **Loop:** Deviates **laterally** to reach the wider C1 transverse foramen.
4. **Groove:** Makes a deep groove on the **inferior surface** of the C2 superior articular facet.

### Surgical Risks

- **Transarticular/Pars Screws:** High risk due to the "serpentine" course.
- **Medial Loop:** The VA can loop medially toward the C2 body.
    - **Distance:** Average distance from C2 midline to VA is **12 mm** (transoral view).
    - **High-riding VA:** May preclude C2 pedicle/transarticular screw placement; requires evaluation with preoperative CTA.

## Neurological Anatomy

### C2 Nerve Root

- **Exit Path:** Passes **posterior** to the C1-C2 facet joint (unlike subaxial roots which pass anterior to the facet).
- **C2 Ganglion:** Located just above the C2 arch/lamina, between the C1 posterior arch and C2 lamina.
    - Occupies most of the foramen.
    - Surrounded by a rich **venous plexus** (source of bleeding during C1-C2 posterior exposure).
- **Branches:**
    - **Ventral Ramus:** Contributes to cervical plexus.
    - **Dorsal Ramus:** Divides into:
        1. **Greater Occipital Nerve (GON):** Supplies sensation to posterior scalp/vertex.
        2. **Lesser Occipital Nerve:** Sensation to mastoid/posterior ear.

### Spinal Cord

- **Space:** The spinal canal is spacious at C1-C2 (Steel's Rule of Thirds: 1/3 cord, 1/3 dens, 1/3 empty space/CSF).
- **Diameter:** Sagittal diameter < **14 mm** at C1-C2 suggests stenosis.

## Biomechanics of C1-C2

|Motion|Range (Degrees)|Contribution to Total C-Spine|
|:--|:--|:--|
|**Axial Rotation**|**50°** (total ~90° for C1-C2)|**50%**|
|**Flexion/Extension**|10–20°|Minimal|
|**Lateral Bending**|0° (limited by alar ligaments)|Negligible|

- **Fusion Consequence:** Fusion of C1-C2 results in a **50% loss** of cervical head rotation.

## C2 Anatomy (Axis)

### Osseous Components

- **Vertebral body (C2)**
    - Anterior weight-bearing column
    - Target region for **C2 pedicle screw** purchase (trajectory ends in body)
- **Odontoid process (dens)**
    - **Anterior facet** articulates with **C1 anterior arch**
    - **Posterior facet/groove** interfaces with **transverse ligament** restraint
    - **Alar ligament** attachment site (dens → occipital condyles)
- **Pedicle**
    - Connects **vertebral body** to **posterior elements**
    - Forms **lateral wall** of spinal canal at C2
- **Pars interarticularis / isthmus**
    - Segment between **superior** and **inferior** articular processes
    - Primary corridor assessed by **C2IsH** and **C2InH**
- **Laminae**
    - Thick posterior elements; corridor for **C2 translaminar screws**
- **Spinous process**
    - Prominent posterior midline landmark (exposure/orientation)
- **Transverse process + transverse foramen**
    - Conduit for **vertebral artery (VA)** adjacent to lateral screw paths
- **Superior articular facets**
    - Articulate with **C1 lateral masses** (lateral atlantoaxial joints)
- **Inferior articular facets**
    - Articulate with **C3** (C2–3 facet joints)

### Joints and Stabilizers

- **Atlantoaxial joints (C1–2)**
    - **Median pivot joint:** dens ↔ C1 anterior arch (rotation)
    - **Paired lateral facet joints:** C1 lateral mass ↔ C2 superior facet
- **Key ligament relationships**
    - **Transverse ligament** restrains dens posteriorly (stability of pivot)
    - **Alar ligaments** limit rotation/lateral bending (dens → occiput)

### Neurovascular Relationships 

- **Vertebral artery (VA)**
    - Courses through **C2 transverse foramen**
    - **VA groove / high-riding VA** reduces available **pars/pedicle corridor**
- **Spinal canal**
    - **Medial** boundary for pedicle/pars trajectories (breach → canal risk)
- **C2 nerve root**
    - Exits at **C1–2**; relevant during posterior exposure and screw entry work

> [!warning] "Danger zones" for C2 screw corridors  
> **Lateral/superior**: **VA/transverse foramen** • **Medial**: **spinal canal** • **Posterior element**: **lamina** (for translaminar screw path integrity)

### C2 Screw Options Mapped to Anatomy

|Fixation option|Primary bony corridor|Typical risk structure(s)|When favored|
|---|---|---|---|
|**C2 pedicle screw**|**Pedicle → body**|**VA (lateral/superior)**, **canal (medial)**|Adequate pedicle + safe VA relationship|
|**C2 pars screw**|**Pars/isthmus**|**VA groove/high-riding VA**, **canal**|When pedicle less favorable but pars corridor acceptable|
|**C2 translaminar screw**|**Lamina**|Laminar breach (canal if medial)|When **pedicle/pars corridor constrained** (e.g., VA anatomy, small isthmus)|

## C2 Pedicle/Pars CT Morphometrics (Isthmus)

### Indication

- Pre-op **CT morphometric assessment** to determine feasibility of **C2 pedicle vs pars** screw placement.

### Measurements (CT-based)

|Metric|What it measures|CT plane / how measured|Key use|
|---|---|---|---|
|**C2 isthmus height (C2IsH)**|**Total (outer) craniocaudal bony height** of C2 isthmus/pars|**Parasagittal CT** aligned to **planned screw trajectory**; measure **minimum** superior cortex → inferior cortex along/near screw path|Defines **overall bony envelope**|
|**C2 internal height (C2InH)**|**Cancellous channel (inner) height** between cortices|Same plane/trajectory; measure at **narrowest point** from **inner cortex** of superior wall → **inner cortex** of inferior wall|Estimates **true containment margin** (cortex breach risk)|

> [!exam] High-yield distinction  
> **C2IsH = outer envelope** • **C2InH = inner containment corridor** (more directly tied to "contained screw" feasibility)

### Interpretation / Decision Rules

- **Low C2IsH** → limited **total bone** along planned trajectory.
- **Low C2InH** → limited **containment corridor** → increased likelihood of **cortical breach** with pedicle/pars screw.

> [!warning] If C2InH is low  
> Default to **alternative fixation** or **trajectory modification** rather than forcing a contained pedicle/pars screw.

### Anatomic Modifiers to Integrate With Heights

- **Pedicle width**
- **VA groove anatomy**
- **High-riding VA**
- Planned **entry point** and **trajectory** relative to canal and transverse foramen

### Common Alternatives When Corridor Is Poor

- **C2 laminar screws**
- **C2 pars screw** (if pars trajectory remains safe)
- **Trajectory modification** (only if canal + VA constraints remain acceptable)
