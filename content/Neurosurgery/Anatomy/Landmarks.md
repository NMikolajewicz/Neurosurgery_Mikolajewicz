### Kocher's Point

* * *

Kocher's Point

* places catheter in frontal horn of lateral ventricle
* entry site
	* 2-3 cm from midline
		* approximately mid-pupillary line
	* 1 cm anterior to coronal suture
		* or 10-12 cm behind nasion
		* to avoid motor strip
* trajectory
	* direct catheter perpendicular to surface of brain
	* coronal plane: towards medial canthus of ipsilateral eye
	* AP plane: towards EAM
* insertion length
	* 1\. advance catheter with stylet until CSF obtained
		* 5-7 cm depth (3-4 cm with dilated ventricles)
	* 2\. then advance catheter without stylet 1 cm deeper
	* if CSF not obtained (> 8 cm), tip is probably in cistern (prepontine cisterm)
