| **Feature**               | **Details**                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Anatomy**               | - Deep to the **Sylvian fissure** (lateral sulcus) <br>- Covered by **frontal, parietal, temporal opercula**                                                                                                |
| **Subdivisions**          | - **Anterior**: agranular (limbic-related) <br>- **Posterior**: granular (sensory-related)                                                                                                                  |
| **Function**              | - **Multimodal integration**: sensory (especially pain/temperature), visceral, emotional <br>- Role in **autonomic regulation**, **gustation**, **interoception**, and **language** (dominant hemisphere)   |
| **Clinical Significance** | - **Epilepsy focus** (especially insular epilepsy) <br>- May mimic frontal, parietal, or temporal seizures <br>- Involved in **post-stroke dysautonomia**, speech disturbances, or gustatory hallucinations |
| **Tumors**                | - Commonly ==**low-grade gliomas**==, including oligodendrogliomas and astrocytomas <br>- May extend to opercula or basal ganglia                                                                           |
| **Surgical Approach**     | - Requires **trans-sylvian** or **transopercular approach** <br>- Major risk: proximity to **MCA branches**, **ICAs**, **lenticulostriate arteries**, and **language/motor cortex**                         |
| **Vascular Supply**       | - Mainly via ==**M2 branches** of **MCA**== <br>- Risk of infarct to internal capsule from small penetrating arteries during resection                                                                      |
