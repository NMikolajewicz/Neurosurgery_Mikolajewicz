### Paediatric Spine

* pediatric spine is relatively hypermobile due to distinct anatomical features:
	* ligamentous laxity
	* underdevelopment of neck and paraspinal musculature
	* absent uncinate process (see below)
	* incomplete ossified wedge-shaped vertebrae
	* show, horizontaly oriented facets

### Uncinate Process

* absent in pediatric spine
