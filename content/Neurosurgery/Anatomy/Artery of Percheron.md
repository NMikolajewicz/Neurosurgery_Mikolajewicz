> [!Summary]  
> **Triad:** Altered mental status + vertical gaze palsy + memory impairment  
> **Imaging:** Bilateral paramedian thalamic infarcts  
> **Diagnosis:** Occlusion of **Artery of Percheron**

---

## Anatomy

### Artery of Percheron (AOP)

- Variant of **Posterior Cerebral Artery** (P1 segment)
	- estimated to occur in 4-12% of the population
	- term is used to refer to a solitary arterial trunk that branches from one of the proximal segments of either PCA
- Single perforator supplies:
    - **Bilateral paramedian thalami**
    - ± **Rostral midbrain**

## Vascular Supply – Thalamus

|Territory|Arterial Supply|Clinical Syndrome|
|---|---|---|
|Paramedian|P1 perforators / **AOP**|AMS, memory deficits, vertical gaze palsy|
|Inferolateral|Thalamogeniculate (P2)|Sensory loss, Dejerine–Roussy|
|Posterior|Posterior choroidal|Visual deficits|
|Anterior|Tuberothalamic|Memory + executive dysfunction|

---

## Clinical Features

### Core Findings

- **Altered consciousness (↓ ARAS function)**
- **Vertical gaze palsy**
- **Memory impairment (mediodorsal nucleus)**

### Additional Features

- Hypersomnolence/coma
- Confusion
- ± Oculomotor deficits (midbrain involvement)

---

## Imaging

- **Bilateral symmetric paramedian thalamic infarcts**
- ± "V-sign" in midbrain (if involved)

## Differential Diagnosis

|Diagnosis|Key Distinction|
|---|---|
|PCA infarct (P1)|Usually **unilateral** thalamus|
|Basilar artery occlusion|**Severe brainstem signs**, locked-in syndrome|
|Deep venous thrombosis|Thalamic edema ± hemorrhage|
|Wernicke encephalopathy|Mammillary bodies + periaqueductal involvement|

---

## High-Yield Associations

> [!Pearl]  
> **Bilateral thalamic infarcts = AOP until proven otherwise**

- Rare (~**4–12% of thalamic strokes**)
- Frequently missed on early CT
- Requires **high suspicion + MRI**

---

## Management

### Acute Stroke Protocol

- **IV thrombolysis** if eligible (time window)
- **Mechanical thrombectomy** (limited role; small vessel)

### Supportive

- Airway protection (↓ LOC)
- Manage complications (aspiration, delirium)

---

## Key Pitfalls

> [!warning]
> 
> - **Normal CT does NOT exclude AOP infarct**
> 
> - Misdiagnosed as:
> 
>     - Toxic/metabolic encephalopathy
> 
>     - Infection
>         
>     - Psychiatric disorder
