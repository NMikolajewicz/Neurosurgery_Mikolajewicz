**cerebral veins lack valves**

* Unlike veins in other parts of the body, cerebral veins lack valves

Key Structures and Pathways

* **Superior Sagittal Sinus**
	* Located in the midline within the falx cerebri (a fold of dura mater)
	* receives drainage from the cerebral convexities. 
* **Straight Sinus**
	* Receives drainage from the inferior sagittal sinus and the great cerebral vein of Galen
	* courses posteriorly
* **Transverse Sinuses**
	* Formed from the inferior margin of the superior sagittal sinus, they curve downward and backward as sigmoid sinuses. 
* **Sigmoid Sinuses**
	* drained by the internal jugular veins. 
* **Anastomoses**
	* The superficial and deep venous systems are interconnected through anastomoses (connections between vessels), allowing for alternative drainage pathways. 
* **Emissary Veins**
	* Small veins from the scalp can communicate with the dural sinuses via emissary veins that perforate the skull.
