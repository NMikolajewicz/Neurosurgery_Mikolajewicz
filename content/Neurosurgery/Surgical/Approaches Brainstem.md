## Translabrynthine Approach

* access
	* CN V, VI, VII, VIII
	* centered around CN VII and VIII

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Approaches_Brainstem.resources/image.3.png]]

## Telovar Approach

* posterior approach
* access
	* 4th ventricle
	* cerebral aqueduct
	* lateral recesses
* involves dividing tela choroidea and inferior medullary velum - these roof the interior half of the 4th ventricle
	

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Approaches_Brainstem.resources/image.4.png]]

## Supracerebellar Infratentorial Approach

* posterior approach
* access
	* plate
	* trochlear nerve CN IV
	* posterior 3rd ventricle
	* pineal gland

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Approaches_Brainstem.resources/image.2.png]]

## Retrosigmoid Approach

* access
	* CN IV to XII
	* CPA
* workhorse of posterior fossa
* lesions anterior to brain stem are difficult to reach with this approach

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Approaches_Brainstem.resources/image.png]]![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Approaches_Brainstem.resources/image.1.png]]

* access

	* jugular foramen
