## Bleeding Scale

Based on severity and necessity to act quickly and efficiently

		### Grade 1
	
	* mild to moderate
	* <10 cc/min
		### Grade 2
	
	* not necessarily higher bleeding rate than grade 1, but requires more elaborate hemostasis.
	* able to operate without hemostasis, but will require hemostasis prior to closing dura
		### Grade 3
	
	* ==severe local bleeding==
	* <50 cc/min
	* unable to operative further without immediate hemostasis.
	* e.g., arterial bleeding
		### Grade 4
	
	* ==severe diffuse bleeding==
	* e.g., highly vascularized large tumor

## Hemostatic algorithm

		### Primary methods: ==physical==
	
	* head elevation
	* blood pressure control
	* bipolar forceps
	* irrigation +/- hydrostatic elements
	* hydrogen peroxide saturated cottonoids
		### Secondary methods: ==passive hemostatic products==
	
	* cellulose mesh
	* gelatin sponges
		### Tertiary methods: ==active hemostatic products==
	
	* fibrin sealant
	* floables
	* advanced patches
		* fibrinogen/thrombin coated fleece
		* polyethylene glycol-coated biomaterial

## Hemostatic agents

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Hemostasis.resources/image.3.png]]

### Bone wax

* uses
	* bone surface hemostasis
	* control bleeding from emissary and diploic veins
	* prevent CSF leak
* composition: beewax (with almond oil and salicylic acid, or vasoline)
* mechanism: mechanical via occlusion
* complications:
	* allergies
	* embolism
	* granuloma formation
	* infection (do not use in contaminated fields) - reduced bacterial clearance
	* impaired bone healing

### Gelatin sponges

* products: Gelfoam
* uses: vascular, dura
* absorption: 4-6 weeks
* mechanism: absorbs 45x weight, concentrates clotting factors
* time to hemostasis: 5-10 min
* complications
	* epidural fibrosis
	* swelling in confined space (e.g., spinal cord)
	* giant-cell granuloma formation
	* thrombosis of superficial brain vessels

### Oxidized cellulose

* products: Surgicel
* uses:
	* low flow, diffuse bleeding
	* arterial/vascular, dura
* composition: wood pulp, 50% cellulose
* mechanism
	* hemostatic
	* bactericidal action
* time to hemostasis: 4-8 min
* complications
	* swelling
	* granuloma formation
		* can mimic as tumor recurrence
	* inflammatory reaction (giant cell reaction)
	* cortical vein thrombosis

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Hemostasis.resources/image.8.png]]

### Fibrin sealants

* products: Tisseel, Vistaseal
* uses:
	* hemostatics
	* dural sealant
		* fibrinogen is activated into fibrin, generating adhesive layer
* mechanism
	* solution of fibrinogen, thrombin, calcium chlorid

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Hemostasis.resources/image.10.png]]![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Hemostasis.resources/image.9.png]]

### Floables

* products: FloSeal (gelatin-thrombin sealant)
* uses: parenchyma, vascular, venous sinuses
* composition: gelatin matrix with thrombin
* time to hemostasis: rapid
