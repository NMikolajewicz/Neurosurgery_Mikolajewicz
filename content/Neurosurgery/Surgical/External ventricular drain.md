**External ventricular drains** (**EVDs**)

	common neurosurgical procedure
	
	use
		monitor and treat [raised intracranial pressure](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/raised-intracranial-pressure?lang=us) in the acute setting. 
		
	complications
		infection (1-3%)
		
		malfunction/malposition
		
		intracranial hemorrhage (1%)
		
		seizures
		

Steps for placing EVD

1. Consent
	
2. Preparation and positioning
	
3. Set-up
	
4. Hair and head positioning
	
5. Identification of landmarks
	
6. Identification of trajectory - Aim to the junction of the ipsilateral medial canthus and the ipsilateral EAC
	
7. Preparing the sterile field
	
8. Local anesthetic injection - Anesthetize the pericranium and the subgaleal tunneling tract of the catheter and exit point
	
9. Equipment set-up
	
10. Making the incision
	
11. Burr hole creation
	
12. Durotomy
	
13. Passing the catheter
	

Drainage settings

	drainage set using external auditory meatus (EAM) as reference point
		do not overdrain
		
		clamp EVD while position in OR
		
	settings
		SAH (==unsecured aneurysm==) - ==drain at 10==
		
		SAH (==secured aneurysm==) - ==drain at 0==
		
		==trauma== - "20 pop down to 10"
			monitor ICP
			
			==if ICP > 20, drain at 10 until ICP drops==
			
		==posterior fossa mass== - ==drain at 15-20==
		

CSF output and challenging

	challenging = raising drainage setting wrt EAM
	
	<100 cc per 8 h
		start challenging if >6-7 days post-SAH
		
	<100 c per 24 h
		CT after 24 h - if normal ventricle size, D/C EVD

Approaches

* * *

Frontal approach

	[[Kocher's point]]
		Entry
			10-12 cm in sagittal line posterior to nasion
				1-2 cm anterior to coronal suture
				
			then 3-4 cm laterally
			
		Trajectory
			2 cm anterior to tragus and ipsilateral nasion/glabella/medial canthus
			
		Depth
			6 cm
			

Posterior (occipital) approach

	adults
		4-5 finger widths above and 4-5 posterior to auricle tip
		
		Dandy's point
			3 cm superior to inion and 3 cm off midline
			
			higher risk of visual pathway injury
			
		Frazier burr hole
			6-7 cm superior to inion and 3-4 cm off midline
			
			burr hole is 1 cm anterior to lambdoid suture and allows insertion of catheter down length of body of lateral ventricle.
			
	kids
		3 finger widths above and 3 posterior to auricle tip
		

Transorbital approach

	indicated for herniating kids in ED
		18G 3.5 inch spinal needle
		
		entry: superior medial orbital roof corner
		
		trajectory: towards opposite parietal bossing
