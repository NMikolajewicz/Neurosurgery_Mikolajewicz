## Anterior Lateral

* corridor: middle frontal gyrus

### Transcallosal

* transverse through anterior 30% of corpus callosum

## Atrium/Trigone

### Interhemispheric

* contralateral transfalcine
* ipsilateral
* risks: corpus callosum

![[Neurosurgery/Surgical/_resources/Approaches_Ventricles.resources/image.5.png]]

### Transparietal transcortical

* superior parietal lobule
* risks:
	* dominant: gertsman
	* non-dominant: visual spatial deficits

### Transparietal transsulcal

* interparietal sulcus
