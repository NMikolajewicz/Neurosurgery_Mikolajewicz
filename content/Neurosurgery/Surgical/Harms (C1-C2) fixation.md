> [!exam] **Harms (Goel–Harms) posterior C1–C2 fixation** = **C1 lateral mass screws + C2 pedicle/pars screws + rods** → allows **reduction + fusion**.  
> **Indications:** atlantoaxial instability (RA/trauma), **odontoid nonunion**, **unstable Jefferson** / **TAL disruption**.  
> **Instability imaging:** **ADI >3 mm (adults)** / **>5 mm (kids)**; **Spence** (C1 LM overhang sum) **>6.9 mm** (~**8.1 mm** w/ magnification) ⇒ TAL rupture.  
> **C1 LM pearl:** start near **posterior arch–lateral mass junction**; trajectory ~**10–15° medial**, **15–25° cephalad**; mind venous plexus/**C2 root**.  
> **C2 choice:** **pedicle** (strong) vs **pars**—decide by **pedicle width + VA anatomy** (e.g., **high-riding VA**).  
> **When pedicle unsafe:** consider **C2 translaminar (Wright)** screws (VA-sparing).  
> **Complications:** **vertebral artery injury**, C2 root symptoms (**numbness** if cut vs **occipital neuralgia** if preserved), screw malposition/loosening, nonunion, infection.

## Indications

- **Atlantoaxial (C1/C2) instability**
- **Odontoid nonunion / non-fusion**
- **Unstable Jefferson (C1 burst) fracture**
- **Transverse atlantal ligament (TAL) disruption**

### Imaging thresholds that often support "instability" framing

- **ADI (atlantodental interval)** abnormal if **>3 mm (adults)** or **>5 mm (children)** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4111952/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "A Review of the Diagnosis and Treatment of Atlantoaxial ..."))
- **Rule of Spence / Heller correction**: sum of C1 lateral mass overhang **>6.9 mm** (or **>8.1 mm** with magnification) → suggests **TAL rupture** → **unstable** ([Orthobullets](https://www.orthobullets.com/spine/2015/atlas-fractures-and-transverse-ligament-injuries?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Atlas Fractures & Transverse Ligament Injuries - Spine"))

> [!tip]  
> For "unstable Jefferson," exam answers often cite **TAL disruption** (MRI) and/or **LMD sum >6.9 mm (Spence)**. ([Orthobullets](https://www.orthobullets.com/spine/2015/atlas-fractures-and-transverse-ligament-injuries?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Atlas Fractures & Transverse Ligament Injuries - Spine"))

## Contraindications

- **Vertebral artery (VA) anatomic variation** that makes safe screw placement unreliable
    - Key example: **high-riding vertebral artery (HRVA)** can **preclude safe C2 pedicle screw placement** ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0303846720307460?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 partial transpedicular screw technique for atlantoaxial ..."))

## Key Anatomy and Landmarks

### C1 (Atlas) — Lateral Mass Screw

- Preferred starting zone (classic): **intersection of posterior arch + C1 lateral mass** ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2899538/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Ideal screw entry point and projection angles for posterior ..."))
- Typical trajectories (high-yield ranges)
    - **~10° medial, ~22° cephalad** (commonly cited) ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20075816/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C1 lateral mass anatomy: Proper placement of ..."))
    - Morphometry supports **medial ~13.5° ± 1.9°** and **cephalad ~15.2° ± 2.6°** (with higher maxima) ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2899538/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Ideal screw entry point and projection angles for posterior ..."))
- Planning pearl: **preop 3D CT** helps determine feasibility/route for C1 LM screw ([AO Foundation Surgery Reference](https://surgeryreference.aofoundation.org/spine/trauma/occipitocervical/basic-technique/c1-lateral-mass-screw-insertion?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C1 lateral mass screw insertion"))

### C2 Nerve Root (Exposure Issue)

- The **C2 exiting root** lies close to common C1 LM starting points and often limits exposure ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3503508/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C1-C2 fusion: postoperative C2 nerve impingement—is it a ..."))
- **Transection vs preservation (patient-facing outcomes)**
    - Transection → **↑ occipital numbness**, **no worsening of PROMs/QOL** ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24476905/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 nerve root transection during C1 lateral mass screw ..."))
    - Preservation → can be associated with **occipital neuralgia** with **negative disability/QOL impact** ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24476905/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 nerve root transection during C1 lateral mass screw ..."))

> [!warning]  
> **C2 root management is an exposure trade-off**: cut → numbness; spare → risk occipital neuralgia. ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24476905/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 nerve root transection during C1 lateral mass screw ..."))

### C2 Fixation Options (choose based on VA/pedicle anatomy)

- **C2 pedicle screw** (robust option; VA-risk dependent)
    - Typical trajectory teaching: **20–30° medial**, **parallel/slightly cephalad** in sagittal plane ([3datlasofneurologicalsurgery.org](https://3datlasofneurologicalsurgery.org/approaches/spine/c1-c2-screw-insertion-techniques?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C1-C2 Screw Insertion Techniques"))
- **C2 pars screw** (common alternative when pedicle/VA anatomy limits pedicle screw)
- **C2 translaminar (Wright) screws**: VA-sparing alternative; cited as an established option ([jss.amegroups.org](https://jss.amegroups.org/article/view/4935/html?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Posterior atlantoaxial fusion: a comprehensive review of ..."))

## Core Steps (Posterior Goel–Harms Construct)

- **Position**: prone, rigid head fixation, neutral alignment
- **Exposure**: posterior midline C1–C2; identify **C1 posterior arch**, **C2 lamina/pars/pedicle**, manage venous plexus
- **C2 nerve root**: retract vs **transect** for exposure (see anatomy section) ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24476905/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 nerve root transection during C1 lateral mass screw ..."))
- **Instrumentation sequence**
    - Place **C1 lateral mass screws** (confirmed trajectory) ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20075816/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C1 lateral mass anatomy: Proper placement of ..."))
    - Place **C2 pedicle or pars screws** (consider HRVA) ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0303846720307460?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 partial transpedicular screw technique for atlantoaxial ..."))
    - Connect **rods**, perform **reduction** as needed, tighten set screws
- **Fusion**: decortication + bone graft (posterior elements and/or C1–C2 joint per surgeon preference)

## Outcomes

- Series and reviews commonly report **high fusion rates**, with some reporting **100% fusion** in Harms arthrodesis series ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877056814000371?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Original article C1-C2 stabilization by harms arthrodesis"))

> [!exam]  
> If asked "why Harms vs transarticular?": posterior screw–rod construct is widely adopted, enables reduction, and is often presented as lowering technical/trajectory constraints vs transarticular fixation. ([Springer Link](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-662-64062-3_39 "C1–C2 (Harms) Technique | SpringerLink"))

## Postoperative Care

- **Cervical collar**: commonly **~6 weeks** in published Harms arthrodesis series recommendations ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877056814000371?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Original article C1-C2 stabilization by harms arthrodesis"))
- (Local protocols may use a rigid collar such as Aspen **6–8 weeks**)

## Complications 

|Category|High-yield complications|
|---|---|
|Vascular|**Vertebral artery injury** (risk increased with unfavorable anatomy e.g., **HRVA**) ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0303846720307460?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 partial transpedicular screw technique for atlantoaxial ..."))|
|Neural|**C2 numbness** (esp. if root transected) vs **occipital neuralgia** (if preserved) ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24476905/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "C2 nerve root transection during C1 lateral mass screw ..."))|
|Hardware|Malposition, loosening, nonunion/pseudarthrosis|
|Wound|Infection|
