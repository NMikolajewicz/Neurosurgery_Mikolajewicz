AKA Goel-Harms technique

### Resources

* [Manual of spine surgery](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-662-64062-3_39#:~:text=To%20increase%20the%20fusion%20rate,when%20utilizing%20the%20posterior%20approach.)

### Overview

* C1 lateral mass and C2 pedicular fixation

### Indication

* atlantoxial (C1/C2) instability
* non-fusion of odontoid fractures
* unstable jefferson fracture
* transverse ligament disruption

### Contraindications

* anatomical variation of vertebral artery

### Anatomical considerations

* lateral mass ideal because arch of atlas can be congenitally absent, hypoplastic, cartilagenous or broken.
* C2 nerve root transection is associated with increased occipital numbness but this has no effect on patient-reported outcomes and QOL
	* C2 nerve root preservation can be associated with occipitals neuralgia, which has a negative impact on patient disability and Qol.

### Postoperative care

* Aspen collar for 6-8 weeks

Note the path that the C2 nerve root takes. It is commonly transected during C1 fixation.
