### Background

* Proposed by Harms and Rolinger in 1982
* Alternative to two-step anterior and posterior lumbar interbody fusion
* Allows access to disc space via far lateral approach after facetectomy

### Lumbar Pedicle Screw Entry Points


L1-L4
- Intersection: Vertical line through the lateral aspect of the superior articular facet and a horizontal line through the midline of the transverse process
- Approximately mammary process
L5
- Typically located at the intersection of the transverse process and the lateral aspect of the superior articular facet, similar to L1-L4 but often requires a more lateral starting point due to increased convergence angle.￼

### Indications

* Spondylolisthesis
* Symptomatic degenerative disc disease
* Recurrent lumbar disc herniation with low back pain or radiculopathy
* Pseudoarthrosis
