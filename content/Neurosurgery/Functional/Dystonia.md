## Management
* Oral medications
	* anticholinergics
	* baclofen
	* benzodiazepines
	* Botulinum toxin injections for treatment of focal symptoms can be used in conjunction with oral medications.
* Surgery
	* deep-brain stimulation (DBS) of the globus pallidus interna (GPi)

## GPi-DBS response profiles
**Response profiles**
* inherited dystonia w/o degen (==77%==)
	* DYT1,DYT6, myoclonus-dystonia
* idiopathic dystonia (==51%==)
	* As more genetic mutations linked to dystonia become recognized, patients in this group may potentially be reclassiﬁed as having inherited dystonia without nervous system pathology
* inherited dystonia w/ degen (==27%==)
* acquired dystonia (==11%==)
	* e.g., cerebral palsy
**Predictors of response to Gpi-DBS**
* associated with better response to Gpi-DBS:
	* older age at onset
	* shorter duration of life with dystonia
	* inherited dystonia without degeneration (vs all other groups)
	* truncal involvement
**Status dystonicus**
* Resolution of the crisis was observed in 16/18 patients after GPi-DBS insertion.

### Barry-Albright
Barry Albright Dystonia Scale, which is used to assess dystonia in the eyes, mouth, neck, trunk, and extremities. For each body area, it provides a scale of 0 to 4 to rate the severity of dystonia from absent to severe. A rating of 0 indicates an absence of dystonia, while higher numbers represent increasing levels of dystonia based on the frequency and impact on functioning. The scale provides clinicians with a standardized way to evaluate how much dystonia affects different parts of the body and a patient's daily activities.
