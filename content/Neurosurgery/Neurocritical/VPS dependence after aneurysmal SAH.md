## SAH-VP Score: Predicting VPS Dependence After Aneurysmal SAH With EVD

> [!summary] SAH-VP Score (aSAH → VPS dependence) 
> 
> - **SAH-VP** predicts **need for VPS** after **aSAH** with **EVD** (persistent hydrocephalus).
>     
> - Derivation: **105** EVD-treated aSAH pts (2014–2023); **42.8%** required **VPS**.
>     
> - Strong predictors: **EVD duration ≥14 days** (16d vs 9d median).
>     
> - **EVD infection**: **60%** (VPS) vs **30%** (no VPS).
>     
> - **Failed weaning ≥2** attempts → ↑VPS risk.
>     
> - **Failed closure ≥1** attempt → ↑VPS risk.
>     
> - **WFNS ≥4** on admission associated with VPS dependence.
>     
> - Caveat: **single-center retrospective** model → external validity may vary.
>

### Core concepts

- **SAH-VP score** = predictive model for **VPS requirement** due to **persistent hydrocephalus** after **aSAH**.
- Derived from **retrospective** cohort: **105 aSAH** patients requiring **EVD** (Hospital de Braga, **Jan 2014–Dec 2023**); **42.8%** ultimately needed **VPS**.
- Key predictors: **EVD duration ≥14 days**, **EVD infection**, **failed weaning ≥2**, **failed closure ≥1**, **WFNS ≥4**.
- ICU/practical use: flags high likelihood of **shunt dependence** and helps anticipate longer CSF diversion course.

### Background / Purpose

- Estimate probability of **VPS placement** for persistent hydrocephalus following **aSAH** in patients initially managed with **EVD**.
- Source: [https://pbrainmd.wordpress.com/2025/09/27/sah-vp-score/](https://pbrainmd.wordpress.com/2025/09/27/sah-vp-score/), [https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772529424014206](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772529424014206)

### Study population 

- **N=105** aSAH patients requiring **EVD** (single center).
- Timeframe: **2014–2023**.
- Outcome: **VPS required in 42.8%**.

### Predictors of VPS dependency (SAH-VP components)

- **EVD duration**
    - Median **16 days** (VPS) vs **9 days** (no VPS)
    - Threshold: **EVD ≥14 days** (significant)
- **EVD infection**
    - **60%** (VPS) vs **30%** (no VPS), **p = 0.002**
- **Failed weaning attempts**
    - **≥2 attempts** significantly predicts VPS, **p = 0.003**
- **Failed closure attempts**
    - **≥1 failed closure** significantly predicts VPS, **p = 0.002**
- **WFNS (admission)**
    - Median **4** (VPS) vs **3** (no VPS), **p = 0.045**
    - Threshold: **WFNS ≥4**

> [!question] Exam triggers (what they'll ask)
> 
> - "aSAH + EVD + repeated wean/closure failures" → high risk **shunt-dependent hydrocephalus**.
> 
> - "Which variables predict VPS?" → **EVD ≥14d**, **infection**, **wean failures ≥2**, **closure failure ≥1**, **WFNS ≥4**.
>  

### Clinical interpretation / Use

- Rising SAH-VP risk profile suggests:
    - anticipate longer CSF diversion needs,
    - plan earlier multidisciplinary discussion re: **VPS candidacy** (institution-dependent),
    - heightened vigilance for **EVD infection prevention/detection**.

> [!warning] Pitfalls / complications
> 
> - **EVD infection** is both a complication and a predictor; prevention and early detection matter.
> 
> - Model is **retrospective + single-center**; external validity may vary. ⚠️ Uncertain / institution-specific
>  

> [!summary] Summary
> 
> - **Shunt risk ↑** with: **EVD ≥14d**, **EVD infection**, **wean failures ≥2**, **closure failure ≥1**, **WFNS ≥4**.
> 
> - In the derivation cohort, **~43%** required **VPS**.

> [!note] Supplement (Greenberg)
> 
> - **Chronic hydrocephalus after aSAH**: chronic HCP due to **pia-arachnoid adhesions** / **permanent impairment of arachnoid granulations**; **acute HCP does not inevitably** progress to chronic HCP.
> 
> - Reported **permanent CSF diversion** needs: **8–45%** of ruptured aneurysm patients and **≈50%** of those with **acute HCP** after SAH require permanent diversion.
> 
> - Guideline snippet: **permanent CSF diversion (shunt)** for **symptomatic chronic hydrocephalus** following aSAH; **weaning an EVD over >24 h** does **not** reduce need for permanent diversion.
> 
> - Ventriculostomy nuance: when using an EVD in aSAH, one paradigm targets **ICP 15–25 mmHg** and avoids **rapid pressure reduction** (rebleed concern).
>
