> [!summary] EVD Tract Hemorrhage
> 
> - Key risk factors: platelets <120; WBC <11 ; IPH presence 
> - 14% (1.5–41%) of EVDs placed have tract hemorrhage

## Risk factors 

- lower platelets, lower WBC counts, and IPH are risk factors for EVD tract hemorrhage
	- platelet counts < 120 × 10³/µL 
		- (==OR = 4.47==, 95% CI = 1.01-20, _p_ = 0.048)
	- WBC counts < 11 × 10³/µL 
		- (==OR = 3.3==, 95% CI = 1.01–10.7, _p_ = 0.048)
	- IPH 
		- (==OR = 2.97==, 95% CI = 1.06–10.7, _p_ = 0.048)
-  EVD tract hemorrhage was not associated with mortality (_p_ = 0.8)
-  no correlation between the number of EVD attempts and tract hemorrhage
