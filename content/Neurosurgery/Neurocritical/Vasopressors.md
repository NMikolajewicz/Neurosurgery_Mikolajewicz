### PEARLS
- Target MAP ≥65 mmHg; ensure adequate volume first.
- Prefer central access for all continuous vasopressors.
- treat extravasation with ==phentolamine==.
- In septic shock: NE first-line → add vasopressin 0.03 U/min if escalating NE; consider epi if still refractory.
- Avoid dopamine in sepsis due to arrhythmias; ==reserve for select bradycardic hypotension==.
