### Workup
* Lumbar puncture - CSF collection
	* three-tube test
		* collect bloody CSF in atleast 3 tubes
		* centrifuge blood CSF immediately (< 1h)
			* compare supernatant to tap water (to r/o xanthochromia)
	* traumatic tap
		* CSF clears with sequential tubes
		* no xanthochromia
		* presence of clot favors traumatic tap
		* repeat puncture at higher interspace yields clear CSF
	* SAH
		* CSF does not clear with sequential tubes
		* xanthochromia
			* if bleed >2-4 h ago
		* no clotting
			* blood defibrinated at site of hemorrhage
* CSF evaluation
	* direct observation of color, viscosity, turbidity
	* cell count/differential
	* gram stain and culture
		* larger volumes (>10 mL) improve changes of detecting pathogen
		* gram stain always performed if CSF WBC elevated
		* adjuncts
			* PCR for viruses
			* antigen tests
				* bacterial antigens persist several days after Abx therapy
	* glucose
	* protein

_if cell count, protein and glucose normal, unlikely that other studies will add value._
#### Lumbar puncture
* suspected meningitis overrides all contraindications
* approach
	* 24G needle, IV mannitol 1 g/kg (20 min pre-LP), dexamethasone
	* spinal needle with stylet
		* avoids introducing epidermal cells (==iatrogenic epidermoid tumor==)
	* collect 4 tubes
		* 5 ml each
* contraindications
	* puncture above L3
* complications
	* herniation - transtentorial/cerebellar tonsillar
		* inject 5 mL NS
		* withdraw needle immediately
		* intubate and hyperventilate
		* mannitol
		* steroids
### CSF
* within BBB
* communicates with brain interstitial fluid
* buoyancy
	* reduce in situ weight of brain to ~50 g
	* CSF removal during LP -> increased brain weight -> tension on arachnoid trabeculae, nerve roots and BV -> headache
* total ==CSF volume: 150 mL==
	* 7.5-70.5 mL in ventricles
	* production = ICP independent
	* reabsorption = ICP dependent
* CSF production
	* rate: 500 mL/day; ==20 mL/hour==
	* CSF production is independent of ICP
	* CSF removed by LP is regenerated in 1 hour
	* acetazolamide (carbonic anhydrase inhibitor)
		* reduces CSF production
		* ==acetazolamide ~ ↓CSF==
	* regulation of production
		* adrenergic stimulation (e.g., NE) reduces CSF production
			* ==adrenergic ~ ↓CSF==
		* cholinergic stimulation increases CSF production
			* ==cholinergic ~ ↑CSF==
* arachnoid villi
	* one-way valve
	* prevent blood reflex into CSF
	* open at ΔP = 5 mmHg
		* CSF reabsorption ceases at ICP < 5 mmHg
		* CSF absorption proportionate to ICP and dural venous pressure
			* production is independent of ICP
### Choroid plexus
* present in
	* 4th ventricle ependymal roof
		* blood supply: PICA
	* 3rd ventricle ependymal roof
		* blood supply: PCA branches
	* lateral ventricles medial wall
		* main mass
		* blood supply: anterior/posterior choroidal arteries
### CSF characteristics
#### * color
	* xanthochroma
		* yellowish = increased protein (>100-200 mg/dL)
		* yellow/pink = hemoglobin
			* oxyhemoglobin = RBC lysis
				* disappears over 7-14 days
			* bilirubin
				* leptomeningeal cell product
				* persists 2-4 weeks
			* methemoglobin
				* produced in old hematomas
		* yellow = jaundice, rifampin
		* brown/grey = CNS melanoma
		* green = leukemic meningeal infiltration, pseudomonal meningitis
#### * opening pressure
	* normal = 65-mmH2O (==5-15 mmHg==) with patient laying down
		* 50 mmH2O in neonates
		* 250 mmH2O in extremely obese
	* falsely elevated pressures
		* obesity
		* tense patient (crying child)
		* head elevated above plane of needle
			* i.e., artificially elevated with patient in sitting popsition
#### * cellular components
	* ==normal = few cells (<5/mm3)==
		* newborns have up to 19 lymphocytes/mm3
	* abnormal = polymorphonuclear cells, RBCs
	* general rule
		* ==\>100 WBC = infectious==
		* <100 WBC = noninfectious
	* cytopathology requires large CSF volume (>20 mL)
#### * protein
	* normal = <60 mg/dL (mainly ALB)
		* newborns up to 150-170 mg/dL (leaky BBB)
	* CSF ALB : serum ALB ratio = 1:200
	* itrathecal protein (origin)
		* IgG: CNS lymphocytes
		* transthyretin: choroid plexus
		* GFAP/tau/MBP: brain tissue
	* Froin's syndrome
		* loculation syndrome
		* yellow CSF coagulates spontaneously
			* due to elevated protein
	* itrathecal IgG synthesis
		* MS diagnosis supported by:
			* IgG index
			* oligoclonal bands
#### * glucose
	* normal = >60% plasma glucose (50-100 mg/dL)
		* <50% (40-45 mg/dL) = abnormal
		* <40% (40 mg/dL) = anaerobic glycolysis + PMB leukocytes = ↑CSF lactate
	* glucose in CSF is proportionate to plasma glucose
	* 30 min - 4 hour lag between plasma and CSF changes
* Ions: salt + Mg
	* Na/Cl/Mg: same/greater than serum
	* K/Ca/HCO3/PO4: lower than serum
* acid-base status
	* acidic due to CO2
		* higher pCO2 than blood
		* same HCO3 as blood
