> [!summary] Cerebral Salt Wasting (CSW) 
> 
> - **CSW = renal natriuresis** → Na loss + obligate water loss → **hypovolemic hyponatremia**.
>     
> - Classically after **SAH** (also other intracranial insults).
>     
> - Key bedside clues: **low plasma volume**, **↓daily weights**, **low CVP**, **↑Hct** (volume contraction).
>     
> - Labs: **hyponatremia** with **concentrated urine** (both CSW & SIADH can have concentrated urine).
>     
> - Differentiate from **SIADH**: **CSW = low CVP + negative fluid balance**; **SIADH = water retention**.
>     
> - Tx: **replace volume + salt** with **isotonic or hypertonic IV fluids** (goal **positive salt balance**).
>     
> - Course can be prolonged; add **salt tablets** once PO intake resumes.
>     
> - Remember **pseudohyponatremia** is a separate, less common mimic.
>

### Cerebral Salt Wasting
* CSW is inappropriate natriuresis (urinary loss of Na)
	* results in water loss due to excretion of H2O following the osmotic gradient and ultimately a ==loss of intravascular volume.==
* The volume loss in CSW is accompanied by the “wasting of salt,” so intravascular concentration of Na also drops.
* Causes
	* SAH
* Signs and symptoms: signs of fluid depletion
	* plasma volume is low
	* daily weights will be down trending
	* CVP and other estimations of intravascular volume will be low
	* hematocrit will be high (due to volume contraction).
* less common entity known as pseudo hyponatremia may be seen, which results from various osmotically active ions drawing water from cells, thereby lowering the Na concentration.
* Managment
	* fluid volume must be replaced with ==isotonic or even hypertonic IV fluids==
		* Goal: restore positive salt balance.
	* Treatment may be prolonged and is sometimes ==supplemented with salt tablet==s once oral intake is reestablished.
### CSW vs SIADH
* Differentiate using:
	* Central venous pressure (CVP)
	* Fluid intake/output over past 18 hr
* CSW = low CVP with negative fluid balance
* Both CSW and SIADH result in concentrated urine
	* Since ==SIADH retains water==, the urine will be concentrated.
	* Since ==CSW loses/wastes salt,== the urine will be concentrated.
