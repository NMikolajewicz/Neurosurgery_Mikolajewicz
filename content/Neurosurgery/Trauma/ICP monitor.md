## SIBICC Severe TBI Algorithms

Basic and advanced management algorithms for patients with intracranial pressure – and brain oxygen monitoring
<https://globalneuro.org/EN/resources/sibicc-stbi-algorithm.html>

The SIBICC three-tier ICP management algorithm for severe TBI organizes escalating interventions as follows, with each step to be considered in sequence if intracranial hypertension remains uncontrolled

### Tier 1 Interventions (1L)

* Head of bed elevation (30 degrees)
* Basic sedation and analgesia
* Maintain normothermia
* CSF drainage (if EVD present)
* Consider mild hyperventilation
	* PaCO₂ 32–35 mmHg
	* short-term use
* Hyperosmolar therapy (mannitol or hypertonic saline)
* Blood pressure optimization for cerebral perfusion (CPP)
* Normovolemia/euvolemia
* Avoidance of hypoxia/hyperoxia
* Correction of metabolic derangements

### Tier 2 Interventions (2L,  Refractory to Tier 1)

* Neuromuscular blockade
* Moderate hyperventilation
	* PaCO₂ 30–32 mmHg
	* limit to short-term use
* Repeat neuroimaging to rule out new mass lesion or hydrocephalus
* Induction of mild hypothermia (targeted temperature management)

### Tier 3 Interventions (3L, Highest-Risk)

* High-dose barbiturate coma
* Decompressive craniectomy
* Therapeutic cooling (deep hypothermia, highly selective and controversial)

### O2 thresholds

* <15 mm Hg → intervention threshold.
* <10 mm Hg sustained → strongly associated with poor prognosis.
* Target >25 mm Hg in TBI protocols.
