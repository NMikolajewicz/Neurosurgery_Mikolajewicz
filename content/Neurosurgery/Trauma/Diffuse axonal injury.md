### Classifications
* **stage 1** (lobar)
	* lobar white matter, especially ==grey-white matter junction==
* **stage 2** (callosal)
	* ==corpus callosum==, almost invariably in addition to the lobar white matter
* **stage 3** (brainstem)
	* ==brainstem==, almost invariably in addition to the lobar white matter and corpus callosum
### MR appearance of DAI
- multiple, small, elliptical "lesions" located in white matter.
- Small petechial hemorrhages, which appear hypointense on T2
### Prognosis (unfavorable outcomes)
- Grade 1: 7%
- Grade 2: 40%
- Grade 3: 63%
