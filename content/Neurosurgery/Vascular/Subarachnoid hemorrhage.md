### Risk

* family history
* [hypertension](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/hypertension?lang=us)
* heavy alcohol consumption
* abnormal connective tissue
	* [autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/autosomal-dominant-polycystic-kidney-disease-1?lang=us)
	* [Ehlers-Danlos disease type IV](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/vascular-ehlers-danlos-syndrome-1?lang=us)
	* [Marfan syndrome](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/marfan-syndrome?lang=us)
	* [neurofibromatosis type 1](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/neurofibromatosis-type-1?lang=us)
* female gender: ~1.5x baseline risk
* African descent: ~2x baseline risk
* Japanese or Finnish descent

### Management

* ==CTA / diagnostic angiography==
	* evaluate for cerebral aneurysm
* ventriculostomy
	* if hydrocephalus
	* limited drainage untill aneurysm secured
* high blood pressure
	* antihypertensives
		* reduce organ damage
		* reduce rehemorrhage
* vasospasms
	* nimodipine
		* improves neurological outcomes
	* maintain euvolemia
		* prevent cerebral ischemia
	* transcranial doppler
	* ==CT/MR perfusion==
		* to identify potential ischemic areas
	* cerebral angioplasty +/- selective intra-arterial vasodilator
		* reasonable if symptomatic vasospasm
* document clinical severity
	* ==Hunt-Hess==
		* classifies severity of SAH to predict mortality.
	* ==WFNS==
		* uses the [Glasgow Coma Scale](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/glasgow-coma-scale-1?lang=us) and the presence of focal neurological deficits to grade the clinical severity of [subarachnoid hemorrhag](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/subarachnoid-haemorrhage?lang=us)
	* for prognostication

### Complications

* elevated intracranial pressure
	* often require ICP monitoring
		* serial non-invasive screening is possible in equivocal cases using sonographic indices of [elevated ICP](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/raised-intracranial-pressure?lang=us)
	* [hydrocephalus](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/obstructive-hydrocephalus?lang=us) may require ==extraventricular drain placement==
* [cerebral vasospasm](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/cerebral-vasospasm-following-subarachnoid-haemorrhage?lang=us) causing [delayed cerebral ischemia](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/delayed-cerebral-ischaemia?lang=us)
	* ==triple H therapy== (**H**aemodilution, **H**ypertension, **H**ypervolaemia)
	* calcium channel blockers (e.g. ==nimodipine==)
	* endovascular intervention (e.g. intra-arterial delivery of vasodilating agents (such as NO) and/or balloon angioplasty)
* [hyponatremia](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/missing?article%5Btitle%5D=hyponatraemia&lang=us)
* [coronary spasm](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/missing?article%5Btitle%5D=coronary-spasm&lang=us)
* neurogenic [pulmonary edema](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/pulmonary-oedema?lang=us)
* [pulseless electrical activity (PEA)](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/pulseless-electrical-activity?lang=us) 

### Modified Fisher Scale

* **grade 0**
	* no subarachnoid hemorrhage (SAH)
	* no intraventricular hemorrhage (IVH)
	* incidence of symptomatic vasospasm: 0% 3
* **grade 1**
	* focal or diffuse, thin SAH
	* no IVH
	* the incidence of symptomatic vasospasm: 24%
* **grade 2**
	* focal or diffuse, thin SAH
	* IVH present
	* the incidence of symptomatic vasospasm: 33%
* **grade 3**
	* thick SAH
	* no IVH
	* the incidence of symptomatic vasospasm: 33%
* **grade 4**
	* thick  SAH
	* IVH present
	* the incidence of symptomatic vasospasm: 40%
