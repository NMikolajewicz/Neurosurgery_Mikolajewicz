### BCVI Screening: Denver criteria

* The **Denver criteria** are a set of screening criteria used to determine when CT angiography of the neck is indicated to detect [blunt cerebrovascular injury (BCVI)](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/blunt-cerebrovascular-injury?lang=us) in patients presenting after trauma. 
* i.e., ==used to decide whether CTA is required for BCVI assessment==

<https://radiopaedia.org/articles/denver-criteria-for-blunt-cerebrovascular-injury-1>
Denver criteria aim: identify patient that require CTA, i.e., _i)_ symptomatic or _ii)_ asymptomatic but high risk patients

### Management

* Grade I: Aspirin 325 mg PO daily (81 mg if risk of bleeding)
* Grade II-IV
	* Symptomatic: 
		* Aspirin 325 mg PO daily
		* consideration for surgical stent (Grade IV > II/III due to risk of stroke)
	* Asymptomatic:  same as symptomatic
* Grade V: surgical intervention
* _For all BCVIs, get_ ==_repeat CTA at 7 days to re-evaluate_==_. Continue anti-platelet therapy if persistent BCVI untill follow up._
