> [!summary] Summary
> 
> - **Location clues etiology**: **deep** = **HTN microangiopathy**; **lobar** = **CAA** or structural lesion (**AVM/cavernoma/aneurysm/moyamoya**).
>     
> - **Common sites**: **basal ganglia 50%**, **thalamus 15%**, **pons 15%**, **cerebellum 10%**, **WM 10%**, **brainstem 5%**.
>     
> - **Hematoma expansion is early**: mostly **0–6 h**; **rare after 24 h** (0–3 h ~36%).
>     
> - **High-risk expansion**: **prior anticoagulant use (OR ~6.2)** + **CTA spot sign** (sens ~53%, spec ~88%).
>     
> - **ICH Score (0–6)** predicts **30-day mortality**: **GCS**, **volume >30 mL**, **IVH**, **infratentorial**, **age >80**.
>     
> - **ICH score mortality**: **0:0%**, **1:13%**, **2:26%**, **3:72%**, **4:97%**, **5–6: ~100%**.
>     
> - **Key management**: treat **HTN** (target ~**140/90**, avoid hypotension); **EVD** for **IVH + obstructive hydrocephalus**.
>     
> - **Surgical prognosis flags**: **>60 cc + GCS ≤8 → ~91% 30-day mortality**; **>85 cc → no survivors** (one series).
>

### Core concepts

- **Etiology often inferred by location**: **deep** (HTN microangiopathy) vs **lobar** (CAA/structural lesions).
- **Early hematoma expansion** is a major driver of deterioration and is largely a **first-hours** phenomenon.
- **CTA spot sign** + **prior anticoagulant use** = high-risk expansion phenotype.
- **ICH Score (0–6)** predicts **30-day mortality** using **GCS, volume, IVH, infratentorial origin, age**.

### Causes

- **Hypertension microangiopathy**
    - deep brain structures
    - structural and mechanical age-related changes to arterial walls
- **Cerebral amyloid angiopathy**
    - lobar regions
    - amyloid deposits weaken vascular integrity
- [[Arteriovenous malformations]]
    - abnormal connections between arterial and venous vasculature
- **Cavernous angioma**
    - neurovascular disorder
    - dilated leaky capillaries following dysfunctional endothelium
- [[Moyamoya disease]]
    - rare condition
    - vascular constriction within arteries at the circle of willis
    - results in abnormal vessel formation compensating for blockage of normal blood flow
- [[Aneurysms]]
    - dilations of blood vessels
    - causes by weakening of parent artery angioarchitecture

### ICH by location

- Thalamus (15%)

- Cerebellum (10%)

### ICH score

- Predicts **30-day mortality** following ICH
- Range **0–6**
- Components
    - **GCS**
    - **ICH volume (>30 mL)**
    - **IVH**
    - **Infratentorial origin**
    - **Age (>80 y)**

> [!note] ICH Score summary
> 
> - **Pointing (ICH Score)**: **GCS 3–4 = 2**, **GCS 5–12 = 1**, **GCS 13–15 = 0**; **Age ≥80 = 1**; **Infratentorial = 1**; **Volume ≥30 cc = 1**; **IVH present = 1**
> 
> - **30-day mortality by score**: **0: 0%**, **1: 13%**, **2: 26%**, **3: 72%**, **4: 97%**, **5: 100%**, **6: ?100%**
>  
### Hematoma expansion (early hazard)

- Expansion probabilities by time from onset:
    - **0–3h ~36%**
    - **3–6h ~16%**
    - **6–12h ~15%**
    - **12–24h ~6%**
    - **24–48h ~0%** (rare after 24h)
- If presenting within 3h: **~83%** of expansions occur within **6h**; **~100%** within **24h**.
- **Prior anticoagulant use** increases expansion risk (**OR ~6.2**).
- **CTA spot sign** predicts expansion: **sensitivity ~53%**, **specificity ~88%**; very large association reported (**OR ~92** for significant expansion).

> [!question] High yield
> 
> - "**When** does expansion happen?" → highest risk **0–6h**, rare after **24h**.
> 
> - "What imaging sign predicts growth?" → **CTA spot sign**.
> 
> - "Which patients are highest risk?" → **anticoagulants**, very early presenters.
>  

### Management (high-yield frame)

- Treat HTN; suggested target BP ≈ 140/90; avoid overcorrection → hypotension
- EVD (ventriculostomy): for IVH causing acute obstructive hydrocephalus (e.g., obstruction at 3rd ventricular outlet)
- Catheter strategy in IVH: often place in lateral ventricle contralateral to hemorrhage to avoid immediate clot obstruction; patency issues common; tPA may help
- Intraventricular rt-PA (selected cases): 2–5 mg via ventricular catheter; avoid until bleeding source addressed if aneurysm/AVM suspected
- Surgical prognosis flags: >60 cc + GCS ≤8 → ~91% 30-day mortality; >85 cc → no survivors in one series
- Posterior fossa deterioration: suboccipital craniectomy may be indicated for progressive neuro decline from brainstem compression (classically cited for cerebellar hemorrhage/infarction)

> [!summary] Rapid recall
> 
> - **Deep** ICH → think **HTN microangiopathy**. **Lobar** ICH → think **CAA/AVM/cavernoma**.
> 
> - **Expansion**: mostly **first 6h**, rare after **24h**; ↑ with **anticoagulants** + **spot sign**.
> 
> - **ICH score**: **GCS + volume + IVH + infratentorial + age** → 30-day mortality estimate.
