## EVT for Acute Ischemic Stroke 

> [!exam] **Summaryt**
> 
> - **EVT = standard of care** for eligible **large vessel occlusion ischemic stroke**
> 
> - **Best candidates:** disabling deficit + proximal anterior circulation LVO + salvageable tissue
> 
> - **Classic "GO" criteria:** **ASPECTS ≥6**, **NIHSS ≥6**, **ICA/M1 ± proximal M2 occlusion**, good collaterals, penumbra
> 
> - **Modern update:** **ASPECTS <6 is no longer an automatic "NO GO"** in selected patients
> 
> - **Time matters:** faster puncture → faster reperfusion → better outcome
> 
> - **Goal:** **TICI 2b/2c/3 reperfusion**
> 
> - **Do not delay IV thrombolysis** if eligible, but **do not delay EVT** waiting for thrombolysis response
>

## 1. One-Liner

**Endovascular thrombectomy** is catheter-based clot retrieval for acute ischemic stroke due to **large vessel occlusion**, aiming to rapidly restore perfusion to ischemic but salvageable brain.

---

## 2. Decision Algorithm

```text
Suspected acute ischemic stroke
        ↓
NCCT head
        ↓
Exclude hemorrhage
        ↓
CTA head/neck
        ↓
Is there LVO?
        ↓
Yes → assess:
    - Deficit severity
    - Pre-stroke function
    - Time last known well
    - ASPECTS/core
    - Collaterals
    - Perfusion mismatch if late/uncertain window
        ↓
Eligible → IVT if appropriate + EVT
```

---

## 3. "GO" Criteria for EVT

|Domain|High-yield EVT-favourable features|
|---|---|
|**Clinical deficit**|Disabling stroke; commonly **NIHSS ≥6**|
|**Occlusion**|**ICA terminus**, **M1**, selected **M2**, basilar artery|
|**Imaging core**|Traditionally **ASPECTS ≥6**|
|**Collateral status**|Good/moderate collaterals on CTA|
|**Perfusion**|**Mismatch**: small core, larger penumbra|
|**Function**|Pre-stroke **mRS 0–2** usually strongest evidence base|
|**Timing**|0–6 h strongest; selected patients 6–24 h|

---

## 4. "NO GO / Caution" Features

|Feature|Interpretation|
|---|---|
|**No LVO**|No thrombectomy target|
|**Large established infarct**|Higher hemorrhage/edema risk; benefit depends on selection|
|**Poor baseline function**|Less likely to return to independence|
|**Non-disabling deficit**|Risk-benefit less favourable|
|**Poor collaterals**|Faster core growth|
|**Matched perfusion defect**|Mostly completed infarct, little penumbra|
|**Very distal occlusion**|Evidence evolving; technical/risk considerations|

---

## 5. Important Modern Update: Large-Core Stroke

The slide says **ASPECTS <6 = NO GO**, but that is now too rigid.

Recent randomized trials and AHA science advisory evidence support EVT benefit in selected **large-core ischemic stroke** patients, especially those with anterior circulation LVO, meaningful baseline function, and significant clinical deficit. The AHA advisory summarizes six trials and states that they provide strong evidence of EVT benefit in patients with good pre-stroke function and large ischemic core on initial imaging. ([ahajournals.org](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/STR.0000000000000481?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Large-Core Ischemic Stroke Endovascular Treatment"))

### Practical exam phrasing

|Old teaching|Updated teaching|
|---|---|
|**ASPECTS <6 = no EVT**|**ASPECTS <6 = consider carefully; not automatic exclusion**|
|Large core = futile|Selected large-core patients may benefit|
|Perfusion mismatch required for all late cases|Late-window selection often uses clinical-core mismatch/perfusion criteria, but practice is evolving|

---

## 6. Time Windows

|Window|EVT approach|
|---|---|
|**0–6 h**|EVT for eligible anterior circulation LVO based on NCCT + CTA|
|**6–24 h**|EVT for selected patients using advanced imaging or clinical-core mismatch|
|**>24 h**|Not routine; individualized/select cases|
|**Unknown onset / wake-up stroke**|Imaging-based selection|

## 7. Key Trials to Know

|Trial|High-yield point|
|---|---|
|**MR CLEAN**|First major positive EVT trial; established benefit in anterior circulation LVO|
|**ESCAPE**|Emphasized fast workflow, small core, good collaterals|
|**REVASCAT**|Confirmed EVT benefit with modern devices|
|**SWIFT PRIME**|EVT + medical therapy superior to medical therapy|
|**EXTEND-IA**|Perfusion-selected patients; strong reperfusion/clinical benefit|
|**DAWN**|6–24 h; clinical-core mismatch|
|**DEFUSE 3**|6–16 h; perfusion mismatch|
|**RESCUE-Japan LIMIT**|Large core / low ASPECTS signal of benefit|
|**SELECT2**|Large-core anterior circulation LVO; EVT benefit|
|**ANGEL-ASPECT**|Large infarct core; EVT improved outcomes|
|**TENSION**|Low ASPECTS 3–5; EVT benefit in selected patients|

## 8. Imaging Essentials

### NCCT

|Finding|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Hemorrhage|Contraindicates thrombolysis; changes pathway|
|Hyperdense MCA|Clot sign|
|Loss of gray-white differentiation|Early ischemic change|
|Sulcal effacement|Edema/early infarction|
|ASPECTS|Estimates early ischemic burden|

### CTA

|Finding|Meaning|
|---|---|
|ICA/M1/M2/basilar occlusion|EVT target|
|Tandem cervical ICA lesion|May require angioplasty/stenting strategy|
|Collaterals|Predictor of infarct growth and outcome|
|Aortic arch/access|Procedural planning|

### CTP / MRI perfusion

|Pattern|Interpretation|
|---|---|
|Small core + large penumbra|Best EVT physiology|
|Large core + mismatch|Possible EVT candidate depending on context|
|Matched core/perfusion defect|Less salvageable tissue|

## 9. ASPECTS Quick Reference

**ASPECTS = 10-point CT score for MCA territory ischemia**

|Region|Structures|
|---|---|
|Deep|Caudate, lentiform, internal capsule, insula|
|Cortical|M1–M6 MCA cortical regions|

```text
Start at 10
Subtract 1 point for each ischemic region
Higher = smaller core
Lower = larger core
```

|ASPECTS|Interpretation|
|---|---|
|**8–10**|Small core|
|**6–7**|Moderate early ischemic change|
|**3–5**|Large core but now potentially EVT-eligible in selected patients|
|**0–2**|Very large core; benefit uncertain/high risk|

## 10. EVT Targets

|Occlusion|EVT status|
|---|---|
|**ICA terminus**|Strong indication|
|**M1 MCA**|Strong indication|
|**Proximal M2**|Often treated if disabling deficit|
|**Distal M2/M3**|Selective; evidence evolving|
|**Basilar artery**|Treat aggressively if appropriate; posterior circulation emergency|
|**ACA/PCA distal occlusions**|Case-by-case|

---

## 11. Procedural Concepts

|Concept|Key point|
|---|---|
|**Access**|Usually common femoral artery; radial/brachial in select cases|
|**Techniques**|Stent retriever, aspiration, combined technique|
|**Reperfusion score**|**mTICI 2b–3** = successful reperfusion|
|**First-pass effect**|Complete/near-complete reperfusion on first attempt; associated with better outcomes|
|**Complications**|sICH, vessel perforation/dissection, emboli to new territory, access-site complication|

---

## 12. Post-EVT Management

|Issue|High-yield management|
|---|---|
|**BP**|Avoid hypertension after reperfusion; target depends on local protocol/reperfusion status|
|**Neuro checks**|Frequent monitoring for edema, hemorrhagic transformation, re-occlusion|
|**Imaging**|Follow-up CT/MRI, especially before antithrombotics if thrombolysis given|
|**Antithrombotics**|Usually delayed after IVT until follow-up imaging excludes hemorrhage|
|**Etiology workup**|AF, carotid disease, dissection, cardioembolic source, hypercoagulability when appropriate|
|**Malignant edema**|Watch large MCA infarcts; consider decompressive hemicraniectomy if indicated|

---

## 13. Exam-Style "EVT Candidate" Summary

```text
EVT candidate =
    Disabling ischemic stroke
    +
    Proven LVO on CTA/MRA
    +
    No hemorrhage
    +
    Salvageable brain / acceptable core
    +
    Reasonable baseline function
    +
    Treatment can be delivered rapidly
```

## 14. Common Oral Exam Pitfalls

|Pitfall|Better answer|
|---|---|
|"ASPECTS <6 means never treat"|"Large core is a caution, but recent trials support EVT in selected low-ASPECTS patients."|
|"Wait to see if tPA works"|"Do not delay EVT for IVT response."|
|"NIHSS <6 means no EVT"|"Low NIHSS with disabling deficit or proximal LVO may still be considered."|
|"No perfusion scan means no EVT"|"In early window, NCCT + CTA is often enough."|
|"Successful EVT means TICI 2a"|"Aim for **TICI 2b/2c/3**."|
