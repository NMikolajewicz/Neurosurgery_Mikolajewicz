### General notes

* Never clip a basilar aneurysm - asking for trouble!
* Cavernous sinus aneurysms, lowest rupture rates
* growth of aneurysm at follow up associated with increased rupture risk
* almost 30% of unruptured aneuryms in individuals of working age ruptured during lifelong follow up

### Epidemiology

* 3-5% aneurysm prevalence in general population
* women 2:1
* incidence of SAH: 14/100,000
* most aneurysms don't rupture

### Ruptured aneurysms (SAH)

* overall mortality: 32%
* 10% die before reaching hospital

### Morbidity post-aneurysmal SAH

* modified Rankin score >2: 33% survivors
* 65% have residual deficits even 20 years post SAH
* complete recovery: 5%
* PTSD: 25%
* quality of life determined by emotional state > physical impairment
* young patients: 60% return to work

### Aneurysm morphology

* **Saccular**
	* most common
	* pouch-like bulge on side of artery wall.
* **Fusiform**
	* uniform artery dilation.
	* often affects an extended segment.
* **Dissecting**
	* tear in the artery wall leading to bleeding between layers of blood vessel.
* **Mycotic**
	* result from the weakening of the arterial wall due to microbial invasion.
* **Traumatic**
	* a result of head injuries or trauma to the blood vessels in the brain.
* **Giant**
	* exceeding 2.5 cm in diameter.
* **Blister**

* several limitations: no info on smoking, family Hx, drug/alcohol

	* Hypertension

### Imaging

* MRA with vessel wall imaging
	* used to resolve which aneurysm rupture
	* theory is that ruptured aneurysm is associated with inflammation, associated with peri-aneurysmal enhancement
		

### Triggers for aneurysm rupture

* <https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21546472/>
* Eight triggers increased the risk for subarachnoid hemorrhage:
	* coffee consumption (RR, 1.7; 95% CI, 1.2-2.4)
	* cola consumption (RR, 3.4; 95% CI,1.5-7.9)
	* anger (RR, 6.3; 95% CI, 4.6-25)
	* startling (RR, 23.3; 95% CI, 4.2-128)
	* straining for defecation (RR, 7.3; 95% CI, 2.9-19)
	* sexual intercourse (RR, 11.2; 95% CI, 5.3-24)
	* nose blowing (RR, 2.4; 95% CI, 1.3-4.5)
	* vigorous physical exercise (RR, 2.4; 95% CI, 1.2-4.2).
* The highest population-attributable risks were found for coffee consumption (10.6%) and vigorous physical exercise (7.9%).

### Management

* Conservative
	* 75% managed conservatively
		* imaging follow up
		* Intial follow ups: q6-12 months x3
		* later follow ups: q3-5 years
	* familial aneurysms (7-20%)
		* 2 first-degree family members
		* screening q7 years
	* counseling: life style management and risk reduction
* Surgical
	* clipping
	* bypass 2-3% of MCA aneurysms for flow diversion
