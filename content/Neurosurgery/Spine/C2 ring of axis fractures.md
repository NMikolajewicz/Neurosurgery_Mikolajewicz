> [!summary] **Hangman Fracture Review**  
> • Hangman = **bilateral C2 pars fractures** from **hyperextension + axial load** (MVC, diving).  
> • **<u>Effendi / Levine–Edwards</u>:**  
> – **Type I:** <3 mm translation, minimal angulation → **stable**, collar x6 wks.  
> – **Type II:** >3 mm translation + angulation from **C2–3 disc disruption** → immobilize x12 wks; fuse if persistent instability.  
> – **Type IIa:** flexion–distraction, severe angulation (>11%) with little translation → **avoid traction**, posterior C2–3 fusion.  
> – **Type III:** bilateral C2–3 facet dislocation → **surgical**, typically anterior C2–3 fusion.  
> • **Instability driver:** C2–3 disc injury.  
> • Key point: traction is **contraindicated in Type IIa** due to worsening angulation.

## **1. Effendi / Levine–Edwards Classification**

### **Type I — Stable Hangman Fracture**
- **~65% incidence**
- Mechanism: ==**hyperextension + axial load**==
- ==**<3 mm translation**==, **minimal/no angulation**
- Stable on flexion–extension films
- **Management:** rigid collar x **6 weeks**
---
### **Type II — Unstable Hangman Fracture**
- **~28% incidence**
- Instability due to **C2–3 disc disruption**
- ==**>3 mm translation**== and **significant angulation**
- **Management:** rigid immobilization x **12 weeks**
    - Fusion if alignment not maintained ("failure of nonoperative care")
---
### **Type IIa — Flexion–Distraction Variant** _(Levine & Edwards refinement)_

> [!note] Frequently overlooked but clinically important

- ==Flexion–distraction mechanism==, **minimal translation but severe ==angulation** (>11%)==
- High risk of failure with traction
- **Management:** ==posterior C2–3 fusion== recommended
---
### **Type III — Hangman + C2–3 Facet Dislocation**
- **~7% incidence**
- Type II pattern **with bilateral C2–3 facet dislocation**
- Can sometimes be ==reduced → converts to Type II==
- **Management:** surgical
    - Preferred: ==**Anterior C2–3 fusion** ==(discectomy + plating)
    - Posterior stabilization reasonable if anterior access limited

## **4. Supplementary Clinical Pearls**

> [!tip] **Key Exam + Board Review Points**  
> • Hangman = **bilateral pars interarticularis fracture of C2**.  
> • **Mechanism:** hyperextension + axial load (MVC, diving injury).  
> • **Type I:** stable → collar.  
> • **Type II:** translation/angulation → immobilize; consider fusion if alignment lost.  
> • **Type IIa:** flexion–distraction variant → **avoid traction**; posterior fusion.  
> • **Type III:** facet dislocation → **surgical** (usually anterior C2–3 fusion).  
> • C2–3 disc injury = main driver of **instability**.
