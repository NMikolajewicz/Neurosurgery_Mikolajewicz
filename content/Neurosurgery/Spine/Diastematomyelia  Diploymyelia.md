### Pathophysiology

* diastematomyelia: spinal cord cleft
* diplomyelia: spinal cord duplication
	* due to adhesions between ectoderm and endoderm
	* notochord split

### Presentation

* hypertrichosis - excess hair growth
	* hallmark of split cord
* +/- asymptomatic
* neurological deterioration common
	* evoke on flex/extension movements of lower extremities

### Workup

* MRI
* CT myelogram

### Management

* surgery - IONM
	* untether spinal cord by removing bony/fibrous septum
		* bony septum always extradural
