Epidemiology

* 1/1000 live births
* most common nervous system anomaly

Pathophysiology

* open neural tube defect
* type of spina bifida
* involves meninges and underlying spinal cord
	* no skin covering over defect
* focal segment of spinal cord fails to form tube and neural folds persist as flat plate of tissue (neural placode)
* raw exposured dorsal surface of neural placode represents tissue that should have formed interior of spinal cord

Presentation

* sac-like cystic structure covered by thin layer of partially epithelialized tissue
	* rupture may lead to CSF leak
* spinal cord dysfunction
	* flaccid paralysis and sensory deficits below lesion
	* defects above L3
		* wheelchair bound
		* defects preclude ambulation
	* defects between S1-L3
		* ambulation with assistance devices
	* defects below S1
		* unaided ambulation
* GU tract dysfunction (90% cases)
	* constant urinary dribbling
	* relaxed anal sphincter
* +/- infection
	* sac rupture may result in meningitis
* normal intelligence (75% cases)
* ==[[NSx  General/chiari malformation note]] (>95% cases)==
	* most common cause of death
* hydrocephalus (80-95%)

Workup

* serum
	* elevated alpha-fetoprotein (AFP)
* amniotic fluid (amniocentesis)
	* ==elevated AFP==
	* ==elevated AChE==
* U/S
	* ==lemon sign== \- symmetrical bifrontal narrowing of skull
		* antenatal imaging
		* indentation of frontal bone
		* 90-98% of fetuses with spina bifida
* pre-operative
	* head U/S - Chiari II, HCP
	* renal U/S
	* spine xray
* daily occipital-frontal circumference and baseline trans-fontanel ultrasound
	* screen for hydrocephali (HCP)

Management

* atraumatic delivery - prelabor ==C-section== indicated
* if neurogenic bladder
	* regular ==catheterization==
* orthopedic assessment
	* ambulatory aids
* prophylactic antibiotics
	* ==nafcillin + gentamicin==
	* prevention of meningitis/ventriculitis
* surgical
	* post natal: standard within 24-48 hours
	* pre natal
		* intrauterine fetal MMC repair
		* reduced need for shunting
		* improved motor outcomes
		* increased risk of preterm labor and uterine dehiscence
