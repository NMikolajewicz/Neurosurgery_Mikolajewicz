### Definition

* DISH, also known as ==Forestier disease==
* enthesopathy of the spine and extremities
	* usually presents with back pain and spinal stiffness. 

### Diagnosis

* radiographs of the cervical and thoracic spine.
* CT scan should be performed whenever there is concern for a fracture following low energy trauma. 

### Treatment

* activity modification
* physical therapy
* bisphosphonate therapy.
* Associated spine fractures are treated with long spinal fusion. 

### DISH vs. Ankylosing Spondylitis

* DISH - excess bone formed
* AK - soft tissue converted to bone
