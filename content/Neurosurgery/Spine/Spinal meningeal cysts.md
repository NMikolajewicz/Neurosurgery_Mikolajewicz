Pathophysiology

* congenital non-neoplastic extramedullary

Types

* Type I
	* **extradural meningeal cyst**
	* **not containing nerve roots**
* Type II
	* a.k.a. Tarlov
	* **extradural meningeal cyst**
	* **contains nerve roots**
	* presentation
		* asymptomatic
		* +/- nerve root compression if large
	* management
		* lumboperitoneal shunt
		* open microsurgery
			* resect cyst
* Type III
	* a.k.a. arachnoid cyst
	* **intradural meningeal cyst**
	* presentation
		* asymptomatic
		* +/- cord/nerve root compression
	* management
		* cyst aspiration
