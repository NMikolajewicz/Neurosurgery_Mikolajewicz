Intradural extramedullary spine tumors
Mnemonic - NMSM (neurofibroma, meningioma, schwannoma, metastasis), MNM (meningioma, nerve sheath tumor, metastasis)

Bloody spine Mets: 'MR-CT'

* Melanoma
* Renal cell carcinoma
* Choriocarcinoma
* Thyroid

Blastic vs. Sclerotic mets

* sclerotic: prostate, breast (mixed)
* lytic: renal, thyroid, lung, breast (mixed)

Indications for surgery

* tissue diagnosis
* decompression
* stabilization
* Separation - separate tumor from surrounding structures (cord) to enable subsequent radiotherapy without affecting adjacent structures
