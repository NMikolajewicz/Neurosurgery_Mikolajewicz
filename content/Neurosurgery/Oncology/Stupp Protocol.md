### Stupp Protocol
Standard of care treatment of glioblastoma
Stupp, Warren, et al 2005, New England Journal of Medicine

#### Cohort
- Newly diagnosed glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype) after maximal safe resection or biopsy.
- N = 573 patients
	- 85 centers
	* randomized
* Demographics
	* median age: 56y
	* 84% underwent debulking surgery

#### Treatment Protocol
- Concurrent chemoradiation (weeks 1-6)
	- Radiation: 60 Gy in 30 fractions (2 Gy/fx), 5 d/week over ~6 weeks.
	- Temozolomide: 75 mg/m² PO daily (7 d/week) throughout RT (up to 42 days).
		- weekly CBC; PJP prophylaxis (TMP-SMX)
- Adjuvant temozolomide
	- Start ~4 weeks after completing RT (once counts recover).
	- **Cycle 1:** 150 mg/m² PO daily on days 1–5, q28 days.
	- **Cycles 2–6:** escalate to 200 mg/m² if Cycle 1 well tolerated (no >G2 non-heme tox; ANC/Plt nadirs adequate).
#### Imaging
- MRI about **4 weeks after** finishing RT/TMZ, then q2–3 months
- Expect **pseudoprogression** within ~12 weeks post-RT; 
- use RANO criteria and clinical before declaring progression.
#### Results
* median follow up: 28 months
* median survival
	* radiotherapy + temozolomide = 14.6 months
	* radiotherapy alone = 12.1 months
* unadjusted HR for death
	* radiotherapy + temozolomide HR = 0.63 (95% 0.52 - 0.75)
* 2-year survival rate
	* radiotherapy + temozolomide = 26.5%
	* radiotherapy alone = 10.4%
* Concomitant radiotherapy plus temozolimide resulted in grade 3 and 4 hematologic toxic effets in 7% of patients
	* leukopenia
	* neutropenia
	* thrombocytopenia
	* anemia
