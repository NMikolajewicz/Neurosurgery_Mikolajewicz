> [!abstract] One-Page Summary
> 
> - **Definition:** Primary glial tumors from ependymal lining of ventricles & central canal; spread via CSF possible.
> 
> - **Classify by site + molecular group (WHO CNS5):**  
> **ST-EPN:** ZFTA-fusion (all ages, p65/L1CAM+; not-poor) • YAP1-fusion (very young; good).  
> **PF-EPN:** **PFA** (H3K27me3 loss; **poor**; young children) • **PFB** (H3K27me3 retained; adolescents/adults; not-poor).  
> **Spinal:** "classic" SP-EPN (NF2/22q loss; good) • **MYCN-amplified** (young adults; **poor**).  
> **MPE** myxopapillary (filum/conus; **grade 2**) • **SE** subependymoma (**grade 1**).
> 
> - **Imaging hallmarks:** PF tumor from 4th ventricle floor; spinal intramedullary with **hemosiderin cap sign** and syrinx.
> 
> - **Prognosis:** Best predictor = **extent of resection (GTR)**; molecular group modifies risk.
> 
> - **Treatment (typical):** **Maximal safe GTR** → **focal conformal/proton RT** (≈54–60 Gy); **CSI** only if disseminated. Chemo: limited (infants/recurrence).
> 
> - **CSF seeding risk:** Image **entire neuraxis** at dx and recurrence; consider CSF cytology post-op when safe.

## Classification (WHO CNS5)

> [!info] 2021 WHO updates (used clinically)
> 
> - Histologic labels "ependymoma/anaplastic" de-emphasized; **site + molecular** drive taxonomy & risk.
> 
> - **MPE upgraded to grade 2** (recurrence/dissemination risk).
> 
> - DNA methylation profiling integral (especially PF to assign PFA vs PFB).

**ASCII "map" you can sketch:**

```
Location first → Molecular second
┌───────────── Supratentorial ─────────────┐
│ ZFTA-fusion (+)  → p65/L1CAM (+)         │
│ YAP1-fusion (+)  → p65/L1CAM (–)         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌────────────── Posterior Fossa ───────────┐
│ PFA → H3K27me3 LOSS  (poor; young kids)  │
│ PFB → H3K27me3 RETAIN (not-poor; ↑age)   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
┌────────────────── Spinal ────────────────┐
│ Classic SP-EPN (NF2/22q loss; good)      │
│ MYCN-amplified (aggressive; poor)        │
│ Myxopapillary (filum/conus; Grade 2)     │
│ Subependymoma (Grade 1; very good)       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Clinical Presentation

- **Posterior fossa (PF):** ↑ICP (AM headache, vomiting), ataxia, CN deficits; hydrocephalus common.
- **Supratentorial (ST):** seizures, focal deficits.
- **Spinal:** axial/radicular pain, sensory change, weakness, sphincter dysfunction; myxopapillary → conus/filum symptoms.
- **Dissemination:** multifocal neurologic signs; consider drop metastases.

---

## Imaging & Work-Up

> [!check] Initial staging
> 
> - **MRI brain & entire spine with contrast** (baseline neuraxis).
> 
> - **DWI** (cellular foci), **SWI** (hemorrhage), **post-contrast** heterogeneity/cysts/calcification.
> 
> - **CSF cytology**: typically **post-op** (avoid precipitating herniation).

**Hallmarks**

- **PF ependymoma:** arises from **floor of 4th ventricle**; heterogeneously enhancing; cysts/hemorrhage common.
- **Spinal ependymoma:** central intramedullary, often **syrinx**; **T2 hypointense "hemosiderin cap"** from prior bleed.
- **Signal:** T1 iso/hypo, T2 hyperintense.

---

## Pathology & Molecular

- **Histology:** perivascular **pseudorosettes** ± true rosettes; GFAP+, EMA dot-like.
- **PF groups:**
    - **PFA:** global **H3K27me3 loss** (surrogate IHC); poor prognosis; younger age.
    - **PFB:** **H3K27me3 retained**; adolescents/adults; better outcomes.
- **ST groups:**
    - **ZFTA (RELA)-fusion:** p65/L1CAM positive; not-poor.
    - **YAP1-fusion:** very young; favorable; p65/L1CAM negative.
- **Spinal:**
    - **Classic SP-EPN:** NF2 alterations, chr22q loss.
    - **MYCN-amplified:** aggressive, young adults, poor.

---

## Management Algorithms

### A) General Approach

```
Suspected EPN → MRI brain+spine (± hydrocephalus) → MDT planning
           └→ Maximal safe resection (goal = GTR)
Post-op:
  • Path + IHC + molecular (H3K27me3; ZFTA/YAP1; MYCN)
  • Stage neuraxis (MRI ± CSF cytology when safe)
Adjuvant:
  • Focal RT (≈54–60 Gy) for WHO 2/3 or residual disease
  • CSI only if leptomeningeal dissemination
  • Chemo limited (infants to defer RT; salvage trials)
Surveillance:
  • Long-term craniospinal MRI; educate re: late recurrence risk
```

### B) When GTR isn't achieved

- **Second-look surgery** reasonable (PF series: ~**37–80%** achieve GTR on re-op).
- If STR persists → **adjuvant focal RT**; systemic therapy individualized/trial-based.

---

## Site-Specific Surgical Pearls

> [!tip] Global principle: **GTR drives outcome**
> 
> - Preserve planes; minimize capsule violation (esp. **myxopapillary** to reduce seeding).

**Posterior fossa**

- Hydrocephalus strategy (EVD vs ETV vs delayed shunt).
- Telovelar approach often preferred to minimize cerebellar vermis injury.
- Avoid cranial nerve/brainstem injury—map/monitor.

**Supratentorial**

- Seizure focus & eloquence mapping as needed.
- Aim en bloc/functional GTR respecting venous anatomy.

**Spinal**

- **En bloc resection** favored (lower recurrence than piecemeal).
- Midline myelotomy for central lesions; neuromonitoring; meticulous hemostasis to avoid hemosiderin staining/adhesions.

---

## Adjuvant Therapy

- **Radiation (standard of care):**
    - Local conformal/proton **~54–59.4 Gy** (many centers use up to **60 Gy** in adults) after GTR/STR for WHO 2/3.
    - **CSI** reserved for disseminated disease or high-risk neuraxis involvement.
- **Chemotherapy:**
    - **Infants (<~3 y):** as a bridge to defer RT.
    - **Recurrent disease:** clinical trials; limited definitive benefit.
- **Targeted/biologic:** investigational; guided by molecular subgroup participation in trials.

---

## Prognosis & Follow-Up

- **Best:** GTR, PFB, YAP1-fusion, classic spinal.
- **Worse:** PFA, **MYCN-amplified spinal**, residual disease.
- **Myxopapillary:** high OS but **late local/CSF recurrence** risk—**long-term neuraxis surveillance**.

> [!warning] CSF Seeding
> 
> - Baseline neuraxis imaging; repeat at recurrence or if symptoms evolve.
> 
> - Consider CSF cytology timing post-op to avoid herniation risk pre-op.

## Key Takeaways

- **EPN = "resect hard, radiate smart."** GTR first; then focal RT tailored to risk.
- Always **ask for H3K27me3** (PF) and **fusion testing** (ST), **MYCN** (spine).
- **Neuraxis matters:** baseline brain+spine MRI; think CSF seeding.
- **Myxopapillary:** protect capsule; consider adjuvant RT after STR or capsule violation.
- **Second-look** can convert STR → GTR in PF and improves control.

## Glossary

- **GTR:** Gross total resection.
- **STR:** Subtotal resection.
- **CSI:** Craniospinal irradiation.
- **PFA/PFB:** Posterior Fossa Group A/B ependymoma (molecular).
- **ZFTA (RELA)/YAP1:** Defining fusions in supratentorial ependymoma.
- **H3K27me3:** Histone mark; **loss** is a surrogate for PFA.
- **MPE:** Myxopapillary ependymoma (now **WHO grade 2**).
- **SE:** Subependymoma (WHO **grade 1**).
- **Hemosiderin cap sign:** T2 hypointense rim from prior hemorrhage in spinal EPN.

## Quick summary (clinically useful)
- **ST ependymomas** split by fusion: 
	- **ZFTA**: all ages; not-poor prognosis; p65/L1CAM positive
	- **YAP1**: very young; good prognosis; p65/L1CAM negative.
- **PF ependymomas** 
	- **PFA** (young children; H3K27me3 loss; ==poor prognosis==) 
	- **PFB** (adolescents/adults; H3K27me3 retained; not-poor).
- **Spinal**: 
	- typical **SP-EPN**: often **NF2**, chr22q loss; good
	- **MYCN-amplified**: young adults; aggressive; ==poor==
- **MPE** is **grade 2** (filum/conus predilection; good). 
- **Subependymoma** is **grade 1** with very good prognosis.

## 2021 WHO Subtypes 
### Key 2021 WHO changes
- Eliminated "ependymoma" and "anaplastic ependymoma" as standalone entities
- Molecular classification supersedes histologic grading for defined subtypes
- MPE upgraded from grade 1 → 2 due to recurrence/dissemination risk
- No grade assigned to molecularly-defined subtypes (ZFTA/YAP1/PFA/PFB/MYCN) pending prospective data
- DNA methylation profiling integral to classification (mandatory for PFB)
- ==5 distinct entities==:
	- ST-EPN: supratentorial ependymoma
	- PF-EPN: posterior fossa ependymoma
	- SP-EPN: spinal ependymoma
	- MPE: myxopapillary ependymoma
	- SE: subependymoma

## Supratentorial ependymoma
### **Supratentorial ependymoma (ST-EPN)**
- **Essential Criteria:** 
	- (1) Supratentorial tumour with ==EPN histology==; 
	- (2-1) _NOS_: unsuccessful/unfeasible genetic analysis; 
	- (2-2) _NEC_: genetic alteration detected but ==**not** ZFTA or YAP1 fusion==
- **Age:** All ages
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Prognosis:** –
### **ST-EPN, ZFTA-fusion positive**
- **Essential Criteria:** 
	- (1) ST tumour with ==EPN histology==; 
	- (2) ==gene fusion involving **ZFTA**==
- **Desirable Criteria:** 
	- (1) DNA methylation profile aligned with ST-EPN, ZFTA-fusion positive; 
	- (2) immunoreactivity for **p65** or **L1CAM**
- **Age:** All ages (median 8 y, 0–69)
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** ZFTA-fusion / p65, cyclin D1, L1CAM
- **Prognosis:** Not poor
### **ST-EPN, YAP1-fusion positive**
- **Essential Criteria:** 
	- (1) ST tumour with ==EPN histology==; 
	- (2) ==gene fusion involving **YAP1**==
- **Desirable Criteria:** 
	- (1) DNA methylation aligned with ST-EPN, YAP1-fusion positive; 
	- (2) **no** p65 or L1CAM immunoreactivity; 
	- (3) PAS-positive eosinophilic granular bodies
- **Age:** Young children (median 1.4 y, 0–51)
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** YAP1-fusion / –
- **Prognosis:** Good
## Posterior fossa ependymoma
### **Posterior fossa ependymoma (PF-EPN)**
- **Essential Criteria:** 
	- (1) ==PF== tumour with ==EPN histology==; 
	- (2) absence of SE histology; 
	- (3-1) _NOS_: unsuccessful/unfeasible genetic analysis; 
	- (3-2) _NEC_: molecular analysis successful but no molecular group assignment
- **Age:** All ages
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Prognosis:** –
### **PF group A ependymoma (PFA-EPN)**
- **Essential Criteria:** 
	- (1) PF tumour with ==EPN histology==; 
	- (2-1) DNA methylation aligned with **PFA-EPN**; 
	- (2-2) **global ==reduction of H3K27me3**==
- **Desirable Criteria:** (1) Stable genome on copy-number analysis
- **Age:** Young children (median 3 y, 0–51)
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** Unknown / **H3K27me3 reduction**
- **Prognosis:** **Poor**
### **PF group B ependymoma (PFB-EPN)**
- **Essential:** 
	- (1) PF tumour with EPN histology; 
	- (2) DNA methylation aligned with **PFB-EPN**
- **Desirable:** 
	- (1) Chromosomal instability/aneuploidy on copy-number analysis; 
	- (2) ==**retained** nuclear H3K27me3 expression==
- **Age:** Adolescent–adult (median 30 y, 10–65)
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** Unknown / –
- **Prognosis:** Not poor
## Spinal ependymoma
### **Spinal ependymoma (SP-EPN)**
- **Essential:** 
	- (1) Spinal tumour with EPN histology; 
	- (2) absence of histological features of MPE and SE; 
	- (3-1) _NOS_ unsuccessful/unfeasible genetics; 
	- (3-2) _NEC_ molecular analysis done but no group assigned
- **Desirable:** 
	- (1) DNA methylation aligned with spinal EPN; 
	- (2) **loss of chr22q**; (3) **no MYCN amplification**
- **Age:** All ages
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** **NF2** / –
- **Prognosis:** Good
### **SP-EPN, MYCN-amplified**
- **Essential:** 
	- (1) ==Spinal tumour with EPN histology;== 
	- (2) ==**MYCN amplification**==
- **Desirable:** 
	- (1) DNA methylation aligned with spinal EPN, MYCN-amp; 
	- (2) high-grade histopathological features
- **Age:** Young adult (median 31 y, 12–56)
- **WHO grade:** 2–3
- **Prognosis:** **Poor**
## **Myxopapillary ependymoma (MPE)**
- **Essential:** 
	- (1) Glioma with papillary structures and myxoid change (≥ focal myxoid microcysts); 
	- (2) **GFAP+**; 
	- (3) DNA methylation aligned with **MPE**
- **Desirable:** 
	- (1) Papillary arrangements around vascularized fibromyxoid cores; 
	- (2) ==location in filum terminale or conus medullaris==
- **Age:** Adults (median 32 y, 9–66)
- **WHO grade:** **2**
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** Unknown / –
- **Prognosis:** Good
## **Subependymoma (SE)**
- **Essential:** 
	- (1) Circumscribed glioma with clustered tumour nuclei in expansive/focally microcystic fibrillary matrix; 
	- (2) lack of conspicuous nuclear atypia; 
	- (3) absent/minimal mitotic activity; 
	- (4) DNA methylation aligned with **SE**
- **Desirable:** –
- **Age:** Adults (median 40 y, 25–70)
- **WHO grade:** **1**
- **Driver / surrogate markers:** Unknown / –
- **Prognosis:** **Very good**
