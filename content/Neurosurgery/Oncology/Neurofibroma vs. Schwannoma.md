## Summary

| **Feature**             | **Neurofibroma**                                                                          | **Schwannoma**                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cell of Origin**      | Mixed: Schwann cells, fibroblasts, perineural cells, mast cells                           | Schwann cells only                                                                 |
| **Encapsulation**       | ==**Non-encapsulated**==; blends with surrounding nerve fascicles                         | **==Encapsulated==**; displaces adjacent nerve fascicles                           |
| **Nerve Involvement**   | **Infiltrates the nerve** – involves multiple fascicles                                   | **Eccentric to the nerve** – typically compresses but does not invade the nerve    |
| **Multiplicity**        | Often multiple in **NF1** (plexiform or cutaneous types)                                  | Often solitary in sporadic cases; multiple in **NF2** or schwannomatosis           |
| **Syndromic Link**      | Pathognomonic for **Neurofibromatosis type 1 (==NF1==)**                                  | Associated with **Neurofibromatosis type 2 (==NF2==)** and **==schwannomatosis==** |
| **Histology**           | Heterogeneous mix; lacks Antoni A/B patterns; S-100 variable                              | Antoni A/B areas, Verocay bodies, strongly ==S-100 positive==                      |
| **Malignant Potential** | **Yes** (especially ==plexiform type== in NF1 → MPNST risk ~10%)                          | Rare; typically benign                                                             |
| **Radiologic Features** | MRI: T2 hyperintense, often ==central target sign== (central low, peripheral high signal) | MRI: T2 hyperintense, homogeneous or with cystic changes; sharply defined margins  |
| **Surgical Removal**    | Difficult due to ==infiltration==; risk of neurologic deficit                             | Often ==easily resectable== with nerve preservation (due to eccentric location)    |
| **Gross Appearance**    | ==Soft==, diffuse, ill-defined; blends into surrounding tissue                            | ==Firm==, well-circumscribed, often encapsulated                                   |
| **Common Locations**    | Cutaneous, subcutaneous, plexiform (in NF1); spinal nerve roots                           | Cranial nerves (esp. CN VIII), spinal nerve roots, peripheral nerves               |

## **Surgical Implications**
- **Neurofibromas** require **more conservative** due to intraneural growth; 
	- complete excision may **sacrifice function**.
- **Schwannomas** can be **enucleated** with **nerve**, if arising from fascicle.
