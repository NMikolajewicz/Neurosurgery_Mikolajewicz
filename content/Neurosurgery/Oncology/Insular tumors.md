## Classificaion
==Berger-Sanai classificatio==n system is a way to categorize insular gliomas based on their location within the brain:
* Zones: The insula is divided into four zones by the sylvian fissure and a plane that goes through the foramen of Monro:
	* **Zone I**: Located above the sylvian fissure and in front of the foramen of Monro
	* **Zone II**: Located behind the foramen of Monro
	* **Zone III**: Located behind the foramen of Monro
	* **Zone IV**: Located in the anteroinferior quadrant
* **Giant**: Tumors that occupy all four zones are considered "giant" and are the most difficult to treat
* **Tumor location**: The location of the majority of the tumor mass determines its zon
