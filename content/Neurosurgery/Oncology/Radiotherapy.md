


4 R's of radiotherapy

biological equivalent dose (BED)
-  BED = n * d (1 + (d/(alpha/beta)))
- n = number of fractions
- d = dose

alpha/beta ratio predicts radiobiological response (radiosensitivity), low for benign tumors


other factors
- dose rate
- flash radiotherapy - ultrahigh dose rate > 40 Gy/s
	- protects normal tissue in preclincal models
