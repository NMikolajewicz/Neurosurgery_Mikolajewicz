## Differential Diagnosis
- **Germ Cell Tumors (GCTs)** – Most common in pineal (up to 50–75%)
    - _Germinoma_ (most common, radiosensitive)
    - _Non-germinomatous_ (yolk sac tumor, choriocarcinoma, embryonal carcinoma, teratoma)
    - Mixed GCTs
        
- **Pineal Parenchymal Tumors**
    - _Pineocytoma_ – WHO I, benign, slow-growing
    - _Pineoblastoma_ – WHO IV, aggressive, in children
    - _Papillary tumor of pineal region_ – Rare
        
- **Others (less common)**
    - Atypical Teratoid/Rhabdoid Tumor (AT/RT)
    - Gliomas
    - Metastases
    - Meningioma, tectal glioma
## Relative distribution

In a series of 370 pineal tumors in patients aged 3–73 years:
* 27% were germinomas (pediatric)
* 26% were astrocytomas (adult)
* 12% were pineoblastomas
* 12% were pineocytomas
* 4.3% were ependymomas
* 4.3% were teratomas
* 2.7% were ganglioglioneuromas, lymphomas, meningiomas, metastases, and pineal cysts
* 1.6% were mixed embryonal cell tumors (embryonal carcinomas)/malignant teratomas
* 1.1% were choriocarcinomas
* 0.54% were oligodendrogliomas

## Clinical presentation

- **Obstructive hydrocephalus** → headache, nausea, papilledema
- **Parinaud’s syndrome** → vertical gaze palsy, light-near dissociation, lid retraction
- Endocrinopathies (e.g. in β-HCG–secreting GCTs)
## Anatomy

* boundaries for the pineal region
	* superior: splenium of the corpus callosum
	* posterior: vein of Galen
	* inferior: quadrigeminal cistern and tectum
	* anterior: pineal recess and the posterior third ventricle.

## Biomarkers
**Serum/CSF AFP, β-HCG* 
- ↑ AFP → yolk sac tumor
- ↑ β-HCG → choriocarcinoma, embryonal , or some germinomas

## Management

- **Germinomas**:
    - Biospy not required with elevated markers
    - radiosensitive → _RT ±_ chemo (cure rate >90%)
        
- **Non-germinomatous GCTs**:
    - surgery for tissue if markers equivocal
    - _Multimodal: chemo + RT ± surgery
        
- **Pineocytoma**:
    - _Surgical resection_
    - Observe if asymptomatic
        
- **Pineoblastoma**:
    - _Aggressive: surgery + craniospinal irradiation + chemo_
        
- **Hydrocephalus**:
    - _Pre-op CSF_ diversion (EVD or ETV preferred over shunt)
