> [!Medulloblastoma]
> 
> |**Feature**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|**Definition**|A **primitive neuroectodermal tumor (PNET)** of the cerebellum; **WHO Grade IV**|
|**Typical Location**|- **Midline cerebellum (vermis)** in children <br>- **Lateral cerebellar hemisphere** more common in adults|
|**Epidemiology**|- Predominantly **pediatric** (peak age 3–8 years) <br>- Slight male predominance|
|**Histologic Subtypes**|- **Classic** <br>- **Desmoplastic/nodular** <br>- **Large cell/anaplastic** <br>- **MBEN** (medulloblastoma with extensive nodularity)|
|**Molecular Subgroups**|- **WNT** (best prognosis) <br>- **SHH** (intermediate) <br>- **Group 3** (worst prognosis, often with MYC amplification) <br>- **Group 4**|
|**Imaging Features**|- Hyperdense on CT <br>- MRI: T1 hypo-/iso, T2 hyperintense, **homogeneous enhancement** <br>- Often fills the **4th ventricle**, obstructive HCP|
|**Clinical Presentation**|- **Raised ICP**: headache, vomiting, papilledema <br>- **Ataxia**, **truncal instability**, cranial nerve VI palsy|
|**CSF Dissemination**|Common; **CSF cytology** and **spinal MRI** required|
|**Treatment**|- **Surgical resection** (goal: gross total) <br>- **Radiation therapy** (craniospinal axis + boost) <br>- **Chemotherapy** (especially in children)|
|**Prognosis**|- 5-year survival >70% with multimodal treatment <br>- **WNT** subtype has >90% survival|
|**Unique Features**|- Only primary CNS tumor with **documented extraneural metastases** (bone, lymph nodes, etc.)|

## Background
* 2nd most common malignant brain tumor of childhood
	* only high-grade gliomas  more common.
* They most commonly present as midline masses in the roof of the 4th ventricle with associated mass effect and [hydrocephalus](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/hydrocephalus?lang=us).
* Treatment
	* surgical resection (first line)
	* adjuvant therapy
		* radiation therapy
		* chemotherapy
* prognosis  influenced by
	* surgical resection
		* poor if incomplete surgical resection (>1.5 cm3 residual)
	* the presence of CSF metastases at the time of diagnosis
	* age of diagnosis
		* poor if < 3 years of age
	* molecular features
		* ==WNT: best==
		* ==SHH: infants good, other intermediate==
		* ==Group 3: worst==
		* ==Group 4: poor== (better than G3, but worse than WNT/SHH)
	* histological features
		* demoplastic/nodular: good
		* large cell/anaplastic: bad
	* expression of the c-erbB-2 (HER2/neu) oncogene.

## Workup
* CT head
* MR head +/- contrast (preferred)
* MR spine + contrast
	* ==CSF seeding resulting drop metastasis and leptomeningeal spread is common.==
## Differential Diagnosis
* ependymoma
	* floor of 4th ventricle
	* squeezes foramen of Luschka
	* less diffusion restriction
* atypical teratoid/rhabdoid tumor (ATRT)
	* young children
	* aggressive
* pilocystic astrocytoma
	* large cystic component
	* enhancing mural nodule
* brainstem glioma
	* exophytic
* choroid plexus papilloma
	* common in lateral ventricles
* In adults:
	* cerebellar metastasis
	* hemangioblastoma
	* choroid plexus papilloma
	* ependymoma
## Prognostic Staging
* 100% 5 year survival
	* ==no CSF mets, complete surgical resection, negative c-erbB-2==
* 54% 5 year survival
	* ==no CSF mets, complete surgical resection==, ==positive c-erbB-2==
* 20% 5 year survival
	* ==CSF mets== ==+/- incomplete surgical resection==
## WHO Classification
### 2016 edition
four groups
		==WNT (10%)==
		==SHH (30%)==
		==group 3 (25%)==
		==group 4  (35%)==
### 2021 edition
additional subgroups have been recognized based on [DNA-methylation profiling](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/dna-methylation?lang=us) and/or [transcriptome profiling](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/transcriptome?lang=us):
	* [medulloblastoma, WNT-activated](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/medulloblastoma-wnt-activated?lang=us) (10%)
	* [medulloblastoma, SHH-activated](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/medulloblastoma-shh-activated?lang=us) (30%)
		* TP53-wildtype (20%)
		* TP53-mutant (10%)
		* subgroups 1-4
	* non-WNT/non-SHH (60%):
		* group 3  (25%) and group 4 (35%)
			* subgroups 1-8

## Imaging Characteristics

### CT
* Location/effects
	* mass arising from vermis
	* effacement of 4th ventricle
	* obstructive hydrocephalus
	* more lateral in cerebellum
* Characteristics
	* hyperdense (90%)
	* cyst/necrosis (40-50%)
	* calcification (10-20%)
### MRI
* T1
	* hypointense to grey matter
* T1 + GAD
	* 90% enhance, often heterogeneous
	* WNT > group 4 enhancement
* T2/Flair
	* iso/hyperintense to grey matter
	* heterogenous, due to calcification/necrosis/cyst
	* surrounding edema
* DWI/ADC
	* high DWI signal - hypercellular
	* low ADC

### Molecular subgroup based on imaging
####  **WNT-activating**
* cerebellar peduncle / foramen of luschka
* best prognosis
#### **SHH subgroup**
* cerebellar hemisphere
* typically nodular
* intermediate prognosis
####  **Group 3**
* ill-defined margins
* +/- midline
* prominent enhancement
####  **Group 4**
* well-defined margins
* +/- midline
* mild or no enhancement
* +/- hemorrhagic

## Subtype characteristics

### WNT-activating
* least common
* best prognosis
* typically arise from the region surrounding the foramen of Luschka and middle cerebellar peduncle.
#### Pathology
* arise from progenitor cells of the ==lower rhombic lip mossy fiber neuron lineage== that migrate laterally along the anterior extramural migratory streams
#### Location
* foramen of luschka (75%)
* fourth ventricle (70%)
* cisterna magna (30%)
* cerebellopontine cistern (20%)
#### Treatment/Prognosis
* surgery (first line)
	* aim: histology, molecular subtyping, maximal resection
	* adjuvant therapy depends on risk profile
* long-term survival in children almost 100%
### SHH activating
* 2nd most common medulloblastoma group
* stratified by TP53 mutation status
	* TP53 wildtype
	* TP53 mutant
#### Pathology
* divided into
	* two groups based on TP53 status 
	* four subgroups
		* based on [DNA-methylation](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/dna-methylation?lang=us) or [transcriptome profiling](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/transcriptome?lang=us)
#### Treatment/Prognosis
* surgery (first line)
	* aim: histology, molecular subtyping, tumor resection)
	* adjuvant therapy depends on risk profile
* CNS metastasis common
	* 17% infants
	* 22% children
	* uncommon in adults
* intermediate prognosis, age dependent
	* infants: 77% 10 year survival
	* children: 51% 10 year survival
	* adults: 35% 10 year survival
### Group 3
* typically arise from vermis of cerebellum
* worst prognosis of all medulloblastoma groups
* in ==2021 WHO classification, group 3 and 4 are grouped together under non-WNT/non-SHH tumors==, which are divided into 8 subgroups
#### Pathology
* classic or large cell/anaplastic histology
* ==MYC amplification==
#### Radiographic
* radiographically similar to group 4
	* ==midline masses== arising from vermis
* unlike group 4 tumors, group 3 tumors are typically ==less defined and have more prominent enhancement.==
#### Treatment/Prognosis
* Surgery (first line)
	* aim: histology, molecular subtyping, tumor resection)
	* adjuvant therapy depends on risk profile
* CNS metastasis
	* 30% of group 3 tumors (47% in infants)
* worst prognosis, age dependent
	* infants: 39% 10 year survival
	* children: 50% 10 year survival
### Group 4
* most common medulloblastoma group
* typically arise from vermis of cerebellum
* in ==2021 WHO classification, group 3 and 4 are grouped together under non-WNT/non-SHH tumors==, which are divided into 8 subgroups
#### Pathology
* majority are classic histology
	* subset are large cell/anaplastic
#### Radiographic features
* midline masses, arising from vermis
* well-defined
* limited contrast enhancement
#### Treatment/Prognosis
* Surgery (first line)
	* aim: histology, molecular subtyping, tumor resection)
	* adjuvant therapy depends on risk profile
* CNS metastasis
	* common, 31% of cases
* Poor prognosis
	* better than Group 3, but worse than SHH and WNT.
