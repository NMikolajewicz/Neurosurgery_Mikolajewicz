> [!Summary]
> |**Feature**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|**Origin & Definition**|Derived from **embryonic germ cells**, often midline intracranially (pineal/suprasellar).|
|**Common Locations**|- **Pineal region** (most common) <br>- **Suprasellar region** - May occur synchronously in both locations.|
|**Epidemiology**|Typically **children and young adults**, male predominance for pineal tumors.|
|**Types**|- **Germinoma** (most common) <br>- **Non-germinomatous GCTs** (NGGCT): teratoma, embryonal carcinoma, yolk sac tumor, choriocarcinoma, mixed GCTs.|
|**WHO Grade**|Varies: Germinomas = WHO Grade II; <br>NGGCTs range from II to III depending on subtype.|
|**CSF Seeding**|Yes – particularly **NGGCTs**. Whole neuraxis imaging is recommended.|
|**Tumor Markers**|- **AFP**: Elevated in yolk sac tumors <br>- **β-hCG**: Elevated in choriocarcinoma, some germinomas <br>- Germinomas may be **marker-negative**.|
|**Imaging**|- **MRI**: Heterogeneous; germinomas enhance homogeneously <br>- Commonly cause **hydrocephalus** via aqueductal obstruction if pineal<br>- "engulf" pineal tissue and can have central calcification|
|**Symptoms**|- Signs of **increased ICP** (headache, nausea) <br>- **Parinaud syndrome** (pineal location) <br>- **DI or hypopituitarism** (suprasellar involvement)|
|**Treatment: Germinomas**|- **Highly radiosensitive** <br>- Standard: **Radiation + chemotherapy** <br>- Surgery reserved for biopsy or hydrocephalus management (e.g., ETV/shunt)|
|**Treatment: NGGCTs**|- **Multimodal**: Surgery + Chemotherapy + Radiation <br>- Usually **less radiosensitive** than germinomas|
|**Prognosis**|- **Germinomas**: Excellent with combined therapy <br>- **NGGCTs**: Variable depending on subtype and response to therapy|
> 

## Definition
* Neoplastic cells derived from totipotent primordial germ cells fated to become genital organs
* Occur due to mismigration
## Regions affected
* Pineal gland (54%)
* Neurohypophysis (20.4%)
* Basal ganglia
## Three main categories
* Germinomas (50-60%)
* Nongerminomatous germ cell  tumors (10-20%)
	* Embryonal carcinoma
	* Yolk sac tumor
	* Choriocarcinoma
	* Teratoma
* Mixed germ cell tumor (30%)
## Biomarkers
* Embryonal carcinoma: PLAP, CD30, LIN28A, OCT4, cytokeratin
* Mature teratoma: none
	* Diagnosis based on histopathology
	* Well-differentiated somatic tissue from 2-3 germ layers
* Immature teratoma: AFP
* Choriocarcinoma: hCG
* Germinoma: hCG
	* Syncytiotrophoblastic giant cells
