Focus on tumor-associated seizures in this article

## General Notes
* More benign gliomas show a higher frequency of seizures than malignant gliomas.
* The presence of ==mutant IDH is associated with seizures ==in low-grade gliomas (LGGs)
	* mutation of codons 132 and 172 of isocitrate-dehydrogenase 1 (_IDH1_) and 2 (_IDH2_)
* There is no association between seizures and radiochemotherapy
## Clinical Presentation
* usually focal or generalized with motor onset.
* Focal seizures
	* location-dependent and correspond to specific function.
		* e.g., precentral gyrus: focal motor seizures involving the contralateral extremities.
* Visual changes
* altered mental status
* behavioural changes
* altered sensorium
* postictal Todd's paralysis
* severe agitation with psychosis
* status epilepticus (SE)
	* ~7% of all SE cases attributed to brain tumors
	* associated with higher 30-day mortality
* LGG vs HGG
	* patients with low-grade gliomas are more likely to have seizures compared to patients with high-grade gliomas
	* LGG more likely to have secondary generalized seizures.
	* HGG more likely to have focal aware seizures
## Secondary epileptogenesis
* in 1/3 patients, the epileptogenic focus does not correspond to tumour location.
* ==paroxysmal activity in regions that are distant to the original site==.
* This secondary focus is seen more frequently with temporal tumours.
* ==Young age and long duration of illness== are associated with an increased risk of secondary epileptogenesis.
* Early treatment of the primary epileptic lesion may be needed to prevent development of a secondary, potentially irreversible, focus.11
## Management
* ==Initial antiepileptic drugs only after appearance of a single seizure attributable to the presence of a brain tumor. Do not start prophylactically.==
* LEV: keppra, LCM: lacosamide, LTG: lamotrigine, VPA: valproic acid

## Prophylaxis
* <https://www.mayoclinicproceedings.org/article/S0025-6196(11)61852-1/abstract>
* ==there is no statistical benefit of seizure prophylaxis with an AED.==
* Meta-analysis confirmed the lack of AED benefit
	* at 1 week: OR, 0.91; 95% confidence interval \[CI\], 0.45-1.83
	* at 6 months: OR, 1.01; 95% CI, 0.51-1.98) of follow-up.
* The ==AEDs had no effect on seizure prevention== for specific tumor pathology
	* including primary glial tumors (OR, 3.46; 95% CI, 0.32-37.47), cerebral metastases (OR, 2.50; 95% CI, 0.25-24.72), and meningiomas (OR, 0.62; 95% CI, 0.10-3.85).

## Valproic Acid
* valproic acid is associated with (3-month) favourable survival in GBM
* <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4077452/>
* Mechanism of action
	* The mechanism as an HDACi of VPA probably explains these observations.
	* HDACi activity stimulates histone acetylation and DNA demethylation, leading to restored expression of oncogene or tumor suppressor genes with ensuing normalization of cell cycle control.
