> [!Rapid Fire] Peds quick hits 
> **APGAR @ 1 & 5 min:** Appearance, Pulse, Grimace, Activity, Respiration (0–2 each).  
> **ADHD:** onset <7y (classic); 1st line **stimulants** (methylphenidate/amphetamines), 2nd **atomoxetine**.  
> **Autism:** before **age 3** + social + language + repetitive behaviors.  
> **Neonatal HR:** >100 + cyanosis → **O₂**; <100 or distress → **bag-mask**; <60 → **compress + intubate + epi**.  
> **Toilet training:** bowel 2–3y (abnl >4y), urine 3–4y (abnl >5y); self-toileting by 5y.  
> **Language:** 6m babble → 1y 1 word → 2y 2-word → 3y 3-word → 4y near-adult.  
> **FTT:** weight <3rd %ile (or down-crossing curves); severe → ↓ head circumference.  
> **Supplements:** Vit D if breastfeeding; iron if non-commercial formula; fluoride >6m if water <0.3 ppm.  
> **Abuse workup:** skeletal survey (<5y), CT head/chest, bone scan, ophtho exam.

## ADHD

- **Onset:** < **7 years** (classic teaching)
    - _Targeted supplement:_ DSM-5 uses **symptoms before age 12**; exams may still test the older <7 cutoff depending on source.
- **Features:** distractible, impulsive, excitable, hyperactive
- **Management**
    - **1st line:** ==methylphenidate, amphetamines==
    - **2nd line:** atomoxetine (SNRI)

## APGAR score

- Assessed at **1 min** and **5 min** post-natally
- **Criteria (0–2 each):**
    - **A**ppearance (color): all blue/pale → pink body/blue extremities → all pink
    - **P**ulse (HR): absent → <100 → >100
    - **G**rimace (reflex): none → grimace → sneeze/cough
    - **A**ctivity (tone): limp → some flexion → active
    - **R**espiration: absent → irregular/slow → good/crying

> [!tip] Exam pearl  
> **1 min** = initial status; **5 min** = response to resuscitation/transition.

## Autism

- Pervasive developmental disorder
- **Qualitative impairments in:**
    - social interaction deficits
    - spoken language deficits
    - repetitive stereotyped patterns of behavior
- Mental retardation in **~75%** (as written)
- Occurs **before age 3 years**

---

## Bradycardia (neonate)

- Associated with **hypoxemia** in neonates

> [!warning] Resuscitation mindset  
> Neonatal bradycardia is often an **oxygenation/ventilation problem first**.

---

## Child abuse (suspected)

- **Workup**
    - **Skeletal x-ray** (< 5 years)
    - radionuclide bone scanning
    - **CT head/chest**
    - **ophthalmologic exam**

> [!warning] Exam trigger  
> Consider **non-accidental trauma** when history is inconsistent, injuries of different ages, or sentinel bruising in non-ambulatory child.

---

## Developmental delay

- Failure to achieve expected **motor/cognitive milestones**
- Can be due to **encephalopathy**

---

## Diet supplementation

- **Common requirements**
    - **Iron** (if non-commercial formulas)
    - **Vitamin D** (if breastfeeding)
    - **Fluoride**
        - start **> 6 months** if water fluoride **< 0.3 ppm**
    - **Calcium + phosphorus** (preterm infants)
- If **commercial formula-fed:** _no vitamin/mineral supplementation required_

---

## Disruptive behavioral disorders

- **Oppositional defiant disorder (ODD), conduct disorder**
- **Criteria/features:**
    - violates rights of others
    - hostility towards authority figures
    - theft/vandalism
- **Association:** antisocial personality disorder as adult

---

## Elimination (Toileting) disorders

- **Enuresis, encopresis** (primary vs secondary)
    - _Targeted supplement:_ **Primary** = never achieved continence; **secondary** = relapse after continence.
- **When to start toilet training**
    - **Bowel control:** 2–3 years
        - abnormal: incontinence **> 4 years**
    - **Urinary control:** 3–4 years
        - abnormal: incontinence **> 5 years**
- **Self-toileting** by **5 years**

---

## Failure to thrive

- Inadequate nutrition (psychosocial or medical)
- **Presentation**
    - weight **< 3rd percentile**
    - reduced head circumference (severe cases)

---

## Feeding

- Infants **< 34w GA** must be fed by **NG tube**
- **< 6 months:** exclusive breastfeeding
- **6–12 months**
    - breastfeeding + solid foods
    - alternative: formula
    - avoid **wheat, eggs, peanuts, chocolate, honey, whole cow's milk** in 1st year _(as provided)_
- **> 12 months:** full diet ± breastfeeding
- **> 24 months:** same diet as family

---

## Heart rate (newborn resusc-style thresholds)

- **> 100 BPM + respiratory effort + cyanosis** → **O₂ supplementation**
- **< 100 BPM** or respiratory distress or central cyanosis → **bag-mask ventilation**
- **< 60 BPM**
    - chest compression
    - intubation + mechanical ventilation
    - epinephrine

> [!warning] Clinical pearl
> **Ventilation is the key intervention** when HR is low in neonates.

---

## Infant colic

- **Presentation**
    - paroxysms of crying/irritability
    - apparent abdominal pain
    - appetite good
    - weight gain normal
- **Management**
    - self-resolving (**3–5 months**)
    - soothing skills
    - pacifier
- If age **> 9 months**, consider **IBS**

---

## Lactating mother diet

- Avoid foods that cause colic
- Extra **600 kcal**
- Extra **400 mg calcium**

---

## Language milestones

|Age|Milestone|
|---|---|
|6 months|babbling|
|1 year|1-word speaker|
|2 years|2-word phrases|
|3 years|3-word phrases|
|4 years|close to adult speech competence|

> [!tip] Quick heuristic  
> **"1–2–3–4" rule**: 1 word @1 yr, 2-word @2 yr, 3-word @3 yr, ~adult-like @4 yr.

## Learning disabilities

- Subtle CNS dysfunction
    - dyslexia, dysphasia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia, dysnomia
- Discrepancy between **academic potential** and **performance**

---

## Medication doses

- **Weight-based**
- _Targeted supplement:_ avoid exceeding **adult maximum dose** when calculating mg/kg.

---

## Mental retardation

- Global developmental delay
    - delayed intellectual development
        - slow progress (not developmental arrest)
        - **IQ < 70**
    - immature behavior
    - limited self-care skills
- If **< 5 years**, diagnosis should be **developmental delay**

> [!note] Terminology  
> Many settings now use **"intellectual disability"** rather than "mental retardation."

---

## Postmaturity

- Failing placental function **> 42 w GA**
- Placental insufficiency syndrome

---

## Prematurity

- **< 37 w GA**
- Neonates **< 23–24 w GA** do not have sufficient lung development (not compatible with life)

---

## Recurrent pain syndromes

- Episodic pain **≥ monthly x 3 months**, well between episodes
- No organic pathology
- Etiology
    - recurrent abdominal pain syndrome
    - limb pain ("growing pains")
- Presentation associations
    - school phobia
    - separation anxiety
    - stranger anxiety

---

## Sex chromosome abnormalities

- Turner syndrome
- Triple X syndrome
- Klinefelter syndrome
- 47XYY
- 48XXXX
- 49XXXXX
- Fragile X syndrome

## Small for gestational age

- IUGR = intrauterine growth restriction
- Fetal weight **< 10th percentile** for GA
- Low birth weight: **< 2500 g**
- Very low birth weight: **< 1500 g**

---

## Trisomy syndromes

- Down syndrome (T21)
- Edwards syndrome (T18)
- Patau syndrome (T13)
